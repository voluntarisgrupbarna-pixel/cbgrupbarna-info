Frequently asked questions

When does CB Grup Barna play this weekend?

The full calendar for every CB Grup Barna team (under-16 to senior, girls' and boys') is refreshed daily from the official Catalan Basketball Federation calendar and can be seen at cbgrupbarna.info/en/partits/, with the weekend's fixtures, the previous round's results and the season-wide calendar.

Where does CB Grup Barna play its home games?

Most CB Grup Barna home games are played at La Nau del Clot and the Parc del Clot sports hall, in the Clot neighbourhood, Sant Martí district of Barcelona. The exact venue for each game appears in the calendar at cbgrupbarna.info/en/partits/.

Where can I see the result of a CB Grup Barna game?

Results for every CB Grup Barna team are published at cbgrupbarna.info/en/partits/, under last weekend's results, and on each team's own page.

What teams does CB Grup Barna have?

CB Grup Barna has federated teams in the under-16, under-14, under-18 and senior age groups, both women's and men's, with real parity in the number of teams and the budget between the women's and men's programmes. The full list, with each team's record, is in the "By team" section of cbgrupbarna.info/en/partits/.
