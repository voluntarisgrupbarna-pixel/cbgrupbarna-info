About Clínica Dental Bac de Roda

Clínica Dental Bac de Roda is a dental clinic located at Carrer del Concili de Trento 110, Barcelona. It offers general dentistry, implantology, endodontics, orthodontics (including Invisalign), cosmetic dentistry and paediatric dentistry.
