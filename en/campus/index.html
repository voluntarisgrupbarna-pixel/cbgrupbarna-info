What the Barna camp is

The basketball camp run by CB Grup Barna is the intensive training programme the club runs during the school holidays in the El Clot neighbourhood. It is not a leisure activity with a ball: it is skills development, with sessions devoted to one specific aspect of the game and groups formed by age and level.

The club runs it with Time Chamber, which brings the individual-work methodology. That combination is what defines the camp: the repetition volume of a skills academy with the team feel of a neighbourhood club with sixty years of history.

How it is organised

Each week has its own focus, so anyone coming for more than one week does not repeat content. These are the modules the club has run in recent editions:

  • Flow Camp — reading the game and decision-making.
  • TC Basics — fundamentals: footwork, balance, mechanics.
  • Shooting Academy — shooting, in every situation.
  • One & One Mastery — one-on-one play.
  • Ballhandling Lab — ball handling and dribbling under pressure.
  • Skills Lab Experience — combined skills work.

Who it is for

Children who already play basketball or want to start, whether they are CB Grup Barna players or come from another club. Each week the club keeps places for its own players and places open to players from elsewhere, and groups are closed by age and level so that nobody trains above or below where they belong.

If your child has not started yet and is between 4 and 8, the natural route is not the camp but the Escoleta, the club's basketball school, which runs all year round with Julio Torralba.

Next edition

The dates of the next edition and the opening of registration are announced on this page and on @cbgrupbarna. Recent editions have filled up before they began, so the safe way in is to ask for advance notice: the club writes to everyone on the list when places open, a day before announcing it publicly.

Frequently asked questions

What ages is the basketball camp for?

The CB Grup Barna camp is for youth players, from Escoleta age up to under-16 and under-18 categories. Groups are formed by age and level, so a beginner does not train alongside someone who has been competing for years.

Do you have to play for CB Grup Barna to join?

No. The camp is open to players from any club in Barcelona and the province. Each week there are places for Barna players and places for players from elsewhere.

How many places are there each week?

The camp works to a limit of roughly 50 places per week to keep the coach-to-player ratio. Weeks fill in registration order and recent editions have sold out before starting.

Where is the camp held?

In the El Clot neighbourhood, Sant Martí district of Barcelona. La Nau del Clot is CB Grup Barna's main sports venue.

When does registration for the next edition open?

The dates of the next edition are announced on this page and on Instagram (@cbgrupbarna). Anyone who wants advance notice before it opens publicly can ask via the club's WhatsApp (+34 698 425 153) and will be added to the notification list.
