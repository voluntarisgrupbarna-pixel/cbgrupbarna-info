What LOPIVI is

Spain's Organic Law 8/2021 on the comprehensive protection of children and adolescents (LOPIVI) requires any organisation working with minors — sports clubs included — to have a written protection protocol, a designated officer with specific training, and a channel through which any risk situation can be reported with confidence. It isn't just paperwork: it's the legal framework that makes a club a safe place, not just a place where basketball is played well.

How we meet it at CB Grup Barna

✓Board-approved protocol of our own
✓Trained Child Protection Officer
✓Confidential channel, <7-day response
✓Clean record check for all staff

The Child Protection Officer

Ana Fernández, Marketing and Communications Director and a member of CB Grup Barna's Board, is the club's Child Protection Officer. She holds official LOPIVI training, completed and approved by the Junta de Andalucía and the Universidad Pablo de Olavide (April 2026). She's the designated person to listen to and support any situation affecting the club's children, with strict confidentiality.

In her role, she carries the club's voice outward and, at the same time, makes sure that day to day, on the inside, every child feels safe and welcome. For the club, those two things can't be separated.

Clean criminal record check

All of CB Grup Barna's coaching and volunteer staff — coaches, team delegates, club staff — hold a clean certificate from Spain's Central Registry of Sexual Offenders, as LOPIVI requires for anyone in regular contact with minors.

A child's right to enjoy basketball in a safe, respectful environment free of fear isn't up for negotiation. It's the foundation everything else is built on.

Frequently asked questions

What is LOPIVI and how does CB Grup Barna apply it?

LOPIVI (Organic Law 8/2021 on the comprehensive protection of children and adolescents) requires any organisation that works with minors to have a protection protocol, a designated officer, and a reporting channel. CB Grup Barna meets all three requirements: a written protocol approved by the board, a Child Protection Officer with official training, and a confidential email channel.

Who is CB Grup Barna's Child Protection Officer?

Ana Fernández, Marketing and Communications Director and a member of the club's Board, holds official LOPIVI training from the Junta de Andalucía and the Universidad Pablo de Olavide (April 2026).

How do I contact the child protection channel?

By email at protecciomenorcbgrupbarna@gmail.com. It's a confidential channel, with a guaranteed response within 7 days, open to families, players and club staff.

Does all club staff hold a clean criminal record check?

Yes. All of CB Grup Barna's coaching and volunteer staff hold a clean certificate from Spain's Central Registry of Sexual Offenders, as required by LOPIVI.

Have a question or a concern?

The child protection channel is confidential and responds in under 7 days. If you'd rather talk to the club about something else, you can also reach us on WhatsApp.
