3×3 Westfield Glòries

Barcelona's biggest urban 3x3 basketball tournament: CB Grup Barna, Time Chamber and Eix Clot bring it to the Clot-Glòries neighborhood, with FIBA points and €2,000 in prizes split equally between senior women's and men's.

What 3x3 basketball is

3x3 is half-court basketball: teams of 3 to 5 players and a single basket, with 3 on court and up to 2 substitutes. Games last 10 minutes or until 21 points, the scoring moves fast, and in a single day a team can play several knockout rounds in a row. It's the format that entered the Olympic Games at Tokyo 2021 and the one that works best on the street, because it only needs half a court and an afternoon.

The 3rd edition, across three venues in Clot-Glòries

On June 6-7, 2026, CB Grup Barna, Time Chamber and Eix Clot took the tournament to three venues less than 15 minutes' walk apart: Westfield Glòries (main venue and FIBA court, Av. Diagonal 208), La Nau del Clot (the club's own pavilion, Carrer de la Llacuna 172, for the youth categories) and Rambleta del Clot (outdoor court, for senior amateurs and the Barna Màgics).

The tournament has 10 categories, from Escoleta to veterans, and only in seniors are FIBA 3x3 world ranking points and the cash prize awarded. Everyone else gets trophies, medals and gifts from neighborhood businesses.

Prizes

€2,000 in prizes: €1,000 for the winning Senior Women's team and €1,000 for the winning Senior Men's team — an equal prize, unique in Barcelona. The rest of the categories receive trophies and medals, and every team takes home an official tournament t-shirt and gifts from collaborating businesses.

Barna Màgics, the inclusive category

New in the 3rd edition: mixed teams of players with and without functional diversity, with adapted rules. Real competition, not exhibition — same intensity, same trophy. It's played at Rambleta del Clot, during Pride Month, with zero tolerance for any discrimination.

Frequently asked questions

What is 3×3 Westfield Glòries?

It's the urban 3x3 basketball tournament with FIBA points organized by CB Grup Barna, Time Chamber and Eix Clot in the Clot-Glòries neighborhood of Barcelona. The 3rd edition was played on June 6-7, 2026, spread across three venues: Westfield Glòries, La Nau del Clot and Rambleta del Clot.

Who can play?

There are 10 categories, from Escoleta (ages 6-7) to seniors and veterans, plus the inclusive Barna Màgics category. You don't need to be federated to sign up for the open categories; in seniors, however, FIBA 3x3 world ranking points are awarded.

What is the 3x3 format?

Teams of 3 to 5 players (3 on court and up to 2 substitutes) on a single basket, half-court. Short games, 10 minutes or up to 21 points, so a team can play several rounds in a row on the same day. It has been the Olympic basketball format since the Tokyo 2021 Games.

What prizes are there?

€2,000 in prizes: €1,000 for the winning Senior Women's team and €1,000 for the winning Senior Men's team, an equal prize unique in Barcelona. The rest of the categories receive trophies, medals and gifts from neighborhood businesses.

When is the next edition?

The dates of the 4th edition will be announced on this page, on the official website cbgrupbarna-3x3timechamber.com and on Instagram (@cbgrupbarna). Anyone who wants to be notified when registration opens can ask via the club's WhatsApp.

Where exactly is it held?

At three venues less than 15 minutes' walk apart: Westfield Glòries (Av. Diagonal 208), La Nau del Clot (Carrer de la Llacuna 172) and Rambleta del Clot, in the Clot neighborhood and Sant Martí District of Barcelona.
