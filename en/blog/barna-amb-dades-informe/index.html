The Barna, with data: eleven research lines on youth basketball

Three club studies merged into a single report: what science says about how to develop a young player, the Marc Gasol precedent, and the Barna's real position among 22 Catalan and Spanish clubs.

CB Grup Barna · 2026-08-16

Why a basketball club writes a thesis

CB Grup Barna commissioned and merged three independent studies: a doctoral-scope literature review on youth basketball development (eleven research lines, eighteen chapters, close to 900 references to academic and federation sources), the catalogue of Marc Gasol's public statements about his own development model, and a benchmark from the Marketing Department covering 92 Instagram accounts verified one by one.

The result isn't a document to file away: it's the case the club makes to the Board, the District and its sponsors.

What science says about dropout

  • Dropout is concentrated at ages 12–14, not 11 — it coincides with the transition from youth to cadet.
  • Fun weighs two and a half times more than winning in whether a child keeps playing (correlation 0.45 versus 0.18).
  • 92.8% of who stands out at junior level isn't who stands out as an adult. Selecting by performance at age 12 doesn't work.
  • Being born in January rather than December matters more than talent: 50.3% of regional squad selections were born between January and March, and only 6.8% between October and December.

The precedent: Marc Gasol

The Barna's model has a documented reference point: Marc Gasol founded a basketball school in Girona in 2014 —not a team— and didn't create the first senior side until 2017. Three seasons of grassroots work before a single senior team.

"We didn't rush to reach the ACB."

It's, point by point, the same order CB Grup Barna has defended for sixty years: the school first, the team after.

The Barna doesn't compare itself with Barça or with Joventut (la Penya): it compares itself with clubs doing the same thing, developing children through grassroots teams and families. Against this real universe, the club is the third-largest structure and the city's leading digital community.

Frequently asked questions

What does the research conclude about why children quit basketball?

That fun weighs two and a half times more than winning games in whether a child keeps playing, and that dropout is concentrated at ages 12-14, in the transition from youth to cadet.

Who is the reference point for CB Grup Barna's model?

Marc Gasol, who founded a basketball school in Girona in 2014 and did not create the first senior team until 2017: three seasons of grassroots work before a single senior team.

Where does the Barna rank among Catalonia's youth development clubs?

6th of 22 Catalan and Spanish development clubs by Instagram followers, and 3rd by structure with 32 federated teams — the leading development club in Barcelona city.
