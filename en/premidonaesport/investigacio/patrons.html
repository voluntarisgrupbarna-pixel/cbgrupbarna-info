Doctoral research · UB-INEFC

The full academic document.

The academic research "The Barna Method · The Hidden Patterns" (UB-INEFC, 2026) is a systematic study of the structural patterns governing the gender gap in grassroots basketball. Three parts, twenty chapters, five original theories.

The document is open to peer review, replication and criticism. It is not presented as a definitive truth but as a theoretical framework for future research.

Part I · The Hidden Patterns

Chapters 1-6.

  • C1
    The Invisible Market pattern. Female talent as an unlisted asset. (→ T1)
  • C2
    The Broken Ladder pattern. What the official data does not show. (→ T2)
  • C3
    The Abandonment-as-Design pattern. Five expulsion mechanisms.
  • C4
    The 0.75% pattern. The rate that reveals the system.
  • C5
    The Masculinised Governance pattern. 100% male presidents in Barcelona.
  • C6
    The Symbolic 8M pattern. Purple ribbons and no structural change.
Part II · The Five Theories

Chapters 7-11. The original theoretical framework.

T1
Theory of the Invisible Market
Threshold: <40% resources and >30% participants. The first to correct it captures the surplus.
→ Chapter 7
T2
Theory of the Recognition Ladder
Threshold: progression <60% between rungs indicates a structural barrier.
→ Chapter 8
T3
Theory of Cultural Irreversibility
3 simultaneous conditions → a self-reproducing culture.
→ Chapter 9
T4
Theory of Generational Lag
A systemic 20-30 year delay between recruitment and power.
→ Chapter 10
T5
Theory of Productive Authenticity
Build the system · the target defines itself.
