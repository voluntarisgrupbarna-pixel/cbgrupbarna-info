Why sponsor a neighborhood basketball club: Barna's numbers

A neighborhood club isn't a small brand: it's the entry point to a real community of families. These are the numbers behind CB Grup Barna's sponsorship dossier.

CB Grup Barna · 2026-08-16

Barcelona's neighborhood club with the biggest digital reach

CB Grup Barna closed the 2025-26 season with 5,382 Instagram followers, growth of +92% over three seasons. It isn't a one-off spike: the club published 1,109 posts this season and reached 1.97 million accounts, with 8.91 million cumulative views.

A brand that joins in isn't buying a logo on a shirt: it's stepping into the content these people watch every week.

Who's behind these numbers

The bulk of the club's followers are 35 to 54 years old and live in Barcelona: they're the families who make the purchasing decisions, not teenage players. For a business in El Clot or Sant Martí, that means reaching your real customer, not a generic audience.

Four ways to partner

  • Digital presence — logo on weekly stories, mentions in posts, presence in the family mailing list.
  • Digital and pavilion — banner at La Nau del Clot, logo on kit, two dedicated posts a month.
  • Maximum visibility — naming rights for an event, a dedicated reel every month, presence across all club materials.
  • Tailored — a proposal built for your business, joint activations in the neighborhood, in-kind collaborations.

A sixty-year-old club, not a campaign

What sets Barna apart from a one-off sponsorship campaign is that the club is there all year round, has been for sixty years, in the same neighborhood. Matches, camp, 3x3 and the season's activities provide constant touchpoints, not a single day of exposure.
