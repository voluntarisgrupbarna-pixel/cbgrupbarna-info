Women's Pipeline.

An aspirational, competitive model: from mini-basketball to the first team, generation after generation, building the future of women's basketball in Barcelona.

Strategic Youth Report · 2025/26 Season · Source: Official FCBQ + Club
01 · Executive summary

13 federated teams.
8 tiers. One single club.

CB Grup Barna has one of the most aspirational and complete women's pipelines in Barcelona: 13 active federated teams covering every age from U10 to Senior A, plus 2 school teams and 1 Adapted Sport team (Fundación Mullor · Barna Màgics). A project built for the long run.

The structure spans from the earliest introduction (Pre-Mini, U10) to the top regional competition (SuperCopa FCBQ, on the road to LF2). It's a model few neighbourhood clubs anywhere in Catalonia can replicate: breadth, continuity and ambition at every tier.

"Grup Barna's pipeline is aspirational by vocation and competitive by result. It doesn't look for shortcuts: it trains players at home, accompanies them through every stage and brings them to the first team."

Main conclusion · Strategic Youth Report 2025/26

The 2025/26 season is the best proof: champions in Valencia with the Mini team, a surprise from Júnior Vermell with 10 wins and 2 losses, and Senior A chasing the Promotion Play-offs to LF2. The path is set, and the results confirm it.

03 · The pipeline in action

Tier by tier.

Tiers 1-2 · School + Pre-Mini Girls (U10)

Grup Barna's Pre-Mini girls compete with a double team in the Barcelona Territorial Championship. The message the season kicked off with, posted on the club's Instagram, was: "Still on fire." That spark is the seed of the whole pipeline.

Running two active Pre-Mini girls' teams keeps the entry funnel into the club wide and accessible. No girl in the neighbourhood who wants to play basketball should have to go to another club. The Basketball School complements the system with two further groups, including those joining for the first time. Inclusion has been part of the club's DNA from day one (Fundación Mullor · Barna Màgics).

Tier 3 · Mini Girls (U12) · the club's best tier

The Mini tier is, without a doubt, the club's most successful this season. Three trophies in a single season at U12 level is an extraordinary result for any club in the city:

  • ★ Somnis Cup Valencia · L'Alqueria · CHAMPIONS (57-39 vs Valencia Basket · December 2025)
  • ★ Trofeu Molinet Level C · FCBQ · CHAMPIONS
  • ★ LAUBasketball Pre-Mini Tournament · 1st place

The Somnis Cup deserves special mention: it's held at L'Alqueria del Basket, Valencia Basket's facility (a top-tier venue), with clubs from across Spain. Winning it with U12 girls from El Clot is a clear sign that Grup Barna's grassroots women's programme is above the national average.

"Today's Mini players are the 2027-28 Cadet team and the 2031 Senior A. This generation, trained at home and winning outside Barcelona, is the club's most valuable asset five years from now. Three trophies at U12 aren't an accident: they're method."

Infantil A competes in Catalonia's U14 elite tier alongside Basket Almeda, Talenom Boet Mataró 2012, Cardoner Group Manresa CBF and SESE. Being in this competition is already a statement of ambition.

This tier's most telling data point isn't on the scoreboard: it's the 4 players called up for the 2011 Catalan women's pre-selection — Aina Dempere, Berta Cadevall, Carla Rodergas and Carla Calafell. The FCBQ only calls up those who stand out from the rest. Having four from the same neighbourhood club on that list is an extraordinary recognition of the coaching work.

Tier 5 · Cadet Girls (U16)

Cadet A competes in the Preferent, the second regional division, up against some of Catalonia's best-funded recruiting clubs. It's a deliberate club choice: to aim for the most demanding level possible, even when the resource gap is obvious.

What the tournaments reveal: in summer and in outside tournaments, Grup Barna's Cadet team competes at the top level and reaches finals:

  • Runners-up, Christmas / Sant Pep Tournament
  • Runners-up, Sampep Tournament · final against Uni Girona
  • Participation, Torneig de Farners · a prestigious competition

Júnior Vermell (Preferent, 1st Year): the pipeline's big surprise. With 10 wins and only 2 losses, it has the best ratio in the competition, ahead of Joventut Badalona B and TGN Bàsquet. The fact that the second Júnior team — in its first year at this level — sits above historic Catalan clubs is the most direct proof that the pipeline works.

"Today's Júnior Vermell players are next season's Senior B and the 2027-28 Senior A. The 10W-2L ratio isn't luck: it's the result of going through Pre-Mini, Mini and Infantil within the same club."

Pipeline argument

Tier 7 · Senior B (Copa Catalunya)

Senior B has a unique, decisive role: it's the antechamber to the first team. This is where the best Júnior players land to complete their competitive maturation before the final leap to Senior A. The cost in league results is a conscious trade-off, because the goal isn't to win Copa Catalunya with the B team — it's to prepare tomorrow's SuperCopa champions.

Recent history backs this up: in the 2023-24 season this team won its league and was promoted. The capability is more than proven.

Tier 8 · Senior A (SuperCopa FCBQ) · LF2 contender

Senior A's latest result was a convincing win: CEEB Tordera 77 — INSTAX CB Grup Barna A 85 (25 April 2026). With 17 wins and 7 losses in 24 games, the team has spent the whole season in the promotion zone.

05 · Why this pipeline is aspirational

5 arguments.

1
Breadth isn't a cost, it's a vision.

Fielding 13 federated women's teams is a strategic decision few neighbourhood clubs dare to make. Grup Barna makes it season after season because it believes in a founding principle: no girl in the neighbourhood who wants to play basketball should have to go to another club for lack of a team. This is the highest aspiration of a club with a neighbourhood soul.

2
Winning at grassroots level is winning the future.

The Mini team wins Somnis Cups in Valencia. The Pre-Mini team is "on fire." These aren't isolated results: they're the evidence of a method that works. A U12 player who travels to L'Alqueria del Basket, wins a final against Valencia Basket and comes home with the trophy carries that memory forever. It's the club's strongest retention anchor — and its best investment 5-8 years out.

3
Competing in Preferent Cadet is ambition, not a problem.

Few neighbourhood clubs have their Cadet team in the second regional division. Grup Barna does, with two teams in Preferent and its own sponsorship, because it wants its players to grow by measuring themselves against the best. And when the stage is an open tournament — Sampep, Farners — the Cadet team reaches finals and stands up to teams like Uni Girona. That doesn't happen without talent. It doesn't happen without years of pipeline work.

4
Júnior Vermell is the clearest proof the model works.

A second Júnior team with 10 wins and only 2 losses, leading the Preferent ahead of historic Catalan clubs, is no fluke. It's the direct consequence of players who have spent 8 or 10 years developing within the same club. You can't buy this with signings. You build it with patience, method and a pipeline that accompanies players at every stage.

5
The first team is the consequence, not the cause.

Senior A sits 4th in the SuperCopa FCBQ with 17 wins, a player on the official All Star team (Judit Ortiz) and a go-to outside shooter (Marta Zori). This team didn't get here by accident or through star signings: it got here because it has eight tiers of in-house development feeding the roster season after season. The immediate goal is clear — the Promotion Play-offs to LF2 — and the pipeline guarantees that, promoted or not this year, the club will stay competitive in the years ahead.

06 · 3 levers to accelerate the pipeline

What needs to happen now.

1
U12 → U16 development plan · "From Mini to Cadet Barna"

Create a personalised follow-up pathway for promising Mini players: specific skills sessions, visibility at summer tournaments, constant communication with families. The goal: for the 2025 Somnis Cup champions to be wearing the Cadet Grup Barna jersey by 2028. This continuity is the club's most aspirational, most profitable investment.

2
Consolidate Senior B as a launchpad

Senior B is the antechamber to the first team and deserves a stable sporting project. Keep the coaching staff for more than one season, design the roster with an eye on who can debut for the A team in 12-18 months, and give it visibility as a development team — not just a transitional one. The 2023-24 champion B team proved that when there's continuity, results follow.

3
Turn the pipeline into a brand asset

The Somnis Cup in Valencia, the four players in the U14 Catalan pre-selection, or Júnior Vermell's ratio are first-rate recruitment and communication arguments. Every grassroots milestone should get its own press release, its own mention in local media and its own space on the club's website. Grup Barna's pipeline is a brand asset — and it needs to be told with the same energy that goes into building it day by day.

Report verdict

Barcelona's most aspirational
and competitive pipeline.

CB Grup Barna has one of the most aspirational and competitive women's pipelines in Barcelona. Not the one that spends the most — but the broadest, the most committed to its neighbourhood, and the one that proves, season after season, that homegrown development is the most solid path to sporting excellence.

The grassroots shine with three trophies at Mini level, the transition surprises with Júnior Vermell leading its competition, and the top competes in the highest regional league, chasing the Promotion Play-offs to LF2.

"This isn't luck. This is a living, cohesive project with a lot of future ahead of it."
