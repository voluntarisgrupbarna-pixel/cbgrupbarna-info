Frequently asked questions

Where can I watch CB Grup Barna's videos?

This page has a selection of the club's reels by topic, embedded so they can be watched without leaving the website. The full everyday coverage is on Instagram (@cbgrupbarna), and the complete season roundup, built for press and sponsors, is in the press room.

Why doesn't a video load, or why is it slow?

The videos are real Instagram posts and load as they approach the screen, so the page doesn't get too heavy. If one fails to load, the card's "Open on Instagram" button links straight to the original post.
