The 2026-27 season starts now

Pre-season kicks off in the first week of September across every women's age group (Infantil, Cadet and Júnior, A and B teams). Senior A women debut in the Super Copa Femenina on 12 September, at home — Nau del Clot — against Geieg Vedruna B Tot Net. If your daughter wants to start before the league kicks off, now is the best time: Open Trials are running, and the first training session is a free, no-strings trial.

The Barna Method

That's what we call our own model for women's player development: in-house research into the gender gap in Barcelona and Catalan basketball, applied to how we coach, how we allocate resources, and how we support every player, category by category. It isn't a slogan — that's why the project has been a finalist for the Premi Dona i Esport award two years running.

How far it goes

Senior A women compete in the Super Copa Femenina FCBQ, the top Catalan territorial category, with a Senior B side in Primera Categoria Femenina just below. In between, women's teams at every age group — Infantil, Cadet, Júnior — follow the exact same two-team design (A and B) as the men's line, so no player has to settle for a lower level than she's earned.

Positioning

Among Catalonia's reference clubs, Barna is the only one with two senior teams — first and reserve — in both genders at once. Barça, for example, hasn't had a women's team since its project shut down in 2025.
