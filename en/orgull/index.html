Pride Month at CB Grup Barna. Activities open to the neighbourhood, the Sant Martí District and all families.

🏀 No child left out of the game 🏳️‍🌈

See June's activities Read the manifesto

We're a basketball club from the Clot. Every afternoon, the court fills with kids, families, volunteer coaches and older players greeting the littlest ones. This is our home. And it has to be for everyone.

What we say

There's room for you here.

Whoever you are and whoever you love.

We listen to you.

If something hurts you, come talk to us. We have time.

Nobody insults anyone here.

Not on the court, not in the changing room, not on social media.

Every family counts.

With two mums, two dads, single parents, foster families.

Staying silent is not neutral.

In front of a joke that hurts, the adults of this club act. Looking the other way means leaving alone the children who need us.

What we'll really do

1

We'll train our coaches every year

So they know how to properly support any boy, girl or young person at the club. We don't improvise on this.

2

We have a clear protocol

In case a girl tells us she's actually a boy, or a boy who's actually a girl. Confidentiality, respect and the family by their side.

3

Zero tolerance for LGBTI-phobia

Insults and jokes that hurt: not on the court, not in the changing room, not in the stands, not on social media. No matter who they come from.

4

A genuinely open mailbox

Write to us at voluntarisgrupbarna@gmail.com if something happens to you. We read, we listen and we reply within a week.

5

Every June, Pride Month at the club

Activities, rainbow laces, manifesto on the website. Not to look good. To remind ourselves who we want to be.
