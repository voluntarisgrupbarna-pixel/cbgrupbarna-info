Player development

Why 40% of Barna's players train at Time Chamber every week

A summer camp lasts a week, then closes until next year. The academy CB Grup Barna works with is something else: year round, one day a week, with one clear goal — arriving better prepared for pre-season.

CB Grup Barna · 30 Aug 2026

Also available in: català · castellano

A camp is a week. An academy is the whole year

Most summer basketball camps work the same way: one intense week, a big batch of sign-ups, then it closes until next summer. That makes sense for whoever's running it — it's the time of year when most families are looking for something for their kids to do. But it's a one-off experience, not follow-up.

The model CB Grup Barna works with is different. Time Chamber isn't just the summer camp at La Nau del Clot: it's an academy that runs the whole season, with weekly individual coaching sessions — short, highly repetitive, focused on one technical aspect at a time.

40% of the squad, one day a week

Around 40% of the club's players train at the Time Chamber academy one day a week, all season long. It isn't an extra activity disconnected from the team: the goal is specific and technical — arriving better prepared for pre-season and sustaining that improvement week after week, not just during one summer week.

Iandrey Panisa, the trainer behind the method

Iandrey Panisa (@coach_iandrey) is Time Chamber's founder, billed as a "Trainer of Trainers" at ACB, Liga 1 and NBA level. His method doesn't stop when the summer camp ends: he follows up with players individually so they genuinely improve, season after season.

A real case: Aina Hinojosa, 1×1 world champion

The proof of that follow-up isn't a marketing promise, it's a result. Aina Hinojosa, 17, was crowned 1×1 world champion with Jordan Brand's THE ONE and became a brand ambassador for a year. Behind it, as the very post that made it go viral put it, were "many hours of work and individual coaching alongside Iandrey Panisa."

That's exactly the kind of follow-up the academy offers Barna's players: not one weekend of coaching, but a long process that shows up once competition starts.

How to sign up

Time Chamber's summer camp at La Nau del Clot is the best-known way in, but the academy runs all year for the club's players. The full camp programme, with prices and weeks, is on the camp page.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between a summer camp and the Time Chamber academy?

The camp is one specific week in summer. The academy runs all year: one day a week of individual coaching, aimed at getting players ready for pre-season, not at generating one-off summer revenue.

How many Barna players train at the Time Chamber academy?

Around 40% of the club's players, one day a week throughout the season.

Who is Iandrey Panisa?

The founder of Time Chamber, a coach with ACB, Liga 1 and NBA experience. He gives players individual follow-up so they actually improve, not just during the summer camp.
