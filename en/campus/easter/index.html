Easter camp · El Clot · Barcelona

Easter basketball camp in Barcelona

A week of basketball skills training at La Nau del Clot over the Easter holidays. The Flow Camp, the module on offensive flow and playing on the move, is what the club has run in recent editions.

Request information The summer camp →

Why stop at Easter

Easter falls with the season heading into its final stretch, when every team already knows what it is playing for. It is the last window of the year to gain technical ground before the games that decide things, and the natural way in for anyone considering the summer camp who wants to try it first.

The details

What it is
A week of basketball skills training over the school holidays. The same method as the summer camp, in a short format.
Who runs it
CB Grup Barna, with Time Chamber's method.
Where
La Nau del Clot · Carrer de la Llacuna, 172 · 08018 Barcelona.
Getting there
Metro L1 Glòries and L2 Clot · Rodalies Clot-Aragó · on foot from Westfield Glòries.
When
During the Easter school holidays, between late March and mid-April. One week, fitted around the public holidays.
Who it is for
Seven age groups: Escoleta, Premini, Mini, Preinfantil, Infantil, Cadet and Júnior. Boys and girls, by age group and level.
Do you have to be a Barna player?
No. Open to any club in Barcelona and the province; the club's own players get priority when signing up.
Price
Not published on the site: message us with the age and we'll give it to you the same day. The club's own players get priority when signing up.
Language
Catalan and Spanish.
How to sign up
On WhatsApp at +34 698 425 153. Places are limited to keep the coach-to-player ratio.

What is worked on

01

Flow Camp

Offensive flow and playing on the move: reading space without stopping the ball.

02

Ball handling

Control under pressure, which is where the game breaks down in spring.

03

Finishing

Scoring close to the basket through contact.

Frequently asked questions

When is CB Grup Barna's Easter camp?

During the Easter school holidays, between late March and mid-April. One week, fitted around the public holidays. The exact dates for each edition are announced on this page and on @cbgrupbarna.

How much does the Easter camp cost?

Not published on the site: message us with the age and we'll give it to you the same day. The club's own players get priority when signing up.

Do you have to play for CB Grup Barna to join?

No. It is open to players from any club in Barcelona and the province. The club's own players get priority when places open.

What ages is it for?

Seven age groups: Escoleta, Premini, Mini, Preinfantil, Infantil, Cadet and Júnior. Groups are made up by age group and level.

Where is it held?

At La Nau del Clot, Carrer de la Llacuna 172, 08018 Barcelona. Metro L1 Glòries and L2 Clot, and Rodalies Clot-Aragó.
