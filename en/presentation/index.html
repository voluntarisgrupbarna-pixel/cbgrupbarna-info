· Diversity & Inclusion

The Màgics

We provide space to play for young people with intellectual disabilities. Inclusive teams that go beyond basketball: they learn values, make friends and belong to a real community.

  • Real social inclusion, on and off the court
  • Adapted basketball as a tool for life

Sport does not recognise barriers. Neither do the Màgics.

A safe place to grow up

Safety & commitment
LOPIVI compliant

Safeguarding children in sport

The club has put in place a full safeguarding system to guarantee a safe environment for every child and young person. Aim: to create a safe, educational and respectful sporting environment where children can grow as players and as people.

Measures in place

  • A designated safeguarding officer
  • Procedures for dealing with situations of risk
  • Safeguarding training for staff and coaches
  • A code of ethics and conduct for everyone at the club

Ways to get in touch

  • A confidential channel for reporting incidents
  • Direct contact with the safeguarding officer
  • Open communication with families, players and coaches
