Cistella Petita

If the 3x3 is the basketball party open to the whole city, Cistella Petita is the little ones' party: CB Grup Barna's mini-basketball festival for the neighbourhood, built so that children aged 3 to 8 get their big day as players.

Request information About the Escoleta

The little ones' big day

Games cut down to children's size, play, a medal for everyone and families in the stands. For many children it is the first contact with basketball and with CB Grup Barna — and the natural way into the club'sEscoleta .

For the club, Cistella Petita is far more than a fun day out: it is the first link in a path that starts at three years old and that, as Barna's two senior teams show, can reach the top of Catalan basketball without ever leaving the same club.

How to sign up

You do not need to be in the Escoleta to take part: Cistella Petita is open to the whole neighbourhood. Dates and entry for the next edition are announced on this page and on Instagram (@cbgrupbarna); for any question, write to the club's WhatsApp or ask Julio Torralba directly on 646 205 526.

Frequently asked questions

What is Cistella Petita?

CB Grup Barna's mini-basketball festival for the neighbourhood, built so that children aged 3 to 8 get their big day as players: adapted games, play, medals and families in the stands.

Who can take part?

Children aged 3 to 8, whether or not they are in the club's Escoleta. For many of them it is the first contact with basketball and with CB Grup Barna.

When is the next edition?

Cistella Petita is held once a year, in June. Dates are announced on this page and on Instagram (@cbgrupbarna).

Does Cistella Petita lead to the Escoleta?

Yes. It is the natural way into CB Grup Barna's Escoleta, the club's introduction to basketball for children aged 4 to 8.

What is the Cistella Petita and who is it for?

It is the mini-basketball festival run by CB Grup Barna and the neighbourhood, so that children aged 3 to 8 get their big day as players. If the 3x3 is basketball's party for the whole city, the Cistella Petita is the little ones' party, at La Nau del Clot.

Would you like your child to take part?

We will let you know when entry for the next edition opens.
