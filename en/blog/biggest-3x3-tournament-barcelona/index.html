The biggest 3x3 basketball tournament in Barcelona: the 2026 numbers

In 2026, two Barcelona shopping centres hosted a major 3x3 basketball tournament: Westfield Glòries in June and Diagonal Mar in September. Both websites are public. Here is the comparison, number by number.

CB Grup Barna · 2026-09-10

113 teams: the Westfield Glòries record

On 6-7 June 2026 the 3rd edition of the 3x3 Westfield Glòries, organised by CB Grup Barna, was played: 113 registered teams — a tournament record, after 80 teams in 2024 and 100 in 2025 — over 500 players competing, 10 categories (from Escoleta to Senior Pro, including women's brackets and an inclusive category with its own trophy) and 3 venues within a 15-minute walk in Clot-Glòries. Registration sold out before the tournament began.

40 teams: BasketMar at Diagonal Mar

Three months later, on 4-6 September 2026, the Diagonal Mar shopping centre hosted the BasketMar tournament. According to the data published on its own website (diagonalmarcentre.es/basketmar), the tournament offered a maximum of 90 places at €80 per team and closed pre-registration with 40 teams in the competition brackets: 4 Erasmus+, 5 junior men's, 3 senior women's, 7 senior men's, 10 U14 and 11 U16.

What explains the difference

It is not about the venue or the budget: Diagonal Mar is one of the city's biggest shopping centres and BasketMar brought in well-known basketball content creators. The difference lies elsewhere: a 3x3 tournament fills up through community, not through the poster.

  • Three editions of community — Westfield Glòries has grown 80 → 100 → 113 teams because every edition leaves teams that come back the following year.
  • Youth + open — categories for federated club teams from all over Barcelona and open categories for neighbourhood crews, on the same court.
  • Real women's and inclusive brackets — youth and senior women's categories with their own prize, and an inclusive category with a trophy and adapted rules, not an exhibition.
  • The neighbourhood as the engine — the tournament comes from a club with 60 years in El Clot, and the district's families, schools and clubs are the first wave of registrations.

It is the same lesson we tell in our 3x3 basketball guide: it is a street format, and whoever has the street — the community — has the tournament.

Frequently asked questions

How many teams played the 3x3 Westfield Glòries 2026?

113 registered teams, a tournament record after 80 in 2024 and 100 in 2025. Places sold out before the tournament began.

How many teams played BasketMar at Diagonal Mar 2026?

40 teams across six categories, out of a maximum of 90 places, according to the data published on the tournament's official website (diagonalmarcentre.es/basketmar).
