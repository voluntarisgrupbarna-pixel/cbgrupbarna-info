Robert Willett, the NBA skills coach who has trained in El Clot

In summer 2026, NBA skills coach Robert Willett (@bballwillett) ran a session at the CB Grup Barna camp, in La Nau del Clot. The joint post with @cbgrupbarna has passed 29,700 plays.

CB Grup Barna · 2026-08-28

Who is Robert Willett

Robert Willett is a basketball skills coach, active on Instagram as @bballwillett. In El Clot he is known above all for one specific session: the one he ran at the CB Grup Barna camp as part of the Time Chamber Experience × CB Grup Barna programme, the same individual methodology the club uses for skills training all year round.

The session in El Clot, part of Time Chamber Experience

The CB Grup Barna camp runs skills training in themed weeks with Time Chamber: a different technical focus each week — shooting, one-on-one, ball handling, reading the game — with guest coaches who run sessions on court, not visits for a photo. Robert Willett is one of them. The joint post with @cbgrupbarna about his session in El Clot has passed 29,700 plays, the highest figure of any camp content on Instagram.

How to watch the session

The videos of Robert Willett's session in El Clot are on Instagram and on the CB Grup Barna camp page:

  • Reel on Instagram (1)
  • Reel on Instagram (2)

Why he is there, and why we keep pointing it out

We don't say this for volume or budget: a city camp in Barcelona, with no accommodation or residential-camp fees, with an active NBA skills coach among its guest coaching staff, is not common. That is why, in the comparison of basketball camps in Barcelona, we place the Barna camp at the same table as Barça Escola and the residential high-performance camps. Robert Willett is not alone: Nolan Willett, also a skills coach, has run several sessions with Time Chamber in El Clot over the season.

A public message to the "Barna family"

After the session, Robert Willett posted a public message addressed to the "Barna family", thanking the club for the welcome and the experience in El Clot — the same post that has passed the 29,700 plays mentioned above. It is not common for an NBA skills coach to address a Barcelona basketball club this way, in public.
