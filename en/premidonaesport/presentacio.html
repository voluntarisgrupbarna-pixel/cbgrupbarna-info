History and roots

El Clot · Sant Martí · Barcelona · Since 1965.

CB Grup Barna was founded in 1965 in the El Clot neighborhood, in the Sant Martí District. Joan Manuel Serrat, a neighborhood resident, was one of the entity's co-founders. The club has operated uninterruptedly ever since from the Parc del Clot.

Today it is one of the oldest youth basketball clubs in Catalonia and one of the Catalan benchmarks in gender equality, sports training and innovation in grassroots sport management. It maintains an active institutional presence with the Sant Martí District, Barcelona City Council, the FCBQ and the Generalitat de Catalunya.

Three pillars of the Method

What makes CB Grup Barna different.

01
Equal Investment · The 50%

50% of the total budget goes to women's basketball (sector average: 35%).

02
Leadership Pipeline · The 38

38 active coaches · 65.5% of the technical staff. A complete pipeline from mini-basketball to senior level.

03
Organic Response · The 53.7%

53.7% female audience with no planned campaign whatsoever. Empirical validation of the model.
