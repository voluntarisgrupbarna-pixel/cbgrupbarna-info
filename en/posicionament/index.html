Where Barna stands among Catalan basketball's biggest names

Barça, Joventut, Manresa, Girona… when people talk about the giants of Catalan basketball, a neighbourhood club rarely gets a mention. The official records for each club at basquetcatala.cat tell a different story. This is about the positioning of the whole club — men's and women's — not just the women's project.

Inside Barna's pyramid

The club's official record on basquetcatala.cat shows more than 34 federated teams, from Pre-Mini (ages 8-9) through Senior, with parallel men's and women's structures and, in most categories, up to three levels (A, B and often a third promotion team). In both senior teams — men's and women's — there's a first team and a reserve, and both first teams compete in the Super Copa FCBQ, the top territorial category in Catalonia.

The four benchmark clubs

Among Catalan clubs with a first team in the Lliga ACB or Lliga Femenina Endesa, the usual pattern is that the academy and the senior first team aren't the same organisation.

  • FC Barcelona — 10 federated teams in Catalonia, all men's, from Mini to Junior, with no Pre-Mini team. No women's team at all: the blaugrana project (Barça CBS, with CB Santfeliuenc) reached the Lliga F Endesa but shut down at the end of the 2024-25 season for financial reasons. Its men's reserve team plays in the Copa Catalunya, not the Super Copa; the first team plays in the Lliga ACB.
  • Joventut Badalona (la Penya) — the largest academy in Catalonia: 53 teams, from Pre-Mini to Junior, in both genders. No senior team of its own in this organisation, men's or women's: whoever reaches the top leaves the club.
  • Bàsquet Manresa — actually two different clubs depending on gender: the men's academy (17 teams, no senior team) and Manresa CBF women's, a separate organisation with its own board, budget and brand, with a senior team in Lliga Femenina 2, the national league.
  • Fundació Bàsquet Girona — 23 teams, from Pre-Mini to Junior, in both genders, chaired by Marc Gasol. It also has no senior team of its own in either gender: the first team plays in the Lliga Endesa, outside this record.

The real difference

It isn't having a women's team or a large academy — almost every club examined has that. It's that at Barna, coming up through the ranks and reaching the competitive ceiling always runs through the same board, the same budget and the same club, in both genders at once. None of the four benchmark clubs has it set up that way.

At big clubs, the academy and the first team are almost never the same organisation. At Barna, they are: the same player who joins the Escoleta at age four can go on to play the Super Copa without ever changing shirts.

What sets us apart

Of the clubs examined, Grup Barna is the only one with two senior teams — first team and reserve — in both genders at once, within the same organisation that runs the whole developmental pyramid, both competing in the Super Copa FCBQ. Neither Barça, nor la Penya, nor Manresa nor Girona have it this integrated. We're not the club with the most teams in Catalonia — la Penya has 53 — nor the most famous. We are, however, one of the few clubs where coming up through the ranks and competing at the highest level run through the same club, without ever breaking the thread, in both genders.

Frequently asked questions

Is Barna a competitive club or a developmental one?

Both at once: it uses the whole FCBQ ladder (1st year, Preferent/Interterritorial, Promoció) and, on top of that, reaches the very top tier with both senior teams in the Super Copa FCBQ.

Does FC Barcelona have a women's basketball team?

Not since the end of the 2024-25 season. Its project, Barça CBS (with CB Santfeliuenc), reached the Lliga F Endesa but shut down for financial reasons.

Does la Penya (Joventut Badalona) have its own senior team?

Not within the same organisation as its academy. La Penya has 53 developmental teams, the largest academy in Catalonia, but whoever reaches senior level leaves the club.

What makes CB Grup Barna different?

It's the only club among those compared with two senior teams — first team and reserve — in both genders at once, within the same organisation that runs the whole developmental pyramid, both competing in the Super Copa FCBQ.
