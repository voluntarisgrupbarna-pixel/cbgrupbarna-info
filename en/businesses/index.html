Investing in the Barna

CB Grup Barna is not selling advertising space: it is selling access to a community. 450+ families in el Clot and Sant Martí, 400 players, 22 partner businesses, two senior teams in the Supercopa, a women's project that is half the club and its own events in central Barcelona. This page explains what a brand gets and how to come on board.

What is on the other side

A sponsorship is worth what the audience behind it is worth. These are the club's figures, and every one can be backed up: there are no inflated estimates.

On top of that there is the club's own channel. @cbgrupbarna averages 439,000 views a month on Instagram, with content produced by the club itself: match days, the Escoleta, women's basketball, the work of the staff. A partner brand does not appear as a hoarding in the background of a photo: it appears inside a story that people in the neighbourhood watch all the way through.

And then there is the territory, which is what no digital campaign buys well: el Clot and Sant Martí. La Nau del Clot, six courts in the district, the Westfield Glòries 3x3 registered with FIBA, the summer camp and the open days. A local shop is not looking for reach: it is looking to be part of the life of the neighbourhood. That is exactly what the club sells.

Who is behind those views

This is not anonymous traffic: the Instagram professional dashboard (Meta Business Suite) shows who follows the account and where from. The audience is mostly adult —58.8% men, 41.2% women, with the strongest age groups between 45-54 (32%) and 25-34 (22%)— in other words parents, not just young players. Geographically, 88.8% are from Spain and 43.2% from the city of Barcelona, with the rest spread across Badalona, l'Hospitalet, Mataró and other towns in the metropolitan area: exactly the local audience a neighbourhood brand wants to reach.

The account has also picked up pace: the 2025-26 season has reached 1.97 million accounts and 6.44 million views, 32-33% more than the previous season, with shares up 3.9 times. In three seasons followers have gone from 2,800 to more than 5,000 —92% growth— and the account has 8.9 million views in total since it opened.

Source: Instagram professional dashboard (Meta Business Suite), historical data from September 2023 to July 2026 and a season-by-season comparison. Reviewed quarterly; see the digital community benchmark for the most recent follower figure.

Barna Business

This is the idea that changes the conversation. A neighbourhood club usually offers visibility, and the visibility of a neighbourhood club will always be cheaper than that of a big club. Competing on price means losing.

What the Barna does have, and few others do, is a concentration of local businesses in a small area: 22 businesses from el Clot and Sant Martí sharing a badge. Barna Business puts them in touch with each other —around club events and gatherings designed for exactly that— so being a partner stops being a communications cost and becomes a way into the district's business community.

For a local business, that is worth more than a logo on a shirt. And for the club it means sponsorship gets renewed because it works, not out of obligation.

How to come on board

There is no single format, and you do not have to give money. Much of what is agreed today is in kind: product, service, space or experiences for the club's families. For many local shops it is the natural way to start.

  • Support in kind — product, service or a discount for members.
  • Team partner — linked to one particular team and its season.
  • Event partner — 3x3, camp, open days or Premi Dona i Esport.
  • Club partner — a presence across the season, the facilities and the channel.

The starting point is always the same: a meeting to understand what the business needs, and then a tailored proposal with concrete deliverables. The commercial pack can be downloaded beforehand.

Frequently asked questions

What do you get by sponsoring CB Grup Barna?

Visibility in front of 450+ families in el Clot and Sant Martí and 400 players, a physical presence at La Nau del Clot and at events, original content produced by the club, entry to Barna Business and a connection to a women's and inclusive basketball project. It is not just a logo on a hoarding.

How many businesses sponsor the club?

23 partners with a public profile, from local shops to Westfield Glòries, Instax Fujifilm, Nova Farmàcia Clot and the Mercat dels Encants. They can be viewed one by one.

Can you support the club without giving money?

Yes. Much of what the club agrees is in kind: product, service, space, experiences for families or discounts for members.

What is Barna Business?

The network of businesses connected to the club. Beyond visibility, it puts partners in touch with one another and with the business community of el Clot and Sant Martí.
