WILSON

Wilson is part of the ecosystem of partners and supporters behind CB Grup Barna, the youth basketball club of El Clot, Barcelona, as the official ball supplier for every team in the club.

Visit their website See all the partners

About Wilson

Wilson is one of the world's leading basketball equipment brands. It is CB Grup Barna's official ball supplier: every team in the club, from the Escoleta to the first team, trains and plays with Wilson balls.

The partnership

Unlike the club's other partners, the arrangement with Wilson is not a brand activation but the supply of equipment at a substantial discount for the club on the price of their balls. It is an in-kind partnership — equipment in return for visibility — of the same kind the club offers other suppliers and businesses interested in contributing product or services rather than money.
