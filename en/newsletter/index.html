Newsletter

The club's email: once a month, what is actually worth knowing.

What you get

This is not a newsletter about everything. It is a summary of what happens at the club, written for parents whose children play here, for people who come to watch, and for anyone who wants to follow the season from the inside.

  • Match days — what is on this month, and where.
  • School and camps — when registration opens, before anywhere else.
  • What happens in El Clot — the 3x3, the team presentation, new photos in the gallery.
  • Nothing else. One email a month. If there is nothing to say that month, we send nothing.

Your data

We keep your name and email to send you the newsletter, and for nothing else. We do not sell them, we do not pass them on and we do not use them for third-party advertising. Every email carries an unsubscribe link; you can also write to marqueting@cbgrupbarna.info and we will remove you ourselves.

Sending is handled by Brevo, which processes the data on our behalf on servers inside the European Union. Full detail is in the privacy policy.

If the person signing up is under 14, a parent or guardian must do it.
