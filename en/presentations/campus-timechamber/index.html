· Not a holiday club with a ball

Skills training, not a holiday club

The CB Grup Barna camp is the club's intensive training programme over the school holidays. Each week works on one specific part of the game, with groups made up by age and level so nobody trains above or below where they are.

It is open to players from any club in Barcelona and the province, not just Barna's.

Programme

Why with Time Chamber

An individual-training method

· The combination that defines it

Volume of repetitions, the spirit of a club

The club runs the camp with Time Chamber, who bring the individual-training method: short, highly repetitive sessions designed around a single part of the game.

Barna provides the other half: sixty-one years of team atmosphere from a neighbourhood club, groups by age and level, and coaches who know every player by name.

  • Sessions devoted to one part of the game, not a bit of everything
  • Groups by age and level: someone starting out does not train with someone who has been competing for years
  • Places for Barna players and for players from other clubs, every week
