Women's basketball for real, not for show

CB Grup Barna puts 50% of its budget into women's basketball, when the sector average is 35%. Eight federated teams, 38 women coaches and a path that runs from the 4-year-old Escoleta to Liga Femenina Endesa. It isn't a quota policy: it's how the club has been built since 1965.

The Barna Method

A system. Not a campaign.

Three pillars that work together and reinforce each other. The club has built them organically over six decades, and today they're the subject of academic research at UB-INEFC.

01

Equal investment

50% of the club's budget goes to women's basketball. The sector average is 35%.

With an annual €140,000-160,000, that means €70,000-80,000 for women's basketball. It doesn't take more money: it takes sharing what's already there differently.

02

Leadership pipeline

38 active women coaches, 65.5% of the coaching staff. They coach 38 teams every week in El Clot.

A player who becomes a coach — and who coaches boys' teams — changes how girls, boys and families see the game more than any equality campaign.

03

Community response

53.7% of the club's audience on social media are women. In grassroots basketball, digital audiences tend to run 60-70% male.

With no targeted campaign at all. When a club practises equality instead of talking about it, the community that fits shows up on its own.

"We don't look for them. They find us."

The path

From age 4 to Liga Endesa

The Barna Method's Leadership Cycle: six stages that explain how a girl who joins the Escoleta can end up competing at the top level — or coaching from the bench.

  1. EscoletaShe joins the club at age 4 and, from day one, sees women coaching.
  2. FormationFederated competition at every age group: mini, under-14, cadet and junior.
  3. CoachThe player joins the coaching staff. She coaches women's, mixed and men's teams alike.
  4. Copa CatalunyaThe senior side competes at regional level.
  5. Súper Copa FCBQCompetitive excellence and a gateway to the national leagues.
  6. Liga EndesaTalent reaches the elite. The example has a name: Ainhoa López.

Club ambassador

The Ainhoa Effect

Ainhoa López Rodríguez started playing here as a girl. She came up through every age group, played internationally at youth level and reached Liga Femenina Endesa. An autoimmune illness stopped her right when she was at her peak.

She came back to the neighbourhood to recover, and training alongside the club's players she returned to competing professionally. Today she's CB Grup Barna's first official ambassador, and graduated Cum Laude from UB-INEFC.

"I came back to the neighbourhood to recover. And the neighbourhood brought me back to basketball."

It matters because Catalan women's basketball's structural problem isn't only about money: it's about role models close to home. Girls watch the WNBA on TV, but have no one to look up to on their own street. Here, they do.

Strategic project

Línia Femenina: kit designed for them

Every season more than 200 girls, teenagers and women wear the club's kit. And until now they wore clothes that were never designed for them: scaled-down men's sizes, shirts that ran too long, waists that didn't fit. The industry calls it "shrink it and pink it."

The differences aren't about size, they're structural: hip-to-waist ratio, shoulder width, torso length, support, and the need for dark fabric to compete without worrying about stains during menstruation. Feeling insecure about the kit is documented as a direct factor in teenage dropout.

CB Grup Barna is developing the first collection from a Catalan grassroots club designed from scratch for the female body: new patterns, functional materials and co-creation with the players themselves, who test the prototypes and decide.

Frequently asked questions

How many women's teams does CB Grup Barna have?

In the 2026-27 season the club has 8 women's teams competing in FCBQ federated leagues, from Under-14 to Senior, plus the Escoleta groups and the Barna Màgics adapted sports team. The Senior Women's A team competes in the Super Copa Femenina.

How much of the budget does the club put into women's basketball?

50% of the total budget. The sector average is 35%. It's the first of the Barna Method's three pillars.

Can a girl start playing basketball with no experience?

Yes. The Escoleta welcomes girls and boys aged 4 to 8 with no prior experience, and players can join the formation categories throughout the season if there's a place. The club keeps social scholarships so no family is left out for financial reasons.

Who coaches CB Grup Barna's women's teams?

The club has 38 active women coaches, 65.5% of the coaching staff. Many are former players trained at the club, and they coach women's, mixed and men's teams alike.

Where does CB Grup Barna's women's basketball train?

At La Nau del Clot, C/ Llacuna 170-172, Parc del Clot, 08018 Barcelona, in the Sant Martí district.

Does CB Grup Barna have a women's basketball team?

Yes. Women's basketball is one of the club's two main lines, with the same budget and the same priority as the men's side: over 200 players in competition, from the Escoleta up to the Senior A team, which competes in the Super Copa Femenina FCBQ.

What is the Barna Method?

It's the club's own model for developing women's basketball, backed by original research on the gender gap in basketball in Barcelona and Catalonia. It has been a finalist for the Premi Dona i Esport (Women and Sport Award) two years running.

At what age can a girl start playing basketball at Barna?

At age 4, at the Escoleta, in mixed groups. Federated women's competition starts at Under-10 (ages 8-9) and continues through to Senior.

What is the Premi Dona i Esport, and where does Barna fit in?

It's an award recognising work for equality in sport, and the Barna Method — the club's own model for developing women's basketball, backed by original research on the gender gap in Barcelona basketball — has been a finalist two years running.
