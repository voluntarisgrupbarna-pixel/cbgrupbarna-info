From Macro to Micro.

NBA · WNBA · Euroleague · Catalonia · El Clot. The same gap patterns appear at every scale. Empirical validation of the Method.

Three scales · one same logic

NBA · WNBA · Catalonia.

The Macro to Micro document is the comparative study that shows how the same gender gap patterns appear at three different scales: the NBA/WNBA in the United States, the Euroleague Women in Europe and grassroots basketball in Catalonia.

The aim of the research: to empirically validate that the Five Theories of the Barna Method are not a local phenomenon, but the description of a global structural pattern.

The French model (Bourges, Basket Landes) disproves the demographic logic: cities with fewer than 70,000 inhabitants maintain European elite teams thanks to determined municipal support. Barcelona, with 1.6 million, should be able to operate with far greater ease.

What if...

Three scenarios.
Three figures. One decision.

The data above describes the current state. The question to ask is a different one: what happens if Barcelona corrects the pattern? Three scenarios with numbers derived from the territory's own figures.

01
If Barcelona matches La Seu d'Urgell's conversion rate

Barcelona has 134 women's youth teams, representing approximately ~1,600 players. Today, only 0.75% reach national level: ~12 talents/year. If BCN matched La Seu d'Urgell's rate (12.5%), we would be talking about ~200 talents/year.

The difference is not one of talent: it is one of architecture. The question is how many names of professional players we could be reading in the media today if Barcelona had operated like La Seu over the last decade.

02
If all BCN clubs apply the Grup Barna's 50% parity

This is the first invisible dividend of the Barna Method: every new female coaching staff member trained becomes a role model for the players she coaches. Technical parity is not an expense: it is the cultural infrastructure of the next generation.

03
If the IBE promotes the Barna model · a 5-year projection

Unlike one-off measures, the model is replicable, measurable and transferable: 60 years of empirical evidence in El Clot have already validated it. For the IBE, it is not a new expense; it is a proven execution template that can be encouraged through public tenders, grant criteria and institutional recognition.

“Barcelona does not need to invent anything new. It needs to choose between continuing to lose ~190 talents a year or making use of a model that has already worked in El Clot for six decades.”

Executive conclusion · CB Grup Barna 2026

Methodological note. The three scenarios are conservative projections derived exclusively from the figures cited on this same page (134 teams in BCN, 0.75% current conversion, 12.5% La Seu, 38 coaches at Barna, 17 clubs analysed, European cases). An average of 12 players/team and a current sector technical parity rate of 35% are assumed. The 5-year projections factor in natural generational turnover but not external multiplier effects.
