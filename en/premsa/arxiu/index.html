Sixty years of club history leave a trail. This archive collects references to the Barna in external media —from match reports to club features— ordered from the most recent to the oldest.

Transparency note: entries with a link go straight to the source. Those marked link pending are documented references (outlet, date and headline) that don't yet have a verified direct URL —often older archives or outlets with limited internal search. These will be completed over time.

Permanent listings and directories

These aren't news articles, but they're sources that Google and AI assistants consult often: Enciclopèdia Catalana · Esportpèdia, the City Council's Barcelona Guide listing, the Eix Clot directory, Eurobasket, and the official FCBQ and FEB profiles.

Where the shift comes from

Until a few years ago, most appearances were "Grup Barna as rival": results, previews and reports from other clubs. Since 2026, the Barna has started to appear as a story in its own right: the club project, the two senior teams, event organizing. It's the direction worth continuing to build on.
