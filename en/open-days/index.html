Open days: come and try

All through September, any child can come and train with us. Free, and with no commitment to stay.

How it works

There is no single open day: the whole of September is one. You tell us how old your child is, we work out which group they belong to and we tell you the day and time that group trains. They come, they join in, and afterwards you decide.

  • Ages 4 to 8 — Escoleta. Mixed teams and three groups by age.
  • Age 9 and up — federation teams, by year of birth.
  • What to bring — sports clothes, indoor court shoes and a water bottle. We provide the ball.
  • What it costs — the trial session is free.

We train at La Nau del Clot, in the Sant Martí district. If you would rather come and watch first, before saying anything at home, you can: just tell us.

What happens with your data

What you write here is read by the club's sporting management and is used only to arrange the trial and reply to you. We do not add it to any list or use it for anything else. The details are in the privacy policy, and how we protect children's data, in Child Protection.
