Competitive positioning

The structure of a big club, with a neighbourhood soul

The Catalan Basketball Federation publishes an official record for every club: how many teams it has, which divisions it competes in and whether its first senior team belongs to the same organisation as the youth section. With that public data, here is how Barna stands against Catalonia's reference clubs.

  • FC Barcelona — 10 federated teams in Catalonia, men's only, from under-10 to under-18, with no under-8 side. And no women's team at all: its women's basketball project (Barça CBS, with CB Santfeliuenc) closed at the end of 2024-25 for financial reasons, having reached the Liga F Endesa. Its men's reserve senior side plays in the Copa Catalunya, not the Super Copa; the first team plays in the ACB.
  • Joventut Badalona (la Penya) — the largest youth section in Catalonia: 53 teams, from under-8 to under-18, on both sides. No senior team of its own in this organisation: whoever makes it to the top leaves the club.
  • Bàsquet Manresa — two separate clubs by gender: 17 men's teams with no senior side, and a separate women's club (Manresa CBF) with a senior team in Liga Femenina 2, the national league.
  • Fundació Bàsquet Girona — 23 teams, from under-8 to under-18, on both sides, chaired by Marc Gasol. It has no senior team of its own either: its first team plays in the Liga Endesa, outside this record.
  • CB Grup Barna — more than 34 teams, from under-8 to senior, and the only one on this list with two senior teams — first and reserve — on both sides at once, both competing in the FCBQ Super Copa, the top regional division.

What makes us different

At the big clubs, the youth section and the first team are almost never the same organisation. At Barna they are: the same player who joins the Escoleta at 4 can end up playing in the Super Copa without ever changing shirt.

Source: club records from the Catalan Basketball Federation (basquetcatala.cat), August 2026. Full analysis on the club blog.

How many sponsors and partners does CB Grup Barna have?

23 active partners and sponsors with their own pages in the 2026/27 season — including Wilson, the official ball supplier — plus several institutional and press collaborators (Ajuntament de Barcelona, Generalitat de Catalunya, local media).

What kind of companies sponsor CB Grup Barna?

Mostly shops and organisations from El Clot and Sant Martí, health and wellbeing (opticians, dental clinic, podiatry, herbalist, pharmacy), leisure and tourism (shopping centre, water park), professional services (solicitors, printers) and sporting or social organisations that work with the club.

On the partner wall on this page: every logo links to that company's own page, with what they do, any offer for Barna families, and their Instagram. There is also a map at cbgrupbarna.info/partners-mapa/.

How do I become a sponsor or partner of CB Grup Barna?

Write to us on WhatsApp (+34 698 425 153) or send an email to marqueting@cbgrupbarna.info. We prepare a concrete proposal based on what your brand wants to achieve, with no filler packages or impossible-to-measure promises.

What ways are there to collaborate?

Three transparent starting points — Digital Presence, Brand in Motion and Sports Sponsorship — plus tailor-made collaboration for brands that want a clear goal and very specific deliverables.

Can I collaborate with a product or service instead of money?

Yes. Many of the club's collaborations are in kind: sports equipment, transport, physiotherapy, event catering or printing. They are valued the same as a monetary contribution and matched to an equivalent collaboration tier.

What does my company get in return?

Content with context (not an isolated logo), physical presence at La Nau, kits, tournaments and camps, access to a community of more than 450 families, and a tailor-made proposal with specific deliverables for each brand.

Is banner or specific material production included?

No, unless expressly agreed: it is quoted separately depending on what the activation needs.
