Barna 8M Fund: the club's proposal to the FCBQ to qualify 38 coaches

CB Grup Barna has formally submitted a proposal to the Catalan Basketball Federation to recognise and help qualify the club's 38 coaches who, despite coaching every week, don't hold a federation card.

CB Grup Barna · 2026-08-16

Thirty-eight coaches the system doesn't count

CB Grup Barna has 38 active coaches this season, 65% of the club's entire technical staff. They train players, run teams, mentor and, many of them, coach boys' teams. None holds a federation card — not because they can't, but because the course's cost and schedule requirements make it impossible.

For the Catalan Basketball Federation's statistics, these 38 women simply don't exist. And that's not just an individual recognition problem: when Catalonia designs sports policy with incomplete data, the real figures for women in technical roles fall short of what's actually happening on the courts.

8M as a reminder, not a campaign

On 4 March 2026, coinciding with International Women's Day, the club sent a formal letter to the FCBQ president and the Federation's board. It isn't a symbolic gesture: it's a concrete request, with figures, with a co-financing model, and with the club's commitment to cover part of the cost.

"The future of women's basketball is already training. The system just needs to recognise it."

The proposal, in three levels

  • Level 1 — Qualification scholarship fund. Co-financing for the Level 0 Sports Coach course: proposed split of 30% club and 70% FCBQ (or 100% FCBQ for small clubs), with a two-season commitment to stay. Barna has 38 immediate candidates for it.
  • Level 2 — Redesign of the FCBQ Mentoring Programme. Changing the access criteria: from "holding a prior qualification" to "being an active coach at a federated club." It requires no financial investment, just a federation policy decision.
  • Level 3 — "From El Clot to Catalonia" pilot programme. Turning Barna's model into a documented, replicable programme, with FCBQ certification as a "Reference Club for Women's Technical Development" and a presentation to Barcelona City Council and the Catalan Sports Council.

Why the club can ask for this with authority

CB Grup Barna puts 50% of its budget into women's basketball, when the sector norm sits around 35%. 65.5% of the technical staff are women. 57% of the club's structure — 20 of the 35 teams — is women's. This isn't a one-off action for 8M: it's sixty years of a model built in El Clot that had never before been put forward as a policy proposal.

What the formal request asks for, exactly

  1. An FCBQ Scholarship Fund for qualifying active coaches without a card.
  2. Amending the access criteria for the FCBQ Mentoring Programme.
  3. Having CB Grup Barna act as a documented pilot club for the model.
  4. A transitional register of coaches in training at the FCBQ, to count Catalonia's real female technical talent.

The full document, with the letter to the Federation's president and all the figures, is available at Barna 8M Fund.

Frequently asked questions

What exactly does the Barna 8M Fund ask for?

An FCBQ scholarship fund to pay for the Level 0 Sports Coach course for active coaches without a card, with proposed co-financing of 30% club and 70% FCBQ, plus a redesign of the Coaches' Mentoring Programme so that anyone already coaching can access it, not only those who already hold a qualification.

How many coaches does CB Grup Barna have without a federation card?

38, 65% of the club's entire technical staff. They train players every week and many run boys' teams, but they don't appear in the FCBQ's official statistics.

When was the proposal submitted?

On 4 March 2026, marking International Women's Day, in a letter addressed to the president of the Catalan Basketball Federation.
