01
Diagnosis · The fundamental paradox

A system that was not designed for them.

Barcelona is the second-largest city in Spain. It has 1.6 million inhabitants, 134 girls' basketball development teams and one of the most extensive training bases in Europe. And yet, its conversion rate of female talent into national-level categories is 0.75%. Out of every hundred girls who start playing, only three make it into the professional game. The other ninety-nine are lost along the way.

This figure is not an anomaly. It is the result of a system built by men, for men, over decades. A system that never thought about the girls of El Clot. A system that CB Grup Barna has spent sixty-one years challenging.

The gender gap in sport is not a deficit of talent. It is a failure in the system's architectural design. And CB Grup Barna is, today, the empirical proof that it can be done differently.

02
The Barna Method · From El Clot, to the World

A pedagogical model that does not distinguish between boys and girls — it distinguishes between athletes who grow as people.

The Barna Method is a coherent educational and sporting model, from the Escoleta to senior level, that has shown over six decades that equality is not an option — it is a methodology. It was not born of a strategic plan, but of a founding conviction refined year after year, team after team, coach after coach.

The Method rests on three structural pillars that reinforce one another and make the club's cultural regression impossible.

🏫
The Escoleta Mixta (from age 5)

Mixed groups from day one. Learning to play together, to dribble the ball together, to win and lose together. Equality is not taught — it is lived from the very first bounce. When a five-year-old girl plays alongside a boy and is valued exactly the same, equality becomes the norm, not the exception.

⭐
The Ainhoa López Case

A career that illustrates the method in action: from El Clot to top-level competition. Player in the Liga Femenina Endesa, international with the Spanish National Team, Cum Laude from UB-INEFC. She returned to the neighbourhood while recovering from an autoimmune disease and is today the club's official ambassador. The Barna Method as a lever for talent and social advancement.

🏗️
Continuity Guaranteed by Structure

The Equality Plan and the 8M Project ensure that the method does not depend on individuals but on structures. Equality as a system, not as an exception. When people change, the structures endure. This is the guarantee of the sustainability of the Barna model.

03
Structure and Commitment · Four Pillars of Equality

Concrete, documented and verifiable actions to build a gender-balanced future.

CB Grup Barna's commitment to equality is not merely declarative. It is structured around four operational strands with assigned owners, measurement indicators and public accountability.

🏋️‍♀️ 8M Project — Coaches

A programme created to identify, train and support women who want to become basketball coaches. We are closing the access gap to the bench. So that today's girls see female technical role models on their teams.

🤝 Women's Mentoring Programme

Structured mentoring for female coaches and referees. Women who have walked the path support those who are starting out. Knowledge as a collective asset that is not lost between generations.

📋 Club Equality Plan

A formal document with measurable objectives. Not a statement of intent — an operational roadmap with owners, deadlines and success indicators for every area of the club. Reviewable and updatable annually.

🛡️ Child Protection Officer

The backbone project of 2024. Clear protocols, mandatory training and safe reporting channels. So that basketball is a safe space for every child in El Clot and Barcelona.

Women's Leadership · Women Who Decide

Equality begins at the decision-making table.

In the last three years, CB Grup Barna has opened its Board of Directors to women's voices for the first time in its sixty-year history. Not as a symbolic gesture — as a founding act of a new governance model.

The board is a reflection of the club's values. If we want gender-balanced basketball, we have to be gender-balanced ourselves first. This conviction has permanently transformed the club's decision-making structure.

06
Sports Policy · Institutional Advocacy

The club as an active agent of local sports policy.

CB Grup Barna does not wait for institutions to define policy. It proposes, negotiates and implements. In recent years, the club has established formal alliances with the Sant Martí District and Barcelona City Council for the promotion of women's sport, becoming a leading interlocutor in shaping local sports policy.

The Barna model has been formally presented to the Sports Department of the Government of Catalonia and to the Barcelona Mayor's Office team, with the Unified Report on women's basketball in Barcelona serving as the base document for the 2026-2030 Plan.

07
Economic Innovation · Funding Model

KM0 sponsors: when the local economy funds the women's future.

One of CB Grup Barna's most significant innovations in recent years is the KM0 sponsors programme: a funding model in which local businesswomen invest directly in the club's women's teams. It is not philanthropy — it is a strategic bet and a demonstration that the local economy can play an active role in building a more equitable sport.

The circle is virtuous: neighbourhood businesswomen who believe in gender equality fund sporting opportunities for the neighbourhood's girls. When the women who lead the local economy decide to invest in local women's sport, the message is powerful: women's basketball has value. It has value for the community, for business and for the future.

The club has produced videos featuring testimonials from its sponsoring businesswomen, giving voice to the reasons why they decided to link their brand to CB Grup Barna and women's basketball. These videos have had a strong impact on the club's social media and have generated a pull effect for other potential sponsors.

When the neighbourhood's women invest in the neighbourhood's girls, the circle of equality closes. CB Grup Barna created this model. And it works.

09
Closing case · Why the Award

Why CB Grup Barna deserves the City Sports Club Award.

Because it is not a club that created an equality programme to win an award. It is a club that has built, over sixty-one years, a culture, a methodology and structures that make equality its way of being.

Because the 200 girls who play on CB Grup Barna's teams deserve nothing less than the recognition of their city. Because El Clot is Barcelona, and Barcelona should celebrate what El Clot makes possible.

Because the Barna Method is replicable. Recognising it is an incentive for other clubs in the region to adopt it. The award is not the goal — it is the catalyst for the systemic change Barcelona needs to become the women's basketball capital of Europe.
