Cistella Petita

If the 3x3 is the city's open basketball party, the Cistella Petita is the party for the youngest: CB Grup Barna's neighborhood mini-basketball festival, designed so children aged 3 to 8 experience their big day as players.

Request information Discover the Escoleta

The little ones' big day

Games adapted to children's size, activities, medals for everyone and families in the stands. It's often many children's first contact with basketball and with CB Grup Barna — and the natural gateway into the club's Escoleta.

For the club, the Cistella Petita is much more than a fun day out: it's the first link in a journey that starts at age 3 and, as the club's two senior teams show, can reach the elite of Catalan basketball without ever leaving the same club.

How to sign up

You don't need to belong to the Escoleta to take part: the Cistella Petita is a day open to the whole neighborhood. Dates and registration for the next edition are announced on this page and on Instagram (@cbgrupbarna); for any question, you can write via the club's WhatsApp or contact Julio Torralba directly at 646 205 526.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Cistella Petita?

CB Grup Barna's neighborhood mini-basketball festival, designed so children aged 3 to 8 experience their big day as players: adapted games, activities, medals for everyone and families in the stands.

Who can take part?

Children aged 3 to 8, whether or not they belong to the club's Escoleta. It's often many children's first contact with basketball and with CB Grup Barna.

When is the next edition?

The Cistella Petita is held once a year, in June. Dates are announced on this page and on Instagram (@cbgrupbarna).

Does the Cistella Petita lead into the Escoleta?

Yes. It's the natural gateway into CB Grup Barna's Escoleta, the basketball introduction program for children aged 4 to 7.

Want your child to take part?

We'll let you know when registration opens for the next edition.
