Theory 1 · Research framework

The Invisible Market.

Female talent exists, it generates value, but the system does not measure it correctly. When a club corrects the undervaluation, it captures a surplus the rest of the market cannot see.

The first hidden pattern

Female talent as an unpriced asset.

Grassroots women's basketball operates as an invisible market: it exists, it generates value, but it is not priced. The 157,432 women with a federation licence in Spain (35.7% of the total) represent an asset the system systematically undervalues. Not because it is worth less, but because nobody measures it correctly.

When a club assigns 65% of its budget to the men's side, it is not doing so from performance analysis but from inertia. The men's side has always had more; therefore, it "deserves" more. This is a cognitive bias called anchoring: the historical distribution determines the future distribution without any data-based review.

CB Grup Barna broke the anchor decades ago. The result: the women's senior team competes in the FCBQ Super Cup; the men's team fights to avoid relegation from the Copa Catalunya. Female talent was not inferior; it was invisible to the market.

«Investment creates level. Level creates role models. Role models create demand.»

Pillar 01 · The Barna Method
Theoretical formulation

The Invisible Market Theory.

In any sporting ecosystem where a segment systematically receives less than 40% of resources despite representing more than 30% of participants, an untapped surplus of talent exists. The first club to correct the undervaluation will capture that surplus at a marginal cost lower than that of the overvalued segment.

This explains why the 50% of CB Grup Barna's budget allocated to women generates results superior to the men's side with the same total budget. It is not magic; it is microeconomics: in an undervalued market, every euro yields more.

International validation

The WNBA explosion: the invisible market is revealed.

The WNBA has experienced growth of +183% in valuation, +48% in attendance and +139% in audience in a single year. The $2.2bn broadcast deal (4× the previous one) takes effect in 2026.

The WNBA's invisible market is now visible. What changed? Not the talent (the players were already there); the investment in visibility. The lesson applies directly to Catalan basketball: the 25,965 players with an FCBQ licence are an invisible market waiting to be revealed.

The Barna Method is the revelation mechanism.
