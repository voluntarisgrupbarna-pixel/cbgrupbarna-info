Summer camp

Time Chamber Camp 2026: programme, prices and venue

Six weeks, six different technical focuses, at La Nau del Clot. The camp CB Grup Barna runs with Time Chamber now has its programme and prices set for the summer.

CB Grup Barna · 2026-08-16

Skills training, not a holiday club

CB Grup Barna's camp is not a leisure activity with a ball: it is skills training, with sessions devoted to one specific aspect of the game and groups formed by age and level. The club runs it with Time Chamber, which brings the individual-work methodology — short, highly repetitive sessions on a single aspect of the game. The Barna brings the other half: sixty-one years of the team atmosphere of a neighbourhood club.

The six weeks

  • Flow Camp (23–27 June) — Offensive fluidity and playing on the move.
  • TC Basics (30 June–4 July) — Fundamentals: footwork, balance, mechanics.
  • Shooting Academy (7–11 July) — Shooting mechanics, rhythm and confidence.
  • One & One Mastery (14–18 July) — One-on-one, fakes and offensive creativity.
  • Ballhandling Lab (21–25 July) — Ball handling and dribbling under pressure.
  • Skills Lab Experience (28 July–1 Aug) — Combined work on every skill.

Each week has its own identity: anyone coming for more than one week does not repeat content.

The venue: La Nau del Clot

Carrer de la Llacuna 172, 08018 Barcelona — the club's main sports venue, in the Clot neighbourhood. Early drop-off from 9am and lunch included with the full day.

Who can come

The camp is open to players from any club in Barcelona and the province, not just Barna players. Every week there are places for club members and places for players from outside, and groups are formed by age and level.

The full programme, with every week, the prices and photos from the previous edition, is on the camp page. There is also a full presentation to share with families.

Frequently asked questions

How much does the Time Chamber camp cost?

€195 for the full week (9am to 5pm, lunch included) and €160 for half days (9am to 1.30pm). It can be paid in up to 3 instalments.

What weeks does the camp have?

Six: Flow Camp, TC Basics, Shooting Academy, One & One Mastery, Ballhandling Lab and Skills Lab Experience, each with a different technical focus.

Do you have to be a CB Grup Barna player to sign up?

No. The camp is open to players from any club in Barcelona and the province.
