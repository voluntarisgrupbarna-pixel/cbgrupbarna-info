Christmas basketball camp in Barcelona

Basketball skills training over the Christmas school holidays, at La Nau del Clot, Sant Martí district. Short format, groups by age and level and limited places, open to players from any club in Barcelona and the province.

What Barna's Christmas camp is

The Christmas holidays last two weeks and fall right in the middle of the season. That is what the Christmas camp is for: so the holidays are not two blank weeks, and so anyone who wants to improve one specific thing in their game has somewhere to do it.

It is skills training, not a holiday club with a ball. Every session has a technical goal — shooting, ball handling, 1v1, reading the game — with a lot of repetition and individual correction, following the individual-training method of Time Chamber, the same partner the club runs the summer camp.

Dates, times and price

The camp runs on the weekdays of the Christmas school holidays, at La Nau del Clot. The exact dates and the price for this year's edition are published on this page during October, at the same time as on Instagram (@cbgrupbarna), just before sign-ups open.

Anyone who wants to hear before it goes public — the weeks fill up in the order people sign up — can ask on the club's WhatsApp and join the notice list.

Who it is for

For youth players, fromEscoleta age up to under-16 and under-18. Groups are made up by age and by level: someone starting out does not train with someone who has been competing for six years, or the other way round. It is mixed, girls and boys, with the same standards.

And it is open: you do not have to play for CB Grup Barna. Around 150 childrencame through the 2026 summer camp, from the club and from other clubs in Barcelona and the province.

Where it is held

At La Nau del Clot, Carrer de la Llacuna 172, 08018 Barcelona, in El Clot, Sant Martí district. It is the club's main court, right by the Parc del Clot and well connected by metro and bus from anywhere in Barcelona. The other courts we train on are listed under facilities.

And at Easter?

The same format, one term later. Everything we do outside the summer is explained under basketball skills training in Barcelona: Christmas, Easter and the long six-week camp in June and July.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a basketball camp in Barcelona at Christmas?

Yes. CB Grup Barna runs one at La Nau del Clot, in the Sant Martí district, on the weekdays of the Christmas school holidays, in a short skills-training format.

When are the dates and how much does it cost?

The dates and price for each edition are published on this page and on Instagram during October, before sign-ups open. To hear earlier, ask on the club's WhatsApp (+34 698 425 153).

What ages is it for?

From Escoleta age up to under-16 and under-18, always in groups by age and level.

Do you have to be a club player?

No. It is open to players from any club in Barcelona and the province.

What is worked on?

Shooting, ball handling, 1v1 and reading the game, with Time Chamber's individual-training method. Every session has a specific technical goal.

Where is it held?

At La Nau del Clot, Carrer de la Llacuna 172, 08018 Barcelona.
