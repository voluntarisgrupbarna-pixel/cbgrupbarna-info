The Barna, among the giants of Catalan basketball: what the FCBQ says

Barça, Joventut, Manresa, Girona… when people talk about the giants of Catalan basketball, a neighborhood club rarely gets a mention. The official club record at basquetcatala.cat says something else.

CB Grup Barna · 2026-08-13

What "development and competitive club" means

It isn't a slogan: it's how the Catalan Basketball Federation (FCBQ) itself organizes competition. Every category has several levels —1st year for players just entering it, Preferent or Interterritorial for those already a year in, and Promoció for anyone who wants to keep playing without the pressure of the top level. A "development" club is one that uses this whole ladder. A "competitive" club is one that, on top of that, reaches the top rung. The Barna does both at once, and the federation's own figures make the case better than any mission statement.

The Barna's pyramid, from the inside

The club's official record at basquetcatala.cat shows more than 34 federated teams, from Pre-Mini (ages 8-9) through to Senior, with parallel men's and women's structures and, in most categories, up to three levels: an A team at the competitive ceiling (Interterritorial, or Preferent where there is none), a B team made up of players in their first year in the category, and often a third development team. In both senior sides —men's and women's— there's a first team and a reserve, and the two first teams compete in the FCBQ Súper Copa, the top Catalan territorial category.

And what do the giants of Catalan basketball have?

This is the surprising part: among clubs with a first team in Liga ACB or Liga Femenina Endesa, the usual pattern is that the grassroots base and the senior first team aren't the same entity.

  • FC Barcelona — 10 federated teams in Catalonia, all men's, from Mini to Junior, with no Pre-Mini. And no women's team at all: the blaugrana women's basketball project (Barça CBS, in partnership with CB Santfeliuenc) closed at the end of the 2024-25 season for financial reasons, after reaching Liga F Endesa. Its men's reserve team plays Copa Catalunya, not Súper Copa; the first team plays Liga ACB.
  • Joventut Badalona (la Penya) — Catalonia's largest grassroots base: 53 teams, from Pre-Mini to Junior, in both genders. But with no senior team of its own within this entity: whoever reaches the top leaves the club.
  • Bàsquet Manresa — actually two different clubs depending on gender: the men's base (17 teams, no senior team) and Manresa CBF women's, a separate entity with a senior team in Liga Femenina 2, the national league.
  • Fundació Bàsquet Girona — 23 teams, from Pre-Mini to Junior, in both genders, chaired by Marc Gasol. It has no senior team of its own either: the first team plays in Liga Endesa, outside this record.

It's a logical pattern for clubs that feed the professional elite: the grassroots base develops players, and whoever reaches the top usually jumps to a separate professional structure. It makes sense for them. It isn't, however, the only way to grow.

At the big clubs, development and the first team are almost never the same entity. At the Barna, they are: the same player who joins the Escoleta at age four can go on to play the Súper Copa without ever changing shirts.

What sets us apart

Of the clubs analyzed, Grup Barna is the only one with two senior teams —first and reserve— in both genders at once, within the same entity that runs the entire development pyramid, both competing in the FCBQ Súper Copa. Neither Barça, nor la Penya, nor Manresa nor Girona have it this integrated. We're not the club with the most teams in Catalonia —la Penya has 53— nor the most renowned. We are, though, one of the few clubs where developing as a player and competing at the highest level happen at the same club, the thread never broken.

Frequently asked questions

How many teams does CB Grup Barna have?

More than 34 federated teams, from Pre-Mini to Senior, with parallel men's and women's structures and up to three levels per category (A, B and Black), according to the club's official record at basquetcatala.cat.

What is the FCBQ's Súper Copa?

It's the top territorial senior category organized by the Catalan Basketball Federation. CB Grup Barna competes in it with its two first teams, men's and women's, in the 2026-27 season.

Does the Barna play against Barça or la Penya?

Not with the first teams: Barça plays in Liga ACB and la Penya (Joventut Badalona) does too, outside the FCBQ's territorial competitions. At development level the paths do cross, within the same federation structure.

What sets the Barna apart from a small neighborhood club?

Having a complete structure, from Pre-Mini to Senior, and its own senior first team in both genders competing in the Súper Copa — something neither Barça, la Penya nor Girona have within the same entity as their grassroots base.
