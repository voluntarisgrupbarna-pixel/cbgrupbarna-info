3x3 basketball in Barcelona: every tournament and circuit, compared

Which 3x3 basketball tournaments and circuits run in Barcelona, how a federation circuit with FIBA points differs from an urban festival or a neighbourhood court tournament, and where CB Grup Barna's 3x3 Westfield Glòries plays. Written by CB Grup Barna, organiser of the tournament in El Clot-Glòries since 2024.

The main 3x3 tournaments and circuits in Barcelona

Three quite different families of 3x3 coexist in Barcelona: the FCBQ's federation circuit, with points for the FIBA ranking and official structure; urban festivals, multi-sport events where 3x3 is one discipline among several; and neighbourhood court tournaments, lightweight and local. Here is a snapshot of the best-known options:

Federation, festival or neighbourhood court: the first decision

A federation circuit such as the FCBQ's awards points for the FIBA ranking and has official structure across Catalonia: worth it if the goal is serious competition and ranking points. An urban festival such as Extreme Barcelona puts 3x3 alongside other Olympic disciplines — BMX, skateboarding, breaking — with an international feel and free admission. A neighbourhood court tournament such as Street Champion is the lightest format: a small entry fee, a street atmosphere, without much more structure.

The 3x3 Westfield Glòries does not fit neatly into any of the three boxes: it started as a neighbourhood tournament — CB Grup Barna, Time Chamber and Eix Clot — but has moved up a level to carry FIBA status with world ranking points at senior level since its 3rd edition (2026), tracked on FIBA Play, without ever losing its neighbourhood feel or its equal prize money.

FIBA points: who has them in Barcelona

The Circuit 3x3 FCBQ awards FIBA ranking points in its federation categories. The 3x3 Westfield Glòries joined it in 2026: the Senior Men's Pro and Senior Women's Pro categories award points for the FIBA 3x3 world ranking, tracked on FIBA Play, while every other category — from Escoleta to veterans — competes without that requirement. Neither of the urban festivals (Extreme Barcelona) nor the court tournaments (Street Champion) award FIBA points: they are participation formats, not ranking ones.

The gap left by the Circuito 3x3 CaixaBank

The Circuito 3x3 CaixaBank is, with 11 cities in 2026, Spain's largest urban basketball festival. Barcelona is not among its stops this edition: a real visibility gap for the city that none of the local tournaments — the Westfield Glòries included — can fill on its own, but one that marks out where there is room to grow for whoever organises 3x3 in the city.

The 3x3 Westfield Glòries, in one line

An urban 3x3 basketball tournament in El Clot-Glòries organised by CB Grup Barna, Time Chamber and Eix Clot: 10 categories from Escoleta to veterans, FIBA points and FIBA Play tracking at senior level, and €2,000 in equal prize money — €1,000 for the winning Senior Women's team and €1,000 for the winning Senior Men's team, unique in Barcelona. The 3rd edition (June 2026) brought together a record 113 teams and around 508 players across three venues within a 15-minute walk of each other, with the inclusive Barna Màgics category played during Pride Month.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best 3x3 tournament in Barcelona?

It depends what you are looking for. For federation competition with FIBA points and official structure, the Circuit 3x3 FCBQ; for an international urban festival, Extreme Barcelona; for a neighbourhood tournament with an equal prize and FIBA points at senior level, CB Grup Barna's 3x3 Westfield Glòries.

Are there 3x3 tournaments with FIBA points in Barcelona?

Yes. The Circuit 3x3 FCBQ and, since its 3rd edition (2026), the 3x3 Westfield Glòries at its senior categories.

Does Barcelona have a stop on the Circuito 3x3 CaixaBank?

No, in this 2026 edition the circuit's 11 stops do not include Barcelona.

Do you have to be federated to play?

Not always: the Circuit 3x3 FCBQ and the 3x3 Westfield Glòries are open at non-federated categories; only senior players competing for FIBA points face requirements. Extreme Barcelona and Street Champion are open to any team.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best 3x3 basketball tournament in Barcelona?

It depends what you are looking for. For federation competition with FIBA ranking points and official structure across Catalonia, the Circuit 3x3 FCBQ. For an international urban sports festival alongside other Olympic disciplines, Extreme Barcelona at Parc del Fòrum. For a neighbourhood tournament with an equal cash prize (€1,000 for the winning women's senior team and €1,000 for the men's, unique in Barcelona) and FIBA points at senior level, the CB Grup Barna's 3x3 Westfield Glòries in El Clot-Glòries, which brought together 113 teams across three venues in 2026.

Are there 3x3 tournaments with FIBA points in Barcelona?

Yes. The Circuit 3x3 FCBQ awards points for the FIBA 3x3 ranking in its federation categories, and CB Grup Barna's 3x3 Westfield Glòries also carries FIBA status with world ranking points at senior level since its 3rd edition (2026), tracked on FIBA Play.

Does Barcelona have a stop on the Circuito 3x3 CaixaBank?

No. The Circuito 3x3 CaixaBank 2026, Spain's largest urban basketball festival with 11 cities, does not include Barcelona among its stops this edition.

Do you have to be a federated player to play 3x3 in Barcelona?

Not always. The Circuit 3x3 FCBQ is open across every category, from pre-mini to senior, with no federation licence required to take part. The 3x3 Westfield Glòries does not require one at its open categories either; only senior, where FIBA points are awarded. Extreme Barcelona and Street Champion are open to any team.

Where is the 3x3 Westfield Glòries played?

Across three venues in the El Clot-Glòries neighbourhood, all within a 15-minute walk of each other: Westfield Glòries, La Nau del Clot and the Rambleta del Clot. The tournament is organised by CB Grup Barna, Time Chamber and Eix Clot.
