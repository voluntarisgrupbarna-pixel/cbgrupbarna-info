What the Barna camp is

The CB Grup Barna basketball camp is the intensive training programme the club runs during school holidays in the El Clot neighbourhood. It isn't a leisure activity with a ball: it's skills training, with sessions dedicated to one specific aspect of the game and groups organised by age and level.

The club runs it together with Time Chamber, which brings its individual-training methodology. That combination is what defines the camp: the repetition volume of a skills-training academy with the team spirit of a neighbourhood club with sixty-one years of history.

How it's organised

Each week has its own focus, so anyone attending more than one week doesn't repeat content. These are the modules the club has run in recent editions:

  • Flow Camp — game reading and decision-making.
  • TC Basics — fundamentals: footwork, balance, mechanics.
  • Shooting Academy — shooting, in all its game situations.
  • One & One Mastery — one-on-one play.
  • Ballhandling Lab — ball handling and dribbling under pressure.
  • Skills Lab Experience — combined skills work.

Who it's for

Boys and girls who already play basketball or want to start, whether they belong to CB Grup Barna or come from another club. Each week the club reserves places for its own players and places open to players from outside, and groups are closed by age and level so no one trains above or below their level.

If your child hasn't started yet and is between 4 and 7 years old, the natural path isn't the camp but the Escoleta, the club's basketball school, which runs all year round with Julio Torralba.

Frequently asked questions

Which coaches and players have visited the camp?

Recent editions have hosted Ainhoa López, a professional player and Spain international who trained in El Clot; Robert Willet (@bballwillett), an NBA skills trainer; and Malak Shady, a basketball creator and 3x3 MVP. They run sessions on court with the camp players.

How many players came to the camp in summer 2026?

Around 150 girls and boys across the six weeks of the 2026 edition, in groups by age and level and with a limit of roughly 50 places per week. Players came both from CB Grup Barna and from other clubs in Barcelona and the province.

What ages is the basketball camp for?

The CB Grup Barna camp is for youth players, from Escoleta age up to cadet and junior categories. Groups are organised by age and level, so a child who's just starting doesn't train alongside someone who's been competing for years.

Do you have to play for CB Grup Barna to sign up?

No. The camp is open to players from any club in Barcelona and the province. Every week there are places for Barna players and places for players from outside.

How many places are there per week?

The camp works with a limit of around 50 places per week to keep a good coach-to-player ratio. Weeks fill up in registration order, and recent editions have sold out before starting.

Where is the camp held?

In the El Clot neighbourhood, Sant Martí District of Barcelona. La Nau del Clot is CB Grup Barna's main sports venue.

When does registration open for the next edition?

The dates for the next edition are announced on this page and on Instagram (@cbgrupbarna). Anyone who wants to be notified before it opens to the public can ask via the club's WhatsApp (+34 698 425 153) and will be added to the notification list.
