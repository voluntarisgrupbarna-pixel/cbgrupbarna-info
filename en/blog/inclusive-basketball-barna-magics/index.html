Inclusive basketball: how an adapted sports team works

The Barna Màgics aren't a corporate social responsibility programme. They're a team of the club: with their own calendar, weekly training sessions, coaching staff and the same shirt as everyone else.

CB Grup Barna · 2026-08-14

What the Barna Màgics are

It's CB Grup Barna's basketball team for people with intellectual disabilities. They train on the same court as every other team, share the gym and changing rooms, compete on their own calendar and appear on the same schedule as the cadet or senior sides. The project runs in partnership with the Fundació Mullor and ACELL, the Catalan federation for sport among people with intellectual disabilities.

The distinction matters: this isn't a parallel activity the club runs on the side. It's a team within the structure, on equal footing with the others.

Why the difference matters

An inclusion programme living in an annex ends up depending on who happens to run it and whether there's budget that year. A team built into the club's structure has the same guarantees as any other: an assigned court, coaching staff, a calendar and a place in the club's communications.

It also changes what happens at the venue. When an adapted sports team trains next to the pre-youth squad, the club's children grow up seeing it as the most normal thing in the world, because to them it is. No awareness talk is needed: sharing a gym for years does the job.

What a training session looks like

It looks a lot more like any other team's session than people imagine. Warm-up, technical work, game situations and a scrimmage. They work on shooting mechanics, defensive positioning and the rules. What changes is the pace of progress and how things are explained, not the demands or the respect.

Competition takes place within the ACELL circuit and, in the context of adapted sport, at Special Olympics-type gatherings. In the 2025-26 season the club has been a lead participant in the Catalan Basketball Federation's +BÀSQUET outreach programme.

Who can take part

People with intellectual disabilities who want to play basketball, regardless of prior level. No experience is needed: some arrive without ever having touched a ball. As with every other team, the first day is a no-commitment trial.

If you're interested for someone in your family, write to us and we'll explain schedules and how registration works: you can do it via the club's WhatsApp or by leaving your details in the form.

The context: a club that does it all at once

Adapted sport is usually the first thing to fall when a club is stretched thin. In El Clot it lives alongside a 1:1 balance between men's and women's teams, 50% of the budget going to women's basketball, and a full pathway starting at age 4. Not because the club is big — it isn't — but because it's how the club understands what a neighborhood club is for.

It's worth saying it plainly: a team like the Màgics doesn't produce flashy results or headlines. It produces something else, and anyone who has spent an afternoon in the stands at La Nau del Clot during one of their games knows exactly what that is.

Frequently asked questions

What are the Barna Màgics?

CB Grup Barna's basketball team for people with intellectual disabilities, built into the club's structure on equal footing with every other team, in partnership with the Fundació Mullor and ACELL.

Is prior experience needed to play?

No. Some players arrive having never played before. As with every other team at the club, the first day is a no-commitment trial.

Where do they train and compete?

They train at La Nau del Clot, the same venue as the club's other teams, in Barcelona's Sant Martí District, and compete within ACELL's adapted sport circuit.

What is the FCBQ's +BÀSQUET programme?

A Catalan Basketball Federation programme to spread basketball across the territory. CB Grup Barna has been a lead participant during the 2025-26 season.

How can someone sign up for the team?

By writing to the club's WhatsApp (+34 698 425 153) or leaving contact details in the website's form. The club explains schedules, how registration works, and arranges a first trial session.

Want information about the club?

Leave us your name and contact details and we'll get back to you with information about the Escoleta, the camp or whichever team fits your child.
