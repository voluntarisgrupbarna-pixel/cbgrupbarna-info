Open days: the Escoleta and girls born in 2018

Two Saturdays to come and try a free training session at La Nau del Clot. This September the open days are for two groups only: the Escoleta, ages 4 to 8, and girls born in 2018, with whom we are building our new U10 girls' team.

Do you have a child of a different age? Places in the rest of the teams (U10 boys, U12, U13, U14, U16, U18, U22 and senior) are not booked here: they are filled through tryouts during the Easter holidays of 2027.

Who they are for

Escoleta · ages 4 to 8

Born between 2018 and 2022

Mixed teams and three groups by age. We are not looking for the best: we want them to want to come back next week. The ball, the game and the gang come first; technique follows on its own. Never played before? Even better: that is how everyone started.

What the Escoleta is like →

Girls born in 2018 · U10 girls

A brand-new team, girls only

We are building the 2018 girls' generation: a U10 team made up only of girls, to start competing in the Catalan Federation in the age group where federated girls' basketball begins. Is she 8, does she want to play with girls and would she love a team of her own? This is the moment. No experience needed.

At the Barna, girls' basketball is not a campaign: 8 federated girls' teams, the same budget as the boys' side and a pathway that runs from the Escoleta to the Spanish women's top flight. A Spar Girona captain and the head coach of Valencia Basket's women's team both came off this court.

How it works

  • When — Saturdays 19 and 26 September, at 9 am. Come on one day or both.
  • Where — La Nau del Clot, Carrer de la Llacuna 170-172, Sant Martí district. Arrive ten minutes early and ask for the Open Days.
  • What to bring — sports clothes, indoor trainers and a bottle of water. We provide the ball.
  • What it costs — nothing. The trial session is free and there is no commitment to stay.
  • You can watch — if you would rather see it before saying anything to your child, you can: just let us know.

Frequently asked questions

When are CB Grup Barna's open days?

Saturdays 19 and 26 September 2026, at 9 am, at La Nau del Clot. They are a free, no-commitment trial session, and this year for two groups only: the Escoleta (ages 4 to 8) and girls born in 2018, with whom we are building our new U10 girls' team. Booking is required: each Saturday takes 50 children.

What is the group for girls born in 2018?

It is the club's new girls' generation: a U10 team made up only of girls born in 2018, starting to compete in the Catalan Federation, the age group where federated girls' basketball begins. No experience needed. CB Grup Barna has 8 federated girls' teams and the same budget for the girls' side as for the boys'.

My child is 9 or older. Can they come to the open days?

No: this September's open days are only for the Escoleta (ages 4 to 8) and for girls born in 2018. For every other age group, from U10 boys to senior, places for the 2027-28 season are filled through tryouts during the Easter holidays of 2027. Apply with the form at cbgrupbarna.info/en/tryouts and the club confirms the day and time by email.

What should we bring on the first training session?

Sports clothes, indoor court shoes and a water bottle. We provide the ball. The trial session is free.

Do we need to book for the open days, or can we just turn up?

You need to book, because each Saturday takes only 50 children and closes when full. It takes a minute with the form: name, age, year of birth, which of the two groups and an email. The confirmation arrives straight away.

My daughter has never played basketball. Will she fit in?

Yes. No previous experience is needed: plenty of our players arrived having never played. Ages 4 to 8 join the Escoleta, in mixed groups by age; girls born in 2018 join the new U10 girls' team. The open-day session is a free trial.

Can I stay and watch the session?

Yes. If you would rather come and see it before telling your child anything, that is fine too: just say so when you book.
