Tryouts: want to play for the Barna?

From U10 to senior, boys and girls, places in CB Grup Barna's federated teams for the 2027-28 season are filled through tryouts during the Easter holidays of 2027, at La Nau del Clot. Fill in the request and we will email you the day and time.

Aged 4 to 8, or a girl born in 2018? Then no tryout is needed: they have a direct place at the September Open Days, Saturdays 19 and 26.

Book at the Open Days →

Who they are for

Age groups go by year of birth, not by school year. For the 2027-28 season, as a guide:

  • U10 (Premini)born in 2018 and 2019Boys. Girls born in 2018 go straight to the Open Days.
  • U12 (Mini)born in 2016 and 2017Boys and girls.
  • U13 (Preinfantil)born in 2015Boys and girls.
  • U14 (Infantil)born in 2014Boys and girls.
  • U16 (Cadet)born in 2012 and 2013Boys and girls.
  • U18 (Júnior)born in 2010 and 2011Boys and girls.
  • U22 and seniorborn in 2009 or earlierMen and women. Both senior A teams compete in the FCBQ Supercopa.

Born in 2019 or later: Escoleta, no tryout. The current teams are listed under Teams.

How it works

  1. Fill in the request below. One minute: name, year of birth, whether they already play for a club and how to reach you.
  2. Before Easter we email you the day and time of the tryout. They take place during the Easter school holidays of 2027 at La Nau del Clot.
  3. Come to the tryout. A training session with the team for their age and the club's coaches. Sports clothes, indoor trainers and water; we provide the ball.
  4. We tell you whether there is a place and in which team, and the steps to register for the 2027-28 season.

What we look at in the tryout

Not just ability. We look at how they play with others, whether they listen, whether they want to learn and whether they enjoy it. Technical and physical level for the age counts, but a player who adds to the team is worth more than one who only shoots. You do not need to come from another club: there is room for beginners too.

If they already play federated basketball, tell us the club: the federation licence is processed with the FCBQ and we need to know in good time.

What happens to your data

What you write here is read by the club's sporting management and is used only to organise the tryout and reply to you. We do not add it to any list or use it for anything else. Full details in the privacy policy, and how we protect children's data in Child Protection.

Frequently asked questions

When are CB Grup Barna's tryouts?

During the Easter school holidays of 2027, at La Nau del Clot, to fill places for the 2027-28 season. The exact day and time for each age group is confirmed by email to everyone who has sent a request, before Easter.

Which ages are the tryouts for?

All federated age groups, boys and girls: U10 (born 2018-2019), U12 (2016-2017), U13 (2015), U14 (2014), U16 (2012-2013), U18 (2010-2011) and U22 and senior (2009 or earlier), as a guide for the 2027-28 season. Children born in 2019 or later go to the Escoleta and girls born in 2018 to the September open days, with no tryout.

Do you need to have played for a club to try out?

No. There is room for beginners and for players who already play federated basketball. At the tryout we do not look only at technical level: we look at how they play with others, whether they listen, whether they want to learn and whether they enjoy it. If they already play for another club, tell us in the request, because the federation licence is processed with the FCBQ and we need to know in good time.

What happens after the tryout?

The club emails you to say whether there is a place and in which team, and the steps to register for the 2027-28 season. If there is no place at that moment, you stay on the waiting list for that age group and we let you know if one opens up.
