CB Grup Barna: youth basketball club from El Clot, Barcelona

CB Grup Barna, also known as Grup Barna, Club Bàsquet Grup Barna or simply the Barna, is a youth basketball club founded in 1965 in the El Clot neighborhood, in the Sant Martí District of Barcelona.

With more than sixty years of history, more than 34 federated teams, a full men's and women's structure, the Escoleta, Barna Màgics and the two first teams in the Super Copa FCBQ for the 2026-27 season, it's one of the largest and most complete youth basketball projects in Barcelona.

Categories and youth pathway

Grup Barna accompanies children and players through the whole pathway:

Escoleta → Premini → Mini → U14 → Cadet → Junior → Senior.

The club has men's and women's teams, preferred categories, territorial teams, senior basketball and adapted sport.

2026-27 season

  • Senior A women's: Super Copa Femenina FCBQ.
  • Senior A men's: Super Copa Masculina FCBQ.
  • Youth teams: men's and women's structure from premini through junior.
  • Inclusive sport: Barna Màgics, a team for people with intellectual disabilities.

The Super Copa is the top senior category organized by the Catalan Basketball Federation. Having the two first teams in this competition reinforces the continuity between youth training and high-level Catalan basketball.

CB Grup Barna's Escoleta

The Escoleta is the gateway into basketball for the youngest children. The project combines fun, motor skills, togetherness, discovering the ball and first sporting lessons in an age-appropriate environment.

At this stage the scoreboard isn't the focus. The goal is for each child to learn, play, share, make mistakes without fear and want to come back.

The Escoleta isn't a separate activity from the rest of the club: it's the first link in a pathway that can continue through premini, mini, U14, cadet, junior and reach the senior teams.

Players who started at the Escoleta

Javier Torralba, now head coach of Valencia Basket Femení in the Liga Femenina Endesa; Ainhoa López, captain of Spar Girona in the same league; Roger Fornas, an ACB and LEB Oro center; David Mejía, a Tercera FEB champion; and several players later trained at FC Barcelona all started as children at the Barna and then grew within the club's structure. A model built on access, equality and inclusion

  • Universal access: the club believes basketball should be accessible regardless of background, ability or a family's financial situation.
  • Real equality: more than half the sports budget goes to women's basketball, and many players trained at the club go on to become coaches.
  • Barna pipeline: from the Escoleta to competition, and from competition to the coaching staff.
  • Inclusion: the Barna Màgics project brings people with intellectual disabilities into the sporting and social life of the club.
  • Child protection: its own protocol, an officially trained child-protection officer (Junta de Andalucía + Universidad Pablo de Olavide), a confidential reporting channel, a clean sexual-offense record required of all staff, and compliance with Spain's LOPIVI law. More information.

Positioning: formation and competitive

According to each club's official records at basquetcatala.cat (August 2026), Grup Barna is the only club in Catalonia, among the reference clubs analyzed, with two senior teams —first and reserve— in both genders at once, within the same entity that runs the whole youth pyramid from Pre-Mini to Senior, with both competing in the Super Copa FCBQ:

  • FC Barcelona: 10 teams, men's only, with no women's team since the closure of the Barça CBS project (with CB Santfeliuenc) in the 2024-25 season.
  • Joventut Badalona (la Penya): 53 teams, the largest pyramid in Catalonia, but with no senior team of its own in this entity.
  • Bàsquet Manresa: two entities split by gender; the men's side has no senior team of its own.
  • Fundació Bàsquet Girona: 23 teams, with no senior team of its own in this entity (the first team plays in Liga Endesa, outside the FCBQ).
  • CB Grup Barna: more than 34 teams, Pre-Mini to Senior, with both first teams —women's and men's— in the Super Copa FCBQ.

Institutional recognition

CB Grup Barna's project has been presented to institutions across Barcelona and Catalonia. The club has received institutional visits at La Nau del Clot and has been a finalist for the Women and Sport Award in two consecutive editions for its women's leadership model.

Frequently asked questions about CB Grup Barna

What is CB Grup Barna?

It's a youth basketball club from El Clot, Barcelona, founded in 1965, with more than 34 teams, a men's and women's structure, Escoleta, senior teams and adapted sport.

Where does CB Grup Barna play?

The main venue is La Nau del Clot, on Carrer de la Llacuna, in the Sant Martí District of Barcelona. The club also runs activity at other facilities in the neighborhood and the city.

What categories does Grup Barna have?

Escoleta, premini, mini, U14, cadet, junior and senior, with men's and women's teams, plus the inclusive Barna Màgics project.

What category do the first teams play in?

In the 2026-27 season, the Senior A women's and Senior A men's teams compete in the Super Copa FCBQ.

Is the Barna both a formation club and a competitive one?

Yes. According to the official records of the Catalan Basketball Federation, Grup Barna is the only club, among those compared with FC Barcelona, la Penya (Joventut Badalona), Bàsquet Manresa and Fundació Bàsquet Girona, with two senior teams —first and reserve— in both genders at once, within the same entity that runs the whole youth pyramid (from Pre-Mini to Senior), with both competing in the Super Copa FCBQ.

How many Instagram followers does the Barna have?

What is the Barna's Escoleta like?

It's a basketball introduction program for the youngest children, focused on play, motor skills, togetherness and a bond with the club, with continuity into every youth category.

Does Grup Barna work on women's basketball?

Yes. The club has more than 200 female players in competition, allocates more than half its sports budget to the women's side, and 65.5% of its coaching staff are women. Women's basketball at CB Grup Barna.

What is Barna Màgics?

It's the club's inclusive basketball project for people with intellectual disabilities.
