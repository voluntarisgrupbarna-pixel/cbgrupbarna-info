About Time Chamber

Time Chamber is a basketball development and high-performance academy based mainly at Barcelona's Velòdrom d'Horta, with a presence in other Catalan towns. It offers individual training combining technical, physical and mental work, and has a partnership with CB Grup Barna with whom it has run camps and showcases.
