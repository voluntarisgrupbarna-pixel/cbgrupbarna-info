Where the club trains and plays

CB Grup Barna splits training sessions and matches across six facilities in the Sant Martí District, all close to one another. Which court a team plays on depends on its category and schedule: check your team's schedule to find out exactly each week.

A common question

If you don't know which court your child trains or plays on, the fastest way to find out is to check your team's page, which is updated daily with upcoming matches and the pavilion assigned by the federation. For the Escoleta (ages 4 to 7): Wednesdays from 5:30pm to 6:40pm at Escola Casas, and Saturdays from 9:00am to 10:30am at La Nau del Clot. For any question, contact Julio Torralba at 646 205 526.
