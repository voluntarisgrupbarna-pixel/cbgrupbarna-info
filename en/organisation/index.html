Frequently asked questions

Who is the president of CB Grup Barna?

For the 2026-27 season the president is Santos García Yagüe and the vice-president is Pere Solanas. Adrià Mateo is technical director. The full list of the board and the section coordinators is on this page, as recorded in the club's official documents.

Who do I contact about what?

For the Escoleta (ages 4 to 8), Julio Torralba on 646 205 526. For general club enquiries, marqueting@cbgrupbarna.info or WhatsApp +34 698 425 153. For sponsorship and partnerships, the marketing director. And for anything to do with child protection there is a separate confidential channel: protecciomenorcbgrupbarna@gmail.com.
