Frequently asked questions

How many senior teams does CB Grup Barna have?

Two: Senior Men's A and Senior Women's A, both playing in the Super Copa FCBQ, the top regional division in Catalonia.

Where do the Grup Barna senior teams play?

Most home games are played at La Nau del Clot (Nau Parc Clot), in El Clot, in the Sant Martí district of Barcelona.

Can you reach the senior team without coming up through the club's Escoleta?

Yes — you can join any age group during the season if there is a place. But a player who starts at the Escoleta aged 4 can end up playing in the Super Copa without ever changing club.
