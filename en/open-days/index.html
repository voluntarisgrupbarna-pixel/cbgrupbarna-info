How it works

There is no single open day: the whole month of September is one. Tell us how old your child is, we will look at which group they belong to and tell you the day and time it trains. They come along, join in, and then you decide.

  • Ages 4 to 8 — Escoleta. Mixed teams and three groups by age.
  • From age 9 upwards — federated teams, by year of birth.
  • What to bring — sports clothes, indoor trainers and a bottle of water. We provide the ball.
  • What it costs — the trial session is free.

We train at La Nau del Clot, in the Sant Martí district. If you would rather come and watch before saying anything to your child, you can: just let us know.

Girls born in 2018: there are places in the team

The 2018 girls' team already exists and already trains. What we are looking for on 19 September are new players: girls who have never played and want to try it, and girls who already play somewhere else and want to move. No experience needed, and no need to have ever touched a ball.

At the Barna, women's basketball is not a section: it is eight league teams, the same number of female players as male ones, the same budget for the women's side as for the men's and 65.5% female coaches on the technical staff. A girl who starts in 2018 has the whole path laid out up to the senior team, inside the same club and on the same court.

Frequently asked questions

When are CB Grup Barna's open days?

On Saturdays 19 and 26 September 2026, 9.00 to 10.30 am, at La Nau del Clot (carrer de la Llacuna, 170-172, 08018 Barcelona). On top of that, the whole of September is a trial month: any child can come and train with us, free of charge and with no commitment to stay.

What should we bring on the first training session?

Sports clothes, indoor court shoes and a water bottle. We provide the ball. The trial session is free.

Do we need to book a trial session in advance, or can we just turn up?

You need to book, because each age group trains on a different day and time. Just send us the child's name, year of birth and a phone number or email: we reply the same day with their day and time.

My child has never played basketball. Will they fit in?

Yes. No previous experience is needed: plenty of our players arrived having never played. Ages 4 to 8 join the Escoleta, in mixed groups by age; from 9 upwards they join the league teams, grouped by year of birth. The first session is a free trial.

Can I stay and watch the session?

Yes. If you would rather come and see it before telling your child anything, that is fine too: just say so when you book the day.

Where are CB Grup Barna's open days held?

At La Nau del Clot, carrer de la Llacuna, 170-172, 08018 Barcelona, inside Parc del Clot in the Sant Martí district. It is walking distance from El Clot, Camp de l'Arpa, Glòries, Poblenou and the Sagrada Família.

Are there trials for girls born in 2018?

Yes. On Saturday 19 September 2026, 9.00 to 10.30 am, at La Nau del Clot, girls born in 2018 can try a session with the team, which already exists and is looking for new players. No previous experience is needed and there are no cuts. The trial is free and you need to book a place.
