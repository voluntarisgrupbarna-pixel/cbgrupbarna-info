Privacy Policy

What we do with the data you leave us on this website, explained without small print: what we ask for, why, how long we keep it, and how to get it deleted.

Last updated · 2026-08-14

Who processes your data

The data controller is Club Bàsquet Grup Barna (CB Grup Barna), a non-profit sports club with tax ID (NIF) G58432584 and registered address at C/ Llacuna 172, Parc del Clot, 08018 Barcelona.

For any question relating to your data you can write to info@cbgrupbarna.com.

What data we collect and why

This website only collects the data you give us, always for a specific reason.

The "Want information?" form on the homepage

We ask you for your name, mobile number, email address and message. This form does not save anything on any server of ours: sending it opens WhatsApp on the club's number with the details you typed, so the conversation stays on your phone and the club's. The processing therefore takes place within WhatsApp, and Meta's own terms also apply to it.

Purpose: to reply to you and give you the information you're asking for. Legal basis: your consent, given by ticking the box before you send it.

Photo gallery forms

In the event galleries (for example the Westfield Glòries 3x3), we may ask you for your first name, surname, email address and mobile number to send you the photos or notify you of new galleries. This data is sent to a club spreadsheet hosted on Google Drive.

Purpose: to send you the photographs and, if you've agreed to it, notify you of club news. Legal basis: your consent.

Document downloads (dossiers and briefing in PDF)

To download some of the club's documents — the Escoleta dossier, the partner briefing and similar — we ask you for your first name and surname, email address and, optionally, mobile number. This data is sent to the same club spreadsheet hosted on Google Drive that we use for the galleries, noting which document you requested.

On the same form you can tick whether you want to receive the club newsletter (news, open days, camp and match days). It's a separate checkbox: you can download the document without subscribing, and you can unsubscribe whenever you like by writing to info@cbgrupbarna.com or from any email we send you.

Purpose: to send you the document, be able to help you if you have questions and, if you've agreed to it, send you the newsletter. Legal basis: your consent, given by ticking the boxes before downloading.

Reviews

The Opina page is just a link to Google's review form. If you write there, your data is processed by Google under its own terms; the club does not collect any of it.

How long we keep it

Contact details are kept for as long as the relationship with the club lasts and, after that, for the time required to comply with legal obligations. If you ask us to delete them sooner, we do.

Who it's shared with

We do not sell or transfer data to third parties. We do use external services that, as data processors, may have technical access to it:

  • Meta (WhatsApp) — the channel through which enquiries from the homepage arrive.
  • Google — the club's spreadsheet and forms (Google Drive), and visit statistics (Google Analytics).
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages) — hosts this website.
  • Flickr — hosts the gallery photographs.

The club does not carry out profiling or automated decision-making with your data.

Cookies and statistics

This website does not use advertising cookies and does not sell data to anyone. The only thing in use is Google Analytics, to know how many visits each page gets and which ones are of most interest.

Analytics does not activate until you accept it. The first time you visit, a notice appears at the bottom of the page with two options: "Only the necessary ones" and "Accept". Until you choose — or if you choose the first option — your browser doesn't even download Google's code: there's no request, no cookie, and no identifier.

If you accept, Google Analytics installs cookies in your browser with an anonymous identifier to distinguish visits. Your choice is stored in your own browser; the club does not receive it.

You can change your choice whenever you like. You can also use the Google Analytics opt-out add-on or block third-party cookies in your browser.

Any other storage the website uses in your browser is technical and never leaves your device: it remembers, for example, whether you've already dismissed a notice or which match-day tab you were viewing.

Photographs and videos of activities

The club takes photographs and videos of training sessions, matches and events, and publishes some of them on the website and on social media to showcase its sporting activity. In the case of minors, publication happens under the authorisation signed by their guardians on the registration form.

If at any point you want us to remove a specific image, write to us at info@cbgrupbarna.com and we will remove it without needing any explanation.

Data on minors

Data on players who are minors is provided by their legal guardian at the time of registration, and is processed to manage their federation records, competition, and communication with the family. This website does not request data directly from minors.

Your rights

You can exercise the rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability at any time, and withdraw any consent you've given. An email to info@cbgrupbarna.com stating what you want is enough.

If you believe we haven't handled your request properly, you can file a complaint with the Catalan Data Protection Authority (APDCAT) or the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

Changes to this policy

If we change how we process data, we will update this page and the date at the top. The version in force is always the one you find here.
