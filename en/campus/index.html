What the Barna camp is

The CB Grup Barna basketball camp is the intensive training program the club runs during school holidays in the Clot neighborhood. It's not a leisure activity with a ball: it's skills training, with sessions dedicated to a specific aspect of the game and groups formed by age and level.

The club runs it with Time Chamber, who bring the individual-work methodology. That combination is what defines the camp: the repetition volume of a skills academy with the team atmosphere of a neighborhood club with sixty years of history.

How it's organized

Each week has its own focus, so anyone attending more than one week doesn't repeat content. These are the modules the club has run in recent editions:

01 Jun 23–27

Flow Camp

Reading the game and decision-making.

02 Jun 30–Jul 4

TC Basics

Fundamentals: footwork, balance, mechanics.

03 Jul 7–11

Shooting Academy

Shooting, in every situation.

04 Jul 14–18

One & One Mastery

The one-on-one game: fakes and offensive creativity.

05 Jul 21–25

Ballhandling Lab

Ball handling and dribbling under pressure.

06 Jul 28–Aug 1

Skills Lab Experience

Combined skills work: the edition's finale.

Who it's for

Children who play basketball or want to start, whether they're with CB Grup Barna or come from another club. Every week the club reserves spots for its own players and spots open to players from outside, and groups are closed by age and level so no one trains above or below where they should be.

If your child hasn't started yet and is 4 to 7 years old, the natural path isn't the camp but the Escoleta, the club's basketball school, which runs all year round with Julio Torralba.

Next edition

Dates for the next edition and the opening of registration are announced on this page and on @cbgrupbarna. Recent editions have filled up before they started, so the safe way in is to request advance notice: the club writes to those on the list when spots open, one day before publishing it.

Frequently asked questions

What ages is the basketball camp for?

CB Grup Barna's camp is for youth players, from Escoleta age through cadet and junior categories. Groups are formed by age and level, so a child who's just starting out doesn't train alongside someone who has been competing for years.

Do you need to play for CB Grup Barna to sign up?

No. The camp is open to players from any club in Barcelona and the wider province. Every week there are spots for Barna players and spots for players from outside.

How many spots are there per week?

The camp works with a limit of roughly 50 spots per week to keep the coach-to-player ratio right. Weeks fill up in order of registration, and recent editions have filled up before they started.

Where is the camp held?

In the Clot neighborhood, Sant Martí District of Barcelona. La Nau del Clot is CB Grup Barna's main sports venue.

When does registration open for the next edition?

Dates for the next edition are announced on this page and on Instagram (@cbgrupbarna). Anyone who wants advance notice before it opens to the public can ask via the club's WhatsApp (+34 698 425 153) and join the notification list.
