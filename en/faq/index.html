The club, in general

What is CB Grup Barna?

A grassroots basketball club from El Clot, Barcelona, founded in 1965, with more than 34 teams, both men's and women's structures, an Escoleta, senior teams in the Super Copa FCBQ and adapted sport.

How do I sign up for CB Grup Barna?

By leaving your details in the information form on the homepage or writing to the club's WhatsApp. For children aged 4 to 7, the usual entry point is the Escoleta.

Where does CB Grup Barna train and play?

The main venue is La Nau del Clot, on Carrer de la Llacuna, Sant Martí district. The club also uses five other courts in the neighbourhood, depending on the team and schedule.

How much does it cost to join the club?

Prices vary by category and programme (Escoleta, federated competition, Camp). The first trial session is free of commitment; for specific prices, contact the club directly.
