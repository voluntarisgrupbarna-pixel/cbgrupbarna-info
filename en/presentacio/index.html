A safe environment to grow up in

Safety & commitment
LOPIVI certified

Child protection in sport

The club has implemented a comprehensive protection system to guarantee a safe environment for every child and young person. Goal: to create a safe, educational and respectful sporting environment, where children can grow both athletically and personally.

Measures in place

  • Appointment of a child-protection officer
  • Response protocols for risk situations
  • Staff and coach training on protection
  • An ethical and conduct commitment for all staff

Communication channels

  • A confidential channel to report incidents
  • Direct contact with the protection officer
  • Open communication with families, players and coaches
