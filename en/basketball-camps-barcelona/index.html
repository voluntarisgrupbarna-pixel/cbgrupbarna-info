Basketball camps in Barcelona: every camp, compared

Which basketball camps run in Barcelona and its metropolitan area, how a skills camp differs from a day camp with a ball, what they cost, and how to choose one by age, season and budget. Written by CB Grup Barna, a youth basketball club in El Clot since 1965.

Skills camp or day camp: the first decision

This is the question that avoids disappointment. A skills camp devotes each session to one specific part of the game, with a high volume of repetitions and groups by age and level: players come back having improved something concrete. A sports day camp mixes basketball with swimming, games and outings: it covers the summer and it is fun, and that is perfectly fine if that is what you want. The problem is paying for one and expecting the other.

The Barna camp is skills work: each week has its own focus — Flow Camp, TC Basics, Shooting Academy, One & One Mastery, Ballhandling Lab, Skills Lab Experience — so anyone coming for more than one week never repeats content.

The four criteria that decide

1. Players per coach. The number that most determines how many repetitions each child gets. A camp that will not tell you is an answer in itself.

2. How groups are formed. By age and by level. A beginner should not train with someone who has been competing for six years, or the other way round.

3. Real hours and drop-off. Full day with lunch or half day, and the hour you can actually drop the child off.

4. What the price includes. Lunch, early drop-off, kit, insurance. Two identical prices can mean two very different things.

There is a fuller breakdown in our guide on what to check before signing anyone up.

By season

Summer. Most of the offer: from late June through August, in single or consecutive weeks. The Barna summer camp runs six weeks in El Clot.

Christmas and Easter. Short skills formats, useful for keeping sharp and working on one specific area mid-season. The club runs them at La Nau del Clot with Time Chamber.

By age

The usual range is 5-6 to 17. High-performance camps normally start at 9-10; club camps also take the youngest ages with adapted work. At the Barna there are groups from Escoleta age up to cadet and junior, and the camp is mixed: boys and girls, same demands.

What it costs

A full-day city camp with lunch usually costs between €150 and €250 per week. Residential camps, with accommodation and full board, run to several hundred euros per week. At CB Grup Barna the price is not published on the site: it depends on the modality —full week from 9am to 5pm with lunch included, or half day from 9am to 1:30pm— and on whether you play for the club. Message us with the age and we'll give it to you the same day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best basketball camp in Barcelona?

There is no single best camp. For brand and the big-club experience, Barça Escola; for high performance with accommodation, residential camps such as Gigantes or ITW Sport; for skills work inside the city with small groups and club prices, the CB Grup Barna camp in El Clot. What decides it is the ratio, the groups, the type of work and what the price includes.

Do you have to play for the club?

Not for the Barna camp: each week there are places for club players and places for players from any other club in Barcelona and the province.

Are there basketball camps for girls?

Yes. The Barna camp is mixed and the club has real parity between men's and women's basketball, with girls' teams in every age group.

When does registration open?

Dates for each edition are announced on the camp page and on Instagram (@cbgrupbarna). Anyone who wants the heads-up before it opens to the public can ask on the club's WhatsApp.

How much does a basketball camp in Barcelona cost?

A full-day city camp with lunch usually costs between 150 and 250 euros per week; residential camps, with accommodation and full board, run to several hundred euros per week. At CB Grup Barna the full week from 9am to 5pm with lunch included is 195 euros, and the half day from 9am to 1:30pm is 160 euros. Always check whether the price includes lunch, early drop-off and kit.

What is the difference between a skills camp and a day camp with a ball?

A skills camp devotes each session to one specific part of the game — shooting, ball handling, one-on-one, reading the game — with a high volume of repetitions and groups formed by age and level. A sports day camp mixes basketball with swimming, games and outings: it is designed for childcare over the holidays and for having fun, not for technical improvement. Both are valid, but you should not pay for one expecting the other.

Are there basketball camps in Barcelona at Christmas and Easter?

Yes. Beyond the summer camps, skills sessions run in every school holiday. CB Grup Barna runs skills work with Time Chamber at Christmas and Easter at La Nau del Clot, on top of the six-week summer camp.

What ages are basketball camps for?

The usual range is from 5-6 years old up to 17. High-performance skills camps normally start at 9-10; club camps also take the youngest ages with adapted work. At CB Grup Barna there are groups from Escoleta age up to cadet and junior, always split by age and level.

Do you have to play for the club to join its camp?

Not at open camps. The CB Grup Barna camp takes players from any club in Barcelona and the province: each week there are places for Barna players and places for players from outside.
