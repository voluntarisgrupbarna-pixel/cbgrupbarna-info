Ten started
on this very court

And none of them knew it at six years old.

A head coach in the Liga Femenina Endesa and a captain in the same league. An ACB player. A Tercera FEB champion. Four from Barça. They all got their first license here, and none of them remembers it as a formality: they remember a schoolyard court and a man who taught them how to dribble.

And it's not just these ten. There are many more: dozens of players trained on this court over sixty years. Here we've chosen a few to introduce people who are special to the club, but the real list wouldn't fit on one page.

01

It all started
in a schoolyard

There was no parquet. There was no stand. There was a hoop nailed to the wall, a concrete floor, and eleven kids in black shirts and red shorts.

The club and the Escoleta were born in 1965. On the left of the photo, wearing glasses and a sweater, is Julio Torralba, the club's founder and a benchmark coach for decades. Today, sixty years later, he's still on the court every week with the Escoleta's children ages 4 to 8.

The club wasn't born in a gym.
It was born in a schoolyard.

That boy is today Javier Torralba, head coach of Valencia Basket Femení. He previously ran Segle XXI, one of Spanish women's basketball's leading training centers, and has worked with Spain's national youth teams.

When he was introduced in Valencia he said he felt "deeply connected to the development and growth of young players." It's not an empty phrase. It's what he saw at home: his father, Julio, still does it every Tuesday.

Father and son.
The same craft, thirty years apart.

From the court in El Clot
to the Liga Femenina Endesa.

Ainhoa López started playing here. Today she's captain of Spar Girona and plays in the same league Javier Torralba coaches.

In the 2004-05 season's girls' pre-mini team she was seven years old. Eleven years later she debuted at the top level of Spanish women's basketball.

Her career, though, isn't defined only by the games. In February 2022, while playing for Barça CBS, she announced she had Hodgkin's lymphoma, and she beat it. In September 2024 she spent twenty-five days in hospital with severe hemolytic anemia and needed twelve transfusions. She returned to the court both times. Today she's a blood-donation ambassador and carries a phrase as her banner: "Living is urgent."

It's the name that best explains why an escoleta exists: because you never know who's standing in front of you.

Those
coming up now

Marta joined the Escoleta as a little girl. This year she turned eighteen and is leaving home. The Escoleta said goodbye to her the way you say goodbye to family.

And right behind her, another cohort is already coming up. There are former Escoleta players in FC Barcelona's youth academy and on Spain's national 3x3 circuit. Some are still minors, which is why we don't put a face or name to them here: their career is theirs, not our marketing.

07

Who trains
at La Nau

The same court where a four-year-old learns to dribble is where professional players train in the off-season.

At La Nau, Time Chamber, a club partner, works with Iandrey Panisa at the helm. International players such as Willy Hernangómez, a European champion with the Spanish national team and an NBA and Euroleague player, have passed through here.

It's not an Instagram anecdote. It's what makes an Escoleta kid walk through that door and see, with their own eyes, what it really means to dedicate yourself to this.

Frequently asked questions about the Escoleta

What is CB Grup Barna's basketball school?

The Escoleta is CB Grup Barna's basketball school for children ages 4 to 8, in the El Clot neighborhood, Sant Martí District of Barcelona. It runs mixed teams and three age groups.

Who runs CB Grup Barna's Escoleta?

The Escoleta is run by Julio Torralba, who founded the club in 1965. At 72 he still coaches every week. He can be reached by phone or WhatsApp at 646 205 526.

Which well-known players trained at CB Grup Barna's Escoleta?

This very court has produced Javier Torralba, now head coach of Valencia Basket Femení in the Liga Femenina Endesa; Ainhoa López, captain of Spar Girona in the same league; Roger Fornas, an ACB and LEB Oro center; David Mejía, a Tercera FEB champion; and several players later trained in FC Barcelona's youth categories. Full details, with photos and federation records, are on this same page.

From what age can you start playing basketball at the Barna?

From age 4. The Escoleta welcomes children ages 4 to 8. From 8, and as they're ready, they move up to the club's federated teams (premini, mini and the rest of the categories).

What are the Escoleta's training hours?

Two weekly training sessions: Wednesdays from 5:30pm to 6:40pm at Escola Casas, and Saturdays from 9am to 10:30am at La Nau del Clot.

Can you try a training session before signing up?

Yes. The first training session is a trial with no commitment. Write or call Julio Torralba at 646 205 526 to book a day.

Where does CB Grup Barna's Escoleta train?

In the El Clot neighborhood, Sant Martí District of Barcelona. La Nau del Clot is the club's main sports venue.
