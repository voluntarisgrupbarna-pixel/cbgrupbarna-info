Why it matters

Three concrete reasons

None of them is looking good.

01

So people find us

Anyone searching «youth basketball Barcelona» or «basketball Sant Martí» sees the clubs with the most reviews and the best rating first. Every review brings us closer to appearing before anyone else.

02

Because it opens doors

Neighbourhood businesses, institutions and possible sponsors look at the club's profile before saying yes. Reviews are our public CV.

03

Because it belongs to all of us

There are more than 450 families here. If one in two writes two lines, Barna becomes the best-rated neighbourhood club in Barcelona.

How to do it

Three steps, one minute

01

Tap the red button

The CB Grup Barna rating window opens on Google. You do not have to search for anything.

02

Pick the stars and write two lines

Say what you find here: the team, the coaches, the atmosphere, how families are treated.

03

Publish

That is it. It goes up straight away and you can edit or delete it whenever you like from your Google account.

Important: write what you really think, in your own words. We do not want identical or invented reviews — Google spots them, deletes them and they damage the club. One honest opinion is worth ten copied ones.
