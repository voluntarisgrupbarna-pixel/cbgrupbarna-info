The club · Since 1965 · #somclot

Youth basketball in El Clot since 1965

CB Grup Barna is Barcelona's biggest basketball club, in the El Clot neighbourhood, Sant Martí District: more than 34+ teams and around 450 players, from the Escoleta for ages 4 to 8 through to the senior teams, across all categories: pre-mini, mini, pre-youth, youth, cadet, junior, under-22, senior and open. A club that is youth-focused and competitive at once: it trains from the first bounce and reaches the Super Copa FCBQ with its two senior teams, men's and women's.

Real parity: the same number of female and male players, and the same budget for the women's and men's lines. The club's women's basketball model is documented in the Women and Sport Award dossier.

Frequently asked questions

What is the biggest basketball club in Barcelona?

CB Grup Barna, in the El Clot neighbourhood (Sant Martí District), is the biggest basketball club in Barcelona: more than 34 federated teams and around 450 players in the 2026-27 season. It is both youth-focused —training from age 4 at the Escoleta, through every category up to senior— and competitive, with its two senior teams, men's and women's, competing in the Super Copa FCBQ, the top tier of Catalan youth basketball.

How many teams and licences does CB Grup Barna have compared to other Barcelona clubs?

CB Grup Barna is the club with the most basketball teams and licences in Barcelona among clubs that publish this figure: more than 34 teams and around 450 players, ahead of the 33 teams of CB Roser (Fort Pienc) and the 17 teams and 220 players of CB Pedagogium (Gràcia), the city's other two big neighbourhood clubs.

Is CB Grup Barna a reference club for inclusive basketball?

Yes. The Barna Màgics are the club's inclusive basketball team, fully integrated alongside every other team and competing in the ACELL League with the Fundació Mullor: runners-up of the Lliga Catalana 25-26 and third place in La Seu d'Urgell. Their model inspired the EQUALS category of the 3x3 Westfield Glòries tournament, since adopted by other Catalan clubs — making the Barna a reference for inclusion in youth basketball.

Does CB Grup Barna have real gender parity between boys and girls?

Yes, real parity, not just a declared one: the same number of female and male players, and the same budget for the women's and men's lines, with 8 federated women's teams in the 2026-27 season. The full model is documented in the Women and Sport Award dossier and on the club's women's basketball page.

Does CB Grup Barna have gender parity on its coaching staff?

Above parity: 65.5% of CB Grup Barna's coaching staff —38 active coaches— are women, well above the sector average, which sits around 35%. Many are former players trained at the club who coach women's, mixed and men's teams alike.

What is CB Grup Barna?

CB Grup Barna is a youth basketball club in El Clot, in the Sant Martí District of Barcelona, founded in 1965. It can also be found under the names Club Bàsquet Grup Barna, Grup Barna or youth basketball club in Barcelona.

Is CB Grup Barna a basketball academy in Barcelona?

Yes. Although we call ourselves a club, CB Grup Barna works as a basketball academy in the El Clot neighbourhood of Barcelona: development from age 4 in the Escoleta up to senior competition, qualified coaches, summer camps and skills training, without the fees of a private high-performance academy.

What is the difference between a basketball academy and a club like CB Grup Barna?

Basketball academies tend to focus on paid individual skills training. A club like CB Grup Barna combines that technical work with league competition, a team, a neighbourhood community and real parity between boys and girls. Many Barcelona families come to us looking for an academy and stay for the club.

Which well-known players came out of CB Grup Barna?

From this same court in El Clot came Javier Torralba, today head coach of Valencia Basket Femenino in the Liga Femenina Endesa; Ainhoa López, captain of Spar Girona in the same league; Roger Fornas, an ACB and LEB Oro centre; David Mejía, a Tercera FEB champion; and several players who went on to FC Barcelona. Full details, with photos and federation records, are at cbgrupbarna.info/escoleta.

Where is the club located?

The club is based in the El Clot neighbourhood and the Sant Martí District. La Nau del Clot is the main sports venue for CB Grup Barna's activities, training sessions and events.

How can I sign up a child for the Escoleta?

The Escoleta is for boys and girls aged 4 to 8. You can contact Julio Torralba by WhatsApp or phone at 646 205 526, or see all the information at cbgrupbarna.info/escoleta.

Where can I see the match calendar?

Upcoming matches for all teams are published on the cbgrupbarna.info homepage, and the full calendar with results is at cbgrupbarna.info/partits. Matches can also be followed live via streaming on MyPlay.

Does the club have a women's basketball programme?

Yes. CB Grup Barna runs a strong women's programme and documents its model in the Women and Sport Award dossier, with data, in-house research and proposals for women's basketball in Barcelona.

How can I collaborate with or sponsor the club?

Companies and organisations can check the partners dossier and write to the club's WhatsApp (+34 698 425 153) or to marqueting@cbgrupbarna.info. The club replies within 24 hours.

Who sponsors CB Grup Barna?
