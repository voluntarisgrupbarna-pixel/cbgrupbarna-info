CB Grup Barna's sponsors: every partner for the 2026/27 season

23 businesses and organizations, most of them from El Clot and Sant Martí, make the club's day-to-day possible. Here's the full list, linked to each one's profile.

CB Grup Barna · 2026-08-17

How many sponsors does CB Grup Barna have

CB Grup Barna has 23 active partners and sponsors with their own profile for the 2026/27 season, plus Wilson as the official ball supplier and several institutional and press collaborators (Barcelona City Council, the Generalitat de Catalunya, and neighborhood media such as Eix Clot or Canal Disset). This isn't an isolated logo on a shirt: every partner gets content, a physical presence at La Nau and access to a community of more than 450 families.

The full list of Barna's partners

Here are all of the club's current sponsors and collaborators, grouped by sector. Each name links to its individual profile, with the business, its offer for Barna families (where there is one) and its Instagram.

Neighborhood shops and food

  • Eix Clot — an association of entrepreneurs in El Clot · Camp de l'Arpa.
  • Eix Comercial Sant Martí — an association of more than 200 shops across the district.
  • La Melosa — a smash-burger joint on Carrer del Clot.
  • Mercat dels Encants — the landmark market at Plaça de les Glòries.

Health and wellbeing

  • Nova Farmàcia Clot — a neighborhood pharmacy on Carrer del Clot.
  • Herbolaris Montserrat — an herbalist on Carrer de Guipúscoa since 1982.
  • Clínica Dental Bac de Roda — general dentistry and orthodontics.
  • Stepback Podologia — sports podiatry specialized in basketball.
  • Tot Salut — a physiotherapy, osteopathy and psychology center.
  • Aquamiga — reverse-osmosis water filtration rental.

Optics and imaging

  • Armand Òptics — an optician chain founded in 1988.
  • Foto Jané — a photography studio and photojournalism outfit.
  • Instax Fujifilm — instant cameras and film.

Leisure and tourism

  • Westfield Glòries — a shopping center and host venue for the club's 3x3 tournament.
  • Illa Fantasia — a water park in Vilassar de Dalt.
  • L'Aquàrium de Barcelona — a marine center at Port Vell.
  • Ovella Negra — a leisure venue in Poblenou.

Professional services

  • Romeo Abogados — real estate, civil and family law.
  • Manual Colors — large-format digital printing since 1976.

Sport, training and social impact

  • Time Chamber — a high-performance academy, co-organizer of the club's Camp.
  • GBK · Globasket — an international grassroots basketball tournament in Lloret de Mar.
  • Panteres Grogues — a leading LGBTIQ+ sports club, basketball section.
  • Fundació Mullor — training and job placement for young people.

In addition, Wilson is the club's official ball supplier, and among the collaborators without their own profile are institutions such as Barcelona City Council and the Generalitat de Catalunya, plus neighborhood media and organizations such as Canal Disset or Clotfede. The geolocated detail on all of them, inside and outside the neighborhood, is on the partners and collaborators map.

How a business becomes a Barna sponsor

The club offers three transparent starting points — Digital presence from €300 a year, Brand in motion from €500 per activation, and Sports sponsorship from €1,500 per team and season — plus tailored collaboration for brands with a specific goal and deliverables in mind. Many collaborations are in kind: sports gear, transport, physiotherapy, catering or printing, valued the same as a cash contribution.

Anyone interested can write via WhatsApp at +34 698 425 153 or to info@cbgrupbarna.com. Every level and the full detail are on the Sponsors and partners page.

Frequently asked questions

How many sponsors does CB Grup Barna have?

CB Grup Barna has 23 active partners and sponsors with their own profile for the 2026/27 season, plus Wilson as official equipment supplier and several institutional and press collaborators (Barcelona City Council, the Generalitat de Catalunya, neighborhood media).

Who are CB Grup Barna's sponsors?

CB Grup Barna's partners include Westfield Glòries, Time Chamber, Eix Clot, Herbolaris Montserrat, Clínica Dental Bac de Roda, Aquamiga, Armand Òptics, Manual Colors, La Melosa, Foto Jané, Mercat dels Encants, Tot Salut, Ovella Negra, Romeo Abogados, Fundació Mullor, L'Aquàrium de Barcelona, Eix Comercial Sant Martí, GBK · Globasket, Illa Fantasia, Panteres Grogues, Stepback Podologia, Instax Fujifilm and Nova Farmàcia Clot. The full, up-to-date list is at cbgrupbarna.info/patrocinadors/.

How does a business become a CB Grup Barna sponsor?

By writing via WhatsApp to +34 698 425 153 or by email to info@cbgrupbarna.com. The club prepares a concrete proposal based on the brand's goal, with levels starting at €300 a year and in-kind collaboration for those contributing a product or service.

Where can I see each Barna sponsor's profile?
