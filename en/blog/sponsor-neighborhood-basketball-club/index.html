Why sponsor a neighborhood basketball club: Barna's numbers

A neighborhood club isn't a small brand: it's the entry point to a real community of families. These are the numbers behind CB Grup Barna's sponsorship dossier.

CB Grup Barna · 2026-08-16

Barcelona's leading neighborhood club for digital reach

CB Grup Barna closed the 2025-26 season with 5,382 Instagram followers, growth of +92% over three seasons. It isn't a one-off spike: the club published 1,109 posts this season and reached 1.97 million accounts, with 8.91 million cumulative views.

A brand joining that community isn't buying a logo on a shirt: it's stepping into the content those people watch every week.

Who's behind these numbers

Most of the club's followers are between 35 and 54 years old and live in Barcelona: the families who make the purchasing decisions, not teenage players. For a shop in El Clot or Sant Martí, that means reaching its real customer, not a generic audience.

Four ways to collaborate

  • Digital presence — logo in weekly stories, mentions in posts, presence in the family mailing list.
  • Digital and gym — banner at La Nau del Clot, logo on the kit, two dedicated posts a month.
  • Maximum visibility — naming rights for an event, a dedicated reel every month, presence across all of the club's materials.
  • Tailored — a proposal built for your business, joint activations in the neighborhood, in-kind collaboration.

A sixty-year-old club, not a campaign

What sets Barna apart from a one-off sponsorship campaign is that the club is there all year round, has been for sixty years, in the same neighborhood. Matches, the camp, 3x3 and the season's activities provide constant touchpoints, not a single day of exposure.
