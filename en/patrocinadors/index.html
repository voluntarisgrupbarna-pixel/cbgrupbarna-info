Competitive positioning

The structure of a big club, with the soul of a neighbourhood

The Federació Catalana de Bàsquet publishes the official record of every club: how many teams it has, which categories it competes in, and whether its senior first team belongs to the same entity as its youth setup. Based on that public data, here's how the Barna compares against Catalunya's leading clubs.

  • FC Barcelona — 10 federated teams in Catalunya, men's only, from Mini to Júnior, with no Pre-Mini. And no women's team at all: its women's basketball project (Barça CBS, with CB Santfeliuenc) closed at the end of 2024-25 for financial reasons, after having played in Lliga F Endesa. Its men's senior reserve team plays in Copa Catalunya, not Súper Copa; the first team plays in Lliga ACB.
  • Joventut Badalona (la Penya) — the biggest youth setup in Catalunya: 53 teams, from Pre-Mini to Júnior, in both genders. No senior team of its own within this entity: those who reach the top leave the club.
  • Bàsquet Manresa — two separate clubs depending on gender: 17 men's teams with no senior side, and a separate women's club (Manresa CBF) with a senior team in Lliga Femenina 2, the national league.
  • Fundació Bàsquet Girona — 23 teams, from Pre-Mini to Júnior, in both genders, chaired by Marc Gasol. It also has no senior team of its own: its first team plays in Lliga Endesa, outside this record.
  • CB Grup Barna — more than 34 teams, from Pre-Mini to Senior, and the only club on this list with two senior teams — first and reserve — in both genders at once, both competing in the Súper Copa FCBQ, the top territorial category.

What sets us apart

At the big clubs, youth development and the first team are almost never the same entity. At the Barna, they are: the same player who joins the Escoleta at age 4 can end up playing the Súper Copa without ever changing shirts.

Source: club records from the Federació Catalana de Bàsquet (basquetcatala.cat), August 2026. Full analysis on the club blog.

Frequently asked questions

How do I become a sponsor or partner of CB Grup Barna?

Write to us on WhatsApp (+34 698 425 153) or send an email to info@cbgrupbarna.com. We'll prepare a concrete proposal based on what your brand wants to achieve, with no filler packages or impossible-to-measure promises.

What ways of collaborating are there?

Three transparent starting points — Digital presence, Brand in motion and Sports sponsorship — plus tailor-made collaboration for brands that want a clear objective and very specific deliverables.

Can I collaborate with product or service instead of money?

Yes. Many of the club's collaborations are in kind: sports equipment, transport, physiotherapy, event catering or printing. They are valued the same as a financial contribution and adapted to an equivalent level of consideration.

What does my company get in return?

Content with context (not an isolated logo), physical presence at La Nau, kits, tournaments and camps, access to a community of more than 450 families, and a tailor-made proposal with concrete deliverables for each brand.

Is production of banners or specific materials included?

No, unless expressly agreed: it is budgeted separately according to what the activation needs.

Tell us what you want to achieve. We'll prepare a concrete proposal, with no filler packages or impossible-to-measure promises.
