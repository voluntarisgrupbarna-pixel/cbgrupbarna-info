What 3x3 basketball is

3x3 is half-court basketball: three players per team and a single hoop. Games are short, the score moves fast and in a single day a team can play several rounds back to back. It is the format that entered the Olympics in Tokyo and the one that works best on the street, because all it needs is half a court and an afternoon.

The Barna 3x3, at Glòries

CB Grup Barna brings the tournament to Westfield Glòries, on Plaça de les Glòries Catalanes, with Time Chamber and the local businesses of El Clot and Glòries. The idea is simple: take basketball out of the sports hall and put it where the people are, somewhere thousands walk past on a Saturday morning.

The tournament has youth categories, designed for club teams, and open categories any group can enter without a federation licence. That mix is what gives it its atmosphere: club teams and groups of friends from the neighbourhood on the same court.

How to enter a team

Registration is handled directly on the tournament's official site, cbgrupbarna-3x3timechamber.com, by team rather than by individual player. Places are limited and recent editions have filled before they began, so the sooner you register the safer. If registration for the next edition is not open yet, you can ask for advance notice via the club's WhatsApp or by leaving your details on the information form.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Westfield Glòries 3x3?

It is the 3x3 street basketball tournament CB Grup Barna runs at the Westfield Glòries shopping centre in Barcelona, with Time Chamber and the local businesses of El Clot and Glòries. It is played outdoors, three against three at a single hoop.

Who can play?

There are youth categories and open categories, so both club teams and groups of friends can play. You do not need a federation licence to enter the open categories.

What is the 3x3 format?

Three players per team at a single hoop, on half a court. Games are short and back to back, so a team plays several rounds in one day. It has been basketball's Olympic format since the Tokyo Games.

When is the next edition?

Dates are announced on this page, on the official site cbgrupbarna-3x3timechamber.com and on Instagram (@cbgrupbarna). The tournament usually takes place at the start of summer. Anyone wanting notice of when registration opens can ask via the club's WhatsApp.

Where exactly is it?

At Westfield Glòries, on Plaça de les Glòries Catalanes in Barcelona, next to the El Clot neighbourhood and the Sant Martí district.
