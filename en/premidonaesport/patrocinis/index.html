CB Grup Barna

61 years in El Clot. A neighbourhood. A model.

CB Grup Barna was founded in 1965 in the El Clot neighbourhood, in the Sant Martí District of Barcelona. Joan Manuel Serrat, a local resident, was one of the entity's co-founders.

Today: 450+ members, 40 teams (20 women's + 20 men's, 1:1 parity), 38 active coaches (65.5% of technical staff), among the top 20 clubs in Catalonia.

The Barna Method

A system. Not a campaign.

01
Parity Investment — The 50%

CB Grup Barna allocates 50% of its budget to women's basketball. The sector average is 35%. Investment creates a level. The level creates role models. Role models create demand.

02
Leadership Pipeline — The 38 Coaches

38 active coaches — 65.5% of the technical staff. No federation certification. As far as the FCBQ is concerned, they do not exist. Yet they coach 40 teams every week in El Clot.

03
Organic Response — The 53.7%

53.7% of the social media audience is female. With no planned campaign. Women detect that the club practises equality instead of talking about it.

The 5 Theories of the Barna Method

T1
Theory of the Invisible Market
Threshold: <40% resources and >30% participants. The first to correct it captures the talent surplus. Akerlof (1970) · WNBA +183%.

Theory of the Recognition Ladder
35.7% players → 9% advanced coaching qualification. The ladder loses between 32% and 75% of female talent at every rung. Eagly & Carli (2007).

Theory of Cultural Irreversibility
A programme can be cancelled. A culture, not. 61 years of continued practice have created a culture at CB Grup Barna where parity is the norm. Schein (1992).

Conclusions of the candidacy

Why this candidacy.

01

A basketball club, not an exception

This candidacy is not presented from a place of singularity or exceptionality. It is presented from a sober, measurable claim: 250 federated players play at CB Grup Barna, more than at any other basketball club in Barcelona city. The comparison is strict: only against other basketball clubs, because basketball has its own structural dynamics that cannot be equated with other sports.

02

What this means for Barcelona

If the biggest club has 250 players and the city average is 54, it means that Barcelona's female talent is fragmented across small clubs, without building the critical mass needed for a competitive structure at the highest level. This is one of the factors explaining why Barcelona has no women's team in LF2. Fragmentation is the problem. Concentrating talent in projects like CB Grup Barna is part of the solution.

03

What already works here

What CB Grup Barna demonstrates every week at Parc del Clot is that parity is not an aspiration: it is an established practice. 20 women's teams. 20 men's teams. 38 active coaches. 65.5% female technical staff. 50% of the budget for women's basketball. And, above all, real formative continuity, from age 5 through to seniors, where no girl from the neighbourhood is left without a place to play.

04

Why recognition matters

The 19th Women and Sport Award is, for us, a lever: to make a working model visible, so other Barcelona clubs can replicate it, so institutions reinforce it, and so the girls of the neighbourhood know their club has been recognised as a civic role model. We are not asking for a lifetime-achievement award. We are asking for our track record to stop being an exception.

“Equality in sport is not legislated. It is built, from the ground up, from a neighbourhood. And sometimes, when it works, all it takes is for someone to show it.”

From crisis to opportunity

Barcelona,
capital of women's basketball.

Recognising a crisis is the first step to solving it. Women's basketball in Barcelona is going through a diagnosed structural crisis. But Barcelona also has all the assets needed to become the benchmark for women's basketball in Catalonia and southern Europe: 134 women's youth teams, a network of clubs with history, proximity to FC Barcelona, Joventut and Granollers, and a new generation that no longer accepts the status quo.

Below are 6 concrete proposals with a 2030 horizon. Some CB Grup Barna can lead alone. Others require an alliance with clubs, institutions and sponsors. All are necessary.

01
Sporting ambition · 2030

LF2 Barcelona 2030 Plan

The first Barcelona player with her own team playing Liga Femenina 2 in Barcelona city. A strategic alliance among Barcelona clubs with competitive women's sections. Roadmap: 2026-27 selection of allied clubs · 2028 joint competitive structure · 2030 first BCN team in LF2.

Leads: clubs with senior sections · Supports: IBE, FCBQ
02
Training · 2027

El Clot Women's
Technical Development Centre

Stop the drain of talent to Cornellà and Mataró. A centre in Parc del Clot where the best players from all of Barcelona city's clubs can train 4 days a week without having to travel. Initial capacity: 60 players. Sustained through an agreement with the IBE and partner clubs.

Leads: CB Grup Barna · Supports: BCN clubs, IBE
03
Competition · 2027

BCN Women's League

A competition so that talent can meet. Summer league and winter tournaments with all of Barcelona city's women's clubs. Broadcasts, individual awards, media visibility and a media partner. A format inspired by the already successful 3x3 Westfield Glòries, but at city scale.

Leads: alliance of CB Grup Barna + Time Chamber + BCN clubs
04
Recruitment · 2026

"Barcelona Talent" Programme

Identify talent before it leaves. An early identification programme in the city's schools and clubs with scholarships that allow players to combine studies and a sporting career without having to leave Barcelona. Goal: raise conversion from 0.75% to 3% within 5 years.

Leads: IBE + BCN clubs · Ally: Catalan universities
05
Governance · 2026

Civic Pact for
Women's Basketball

An agreement signed by Barcelona City Council, the IBE, the FCBQ, the 17 clubs with women's sections and major sponsors. Dedicated budget. Venues in prime-time slots for women's basketball (not at 21:30). Institutional visibility. Measurable commitments, audited annually.

Leads: Barcelona City Council · Involves: all stakeholders
06
Leadership · 2030

100 Coaches 2030

Multiply the technical pipeline. A training, mentoring and certification programme to reach 100+ active coaches in Barcelona city by 2030. CB Grup Barna as the leading training club, in partnership with the FCBQ and its Coach Mentoring programme. Eradicate the 9% figure for women in advanced coaching qualifications.

Leads: CB Grup Barna · Ally: FCBQ Mentoring

We are not asking Barcelona to stop being what it is. We are asking Barcelona to also be what it can be: a city where any girl can play basketball, train, reach the elite and return — without ever having to leave.
