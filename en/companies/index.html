Investing in Barna

CB Grup Barna does not sell advertising space: it sells access to a community. 450+ families in El Clot and Sant Martí, 400 players, 22 partner businesses, two senior teams in the Supercopa, a women's project that is half the club, and the club's own events in the centre of Barcelona. This page explains what a brand gets and how to come in.

What is on the other side

A sponsorship is worth what the audience behind it is worth. These are the club's numbers, and every one of them can be backed up: there is no inflated estimate here.

On top of that there is the club's own channel. @cbgrupbarna averages 439,000 views a month on Instagram, with content produced by the club itself: match days, the Escoleta, women's basketball, the staff's work. A partner brand does not show up there as a hoarding in the background of a photo: it shows up inside a story the neighbourhood watches all the way through.

And then there is the territory, which is what no digital campaign buys well: El Clot and Sant Martí. La Nau del Clot, six courts in the district, the 3x3 at Westfield Glòries registered with FIBA, the summer camp and the open days. A neighbourhood business is not after reach: it is after being part of the life of the neighbourhood. That is exactly what the club sells.

Who is behind those views

This is not anonymous traffic: Instagram's professional dashboard (Meta Business Suite) says who follows the account and from where. The audience is mostly adult — 58.8% men, 41.2% women, with the strongest age groups at 45-54 (32%) and 25-34 (22%) — in other words parents, not just young players. Geographically, 88.8% is from Spain and 43.2% from the city of Barcelona, with the rest spread across Badalona, l'Hospitalet, Mataró and other towns in the metropolitan area: exactly the local audience a neighbourhood brand wants to reach.

The account has also picked up speed: the 2025-26 season has reached 1.97 million accounts and 6.44 million views, 32-33% more than the previous season, with shares up 3.9 times. In three seasons the follower count has gone from 2,800 to more than 5,000 — 92% growth — and the account has 8.9 million accumulated views since it opened.

Source: Instagram's professional dashboard (Meta Business Suite), historical data from September 2023 to July 2026 and a season-by-season comparison. Reviewed quarterly; see the digital community benchmark for the most recent follower count.

Barna Business

This is the idea that changes the conversation. A neighbourhood club usually offers visibility, and a neighbourhood club's visibility will always be cheaper than a big club's. Competing on price is losing.

What Barna does have, and few others do, is a concentration of commercial fabric in a small territory: 22 businesses from El Clot and Sant Martí sharing a crest. Barna Business puts them in touch with each other — around the club's events and at meetings built for that — so being a partner stops being a communications expense and becomes a way into the district's business fabric.

For a neighbourhood business that is worth more than a logo on a shirt. And for the club it means sponsorship gets renewed because it works, not out of obligation.

How to come in

There is no single format, and you do not have to put in money. A good share of the current agreements are in kind: product, service, space or experiences for the club's families. For many neighbourhood businesses it is the natural way to start.

  • In-kind partnership — product, service or a discount for members.
  • Team partner — linked to one team and its season.
  • Event partner — the 3x3, the camp, the open days or the Premi Dona i Esport.
  • Club partner — a presence across the season, the venues and the channel.

The starting point is always the same: a meeting to understand what the business needs, and then a tailored proposal with concrete deliverables. The commercial pack can be downloaded beforehand.

Frequently asked questions

What do you get from sponsoring CB Grup Barna?

Visibility in front of 450+ families in El Clot and Sant Martí and 400 players, a physical presence at La Nau del Clot and at the club's events, content produced by the club itself, entry into Barna Business — the network of partner businesses — and a link to a women's and inclusive basketball project. It is not just a logo on a hoarding.

How many companies sponsor the club?

23 partners with a public page, from neighbourhood shops to Westfield Glòries, Instax Fujifilm, Nova Farmàcia Clot and the Mercat dels Encants. They can be looked up one by one .

Can you partner with the club without paying money?

Yes. A good share of the club's agreements are in kind: product, service, space, experiences for families or discounts for members. For many neighbourhood businesses it is the most natural way in.

What is Barna Business?

The network of businesses linked to the club. Beyond visibility, it puts partners in touch with each other and with the commercial fabric of El Clot and Sant Martí.
