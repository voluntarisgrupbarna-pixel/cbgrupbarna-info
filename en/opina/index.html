Why it matters

Three concrete reasons

None of them is about looking good.

01

So people can find us

Anyone searching for "baloncesto base Barcelona" or "bàsquet Sant Martí" sees the clubs with more reviews and a better rating first. Every review gets us closer to appearing before anyone else.

02

So doors open for us

Local businesses, institutions and potential sponsors check the club's profile before saying yes. Reviews are our public track record.

03

Because it belongs to everyone

We're more than 450 families. If one in two writes two lines, the Barna becomes the best-rated neighbourhood club in Barcelona.

How it's done

Three steps, one minute

01

Tap the red button

The CB Grup Barna review window opens on Google. You don't have to search for anything.

02

Choose your stars and write two lines

Tell us what you find: the team, the coaches, the atmosphere, how families are treated.

03

Publish

That's it. It's published instantly, and you can edit or delete it whenever you like from your Google account.

Important: write what you honestly think, in your own words. We don't want copied or made-up reviews — Google detects them, removes them, and they harm the club. One sincere review is worth ten copied ones.
