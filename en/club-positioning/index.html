Where Barna stands among the big clubs of Catalan basketball

Barça, Joventut, Manresa, Girona… when people talk about the big clubs of Catalan basketball, a neighbourhood club is rarely on the list. Each club's official record at basquetcatala.cat says something else. This is the positioning of the whole club — women's and men's — not just of the women's project.

Barna's pyramid, from the inside

The club's official record at basquetcatala.cat shows more than 34 teams, from Pre-Mini (8-9 years old) up to senior, with parallel girls' and boys' structures and, in most age groups, up to three levels (A, B and often a third development side). Both senior sections — women's and men's — have a first team and a reserve, and both first teams play in the Super Copa FCBQ, the top regional division in Catalonia.

The four benchmark clubs

Among the Catalan clubs with a first team in the ACB or the Liga Femenina Endesa, the usual pattern is that the youth section and the senior first team are not the same organisation.

  • FC Barcelona — 10 league teams in Catalonia, all boys', from Mini to Júnior, with no Pre-Mini. Right now it has no girls' youth team of its own: the Barça CBS project, with CB Santfeliuenc, played in the Liga F Endesa until it was closed for financial reasons at the end of the 2024-25 season. Their men's reserve team plays in the Copa Catalunya, not the Super Copa; the first team plays in the ACB.
  • Joventut Badalona (la Penya) — the largest youth section in Catalonia: 53 teams, from Pre-Mini to Júnior, for both girls and boys. No senior team of its own in this organisation, women's or men's: anyone who makes it to the top leaves the club.
  • Bàsquet Manresa — in fact two different clubs depending on gender: the boys' youth section (17 teams, no senior side) and Manresa CBF for women, a separate organisation with its own board, budget and brand, with a senior team in Liga Femenina 2, the national league.
  • Fundació Bàsquet Girona — 23 teams, from Pre-Mini to Júnior, for both girls and boys, chaired by Marc Gasol. It has no senior team of its own either, in either gender: the first team plays in the Liga Endesa, outside this record.

And what if your child wants to start at Barça?

FC Barcelona's basketball school takes children from 4 to 12, but the club's federation record — 10 teams, all boys', from Mini to Júnior — has no Pre-Mini team at all, the 7-8 age group. A child who starts there therefore has no league team to move up to inside the same organisation when they reach that age. At CB Grup Barna there is a team at every age group from 4 onwards — Escoleta, Premini, Mini and all the rest — for both girls and boys, with no gap and no need to change club along the way.

For a girl, the same question runs deeper still: today FC Barcelona has no girls' youth team of its own (the Barça CBS project closed in 2025), while Grup Barna has 8 girls' league teams, with the same budget and the same number of teams as the boys' side.

What is unusual about it

Almost every club analysed has a women's team or a large youth section. At Barna, on top of that, developing players and reaching the competitive ceiling always goes through the same board, the same budget and the same club, for women and men at once — a model that is not the most common among the benchmark clubs.

At the big clubs, the youth section and the first team are almost never the same organisation. At Barna they are: the same player who joins the Escoleta at four can end up playing in the Super Copa without ever changing shirt.

What makes us different

Of the clubs analysed, Grup Barna is the only one with two senior teams — a first team and a reserve — for both women and men at once, inside the same organisation that runs the whole development pyramid, with both playing in the Super Copa FCBQ. We are not the club with the most teams in Catalonia — la Penya has 53 — nor the best known. We are, though, one of the few where developing players and competing at the top level happen at the same club, without ever breaking the thread, for both women and men.

The sentence both tables support

CB Grup Barna brings together a digital community of more than 7,000 people with 32 league teams — the third largest development structure in its comparable universe and the first in the city of Barcelona by community — all from a single brand and with no professional team pulling an audience along behind it.

Some of the benchmark clubs in this field — Penya, Barça, Girona, Manresa — are in fact two or three separate federated organisations with separate accounts (professional company + foundation + youth section): they are not added together as if they were one, so as not to inflate the ranking. Barna gets there with one organisation, one brand and one account.

Frequently asked questions

Is CB Grup Barna the biggest basketball club in the city of Barcelona?

By number of teams within the city itself, yes. CB Grup Barna has more than 34 teams on its FCBQ record, ahead of the 10 teams — all boys', with no senior women's side — in FC Barcelona's youth section, the other big club based in the city. La Penya, with 53 teams, has the largest youth section in Catalonia, but is based in Badalona, not Barcelona.

Is Barna a competitive club or a development club?

Both at once: it uses the full FCBQ ladder (first year, Preferent/Interterritorial, Promoció) and, on top of that, reaches the top rung with both senior teams in the Super Copa FCBQ.

Does FC Barcelona have a women's basketball team?

Not since the end of the 2024-25 season. Their project, Barça CBS (with CB Santfeliuenc), reached the Liga F Endesa but closed for financial reasons.

If my child starts at FC Barcelona's basketball school, will there be a team for them afterwards?

It depends on their age. FC Barcelona's federation record has 10 teams, all boys', from Mini to Júnior, with no Pre-Mini side (the 7-8 age group), even though their school takes children from 4 to 12. A child who starts there has no league team to move up to inside the same organisation when they reach that age. At CB Grup Barna there is a team at every age group from 4 onwards, for both girls and boys, with no gap.

Does la Penya (Joventut Badalona) have its own senior team?

Not inside the same organisation as its youth section. La Penya has 53 development teams, the largest youth section in Catalonia, but anyone who reaches senior level leaves the club.

What is different about CB Grup Barna?

It is the only one of the clubs compared with two senior teams — a first team and a reserve — for both women and men at the same time, inside the same organisation that runs the whole development pyramid, with both playing in the Super Copa FCBQ.

Are you the best basketball club in Barcelona?

It depends what you are looking for, and figures say it better than adjectives: the Barna is the leading development club in the city of Barcelona by structure, 3rd of 22 Catalan development clubs by number of registered teams and 6th by followers. If what you want is a club that takes a child from age 4 to the senior team without changing shirts, this one has been doing that for sixty-one years.
