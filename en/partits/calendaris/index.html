Frequently asked questions

How do I download my team's calendar?

Find your team in the list, tap the image or the download button and it saves to your phone or computer: an image for teams with a single fixture list, a multi-page PDF for the senior teams, who play a home-and-away league.

Why isn't there a calendar for my team?

Development teams (pre-mini, mini, pre-youth) receive their calendar from the FCBQ later than the rest of the categories. It will be published here as soon as the federation makes it official.

What do I do if a match has changed time or venue?

If the Catalan Basketball Federation changes a match after the downloadable fixture list was generated, the team's card shows a notice so you can check the updated details in the club's match app, which is reviewed every day.

How often are the calendars updated?

The live calendar at /partits/ syncs every day with the official FCBQ calendar. The downloadable fixture lists are fixed: if a specific match changes, the app always has the correct data.

Want the live calendar?

The match app syncs every day with the FCBQ: results, streaks and standings.
