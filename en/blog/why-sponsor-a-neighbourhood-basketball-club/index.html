Why sponsor a neighbourhood basketball club: the Barna figures

A neighbourhood club is not a small brand: it is the way into a real community of families. These are the figures behind the CB Grup Barna sponsorship pack.

CB Grup Barna · 2026-08-16

Barcelona's neighbourhood club with the widest digital reach

CB Grup Barna closed the 2025-26 season with 5,382 Instagram followers, a growth of +92% over three seasons. This is not a one-off spike: the club published 1,109 posts this season and reached 1.97 million accounts, with 8.91 million cumulative views.

A brand joining does not buy a logo on a shirt: it enters the content these people watch every week.

Who is behind these figures

Most of the club's followers are aged 35 to 54 and live in Barcelona: they are the families who make the purchase, not the teenage players. For a shop in the Clot or Sant Martí, that means reaching its real customer, not a generic audience.

Four ways to work together

  • Digital presence — logo in the weekly stories, mentions in posts, presence in the family mailing.
  • Digital and the hall — banner at La Nau del Clot, logo on the kit, two dedicated posts a month.
  • Maximum visibility — naming an event, a dedicated reel every month, presence across all the club's material.
  • Tailor-made — a proposal built for your company, joint activations in the neighbourhood, in-kind collaborations.

A club with sixty-one years behind it, not a campaign

What makes the Barna different from a one-off sponsorship campaign is that the club is there all year round, and has been for sixty-one years, in the same neighbourhood. The games, the camp, the 3×3 and the season's activities give constant points of contact, not a single day of exposure.
