About Time Chamber

Time Chamber is a basketball training and high-performance academy with its main base at the Velòdrom d'Horta in Barcelona and a presence in other Catalan municipalities. It offers individualised training combining technical, physical and mental work, and has a collaboration agreement with CB Grup Barna, with whom it has organised camps and showcases.
