What we post, and how often

Posting calendar
  • Match days — 3 to 4 times a week: match reports, photos and reels
  • Training — twice a week: the work you don't see, plus technique
  • Club content — 5 times a week: news, signings and renewals
  • Sponsor mentions — weekly, in stories, posts and reels
  • Events — camps, tournaments, finals and activations, as they come up

Collaboration network

In the 25·26 season we mentioned or collaborated with 197 different accounts: neighbourhood businesses, local photography and press, the Catalan Basketball Federation and other clubs.

What reaches the most people

Events, standout players and neighbourhood values. The season's most-viewed piece reached 35.6K views, and three of the top five were from women's teams.

· In the venue and on the street

Where your logo ends up

Banners and signage at La Nau del Clot, a logo on the kit of every federated team and a presence at trophy ceremonies and finals.

Beyond that, the neighbourhood: agreements with Eix del Clot, the Sant Martí District and the Federació d'Entitats Clot-Camp de l'Arpa.
