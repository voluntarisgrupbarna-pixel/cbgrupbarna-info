The concept

Turning a shopping centre
into a living basketball space.

The 3x3 Westfield Glòries was born with the aim of using sport as a tool for social cohesion, activating spaces and generating real impact. Three editions later, it is a landmark event for street basketball in Barcelona.

The project combines FIBA 3x3 sporting competition, the activation of one of the city's busiest shopping centres, and a network of influencers and content creators who have generated more than 2.4 million potential impressions.

Cistella Petita · Inclusion from age 5

3x3 for the youngest players.

Annual event

Every year, CB Grup Barna holds "Cistella Petita": a 3x3 event adapted for the club's youngest players, where boys and girls from age 5 play their own tournament with age-appropriate rules. A way of making street basketball inclusive from day one.

2026 Edition

New categories.
The same commitment to inclusion.

+
Veterans Category

Successfully introduced in 2024. Strengthens the tournament's intergenerational character and widens the range of participants.

★
EQUALS Category · New for 2026

An inclusive format where players with functional diversity (Specials) share the court with players without disabilities. Real equality, coexistence, sport as inclusion.

♀
Expanded women's category

2026 goal: increase the number of women's teams, boost recruitment of clubs with women's sections, and increase communication visibility before and during the event.
