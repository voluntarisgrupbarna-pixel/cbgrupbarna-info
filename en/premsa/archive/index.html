CB Grup Barna's press archive

sixty-one years of a club leave a trail. This archive gathers references to Barna in external media — from match reports to club features — ordered from the most recent to the oldest.

A note on transparency: every entry has a direct, verified link to its source. The archive does not claim to be 100% of everything published about the club since 1965 — there are unindexed press archives, deleted pages and outlets blocked to search engines — but every reference here is real and can be checked.

Permanent listings and directories

These are not journalism, but they are sources Google and AI assistants consult often: Enciclopèdia Catalana · Esportpèdia, the City Council's Guia de Barcelona listing, the Eix Clot directory, Eurobasket, and the official records FCBQ and FEB.

Where the change comes from

Until a few years ago, most appearances were «Grup Barna as the opponent»: results, previews and match reports from other clubs. Since 2026, Barna has started to appear as the story itself: the club project, the two senior teams, the events it organises. That is the direction worth pushing.
