  • FIBA 3x3 world ranking points in the senior categories, tracked on FIBA Play.

A 3x3 tournament at a Barcelona shopping centre

The tournament began in 2024 with a court built inside the Westfield Glòries shopping centre, on Plaça de les Glòries Catalanes (Av. Diagonal 208), with a simple idea: take basketball out of the sports hall and put it where the people are. That venue is still the main one — it hosts the FIBA court and the finals — but the tournament has outgrown it: since the 3rd edition it is also played at La Nau del Clot, the club's own hall, and at the open-air Rambleta del Clot.

Barcelona has more than one 3x3 event tied to a shopping centre. The El Clot-Glòries one is the one that combines all four things at once: every age group — not just youth categories —, equal prize money, FIBA 3x3 world ranking points and an inclusive category. And it is run by a neighbourhood club with 60 years of history in El Clot, not as a one-off marketing activation: it is put together by the same people who coach at La Nau del Clot all year round.

What 3x3 basketball is

3x3 is half-court basketball: teams of 3 to 5 players and a single basket, with 3 on court and up to 2 substitutes. Games last 10 minutes or until a team reaches 21 points, the scoreboard moves fast and a team can play several knockout rounds in a single day. It became an Olympic format at the Tokyo 2021 Games and works better than anything else on the street, because all it needs is half a court and an afternoon.

Equality, Pride and social commitment

The tournament is played in June, during LGBTI+ Pride Month, and that is not decoration: the official shirt of the 3rd edition carried its colours, the event was designed around those values and Kendall Martin, a trans player and former Barça B player, took part.

Equality is structural, not symbolic: the same categories exist for girls and boys, and the prize money is identical for the women's and men's senior champions. The club was shortlisted for the 19th Women and Sport Award run by Institut Barcelona Esports largely thanks to this tournament.

There is also a social strand: CB Grup Barna has a partnership with FEDER, the Spanish Rare Diseases Federation, to raise awareness of rare diseases through sport. The 3x3, the club event that draws the largest outside crowd, is one of its loudspeakers.

How the 3rd edition went (June 2026)

The 3rd edition of the 3×3 Westfield Glòries was played on 6-7 June 2026 and was a step up from the first two: 113 teams, a tournament record, well above the 80 teams of 2024 and the 100 of 2025. It grew to 10 categories and 3 venues less than a 15-minute walk apart, so it no longer happens inside a shopping centre: it happens across the whole El Clot-Glòries neighbourhood.

The 3rd edition also added official FIBA status with world ranking points and FIBA Play coverage, plus the inclusive Barna Màgics category. Around 508 players competed, backed by 7 content creators with a combined reach of more than 1.9 million Instagram followers — among them Ari Geli and her professional team Panthers — entirely organically, with no payment involved. David Escudé, Barcelona City Council's sports commissioner, also visited the tournament.

Frequently asked questions

What is the 3×3 Westfield Glòries?

It is the street basketball tournament with FIBA ranking points organised by CB Grup Barna, Time Chamber and Eix Clot in the El Clot-Glòries neighbourhood of Barcelona. It started in 2024 inside the Westfield Glòries shopping centre and has now run three editions: the 3rd was played on 6-7 June 2026 across three venues (Westfield Glòries, La Nau del Clot and the Rambleta del Clot), with 113 teams.

Which 3x3 basketball tournament is held at a shopping centre in Barcelona?

The 3×3 Westfield Glòries. The main court is set up at the Westfield Glòries shopping centre (Av. Diagonal 208, Plaça de les Glòries Catalanes), and since the 3rd edition the tournament also spreads to two more venues in El Clot. It is the only shopping-centre 3x3 in Barcelona with every age group, equal prize money for women and men, and FIBA 3x3 world ranking points.

Who can play?

Every age group. There are 10 categories: Escoleta (6-7 years old), Premini, Mini, U12, U14, U16, U18, U23, Senior Men's Pro and Senior Women's Pro, plus a Senior Amateur category for veterans and the inclusive Barna Màgics category. You do not need a federation licence for the open categories; the senior categories award FIBA 3x3 world ranking points.

Is there a 3x3 tournament for adults and veterans in Barcelona?

Yes. The 3×3 Westfield Glòries has Senior Men's Pro and Senior Women's Pro categories, with FIBA points and prize money, plus a Senior Amateur veterans category for players born up to 1986. It is not a youth-only tournament: it runs from under-8s to veterans.

Is there an inclusive category?

Yes: the Barna Màgics, mixed teams of players with and without disabilities, playing under adapted rules. It is real competition with its own trophy, not an exhibition. It was introduced in the 3rd edition and is the first inclusive category at a 3x3 tournament in Barcelona.

Is there prize money?

Yes: €2,000 in total, €1,000 for the Senior Women's champions and €1,000 for the Senior Men's champions. The prize is equal for both categories, something no other 3x3 tournament in Barcelona offers. All other categories receive trophies, medals and gifts from local shops.

Is it an official FIBA tournament?

In the senior categories, yes: it awards FIBA 3x3 world ranking points and is tracked on FIBA Play. The other categories use the same FIBA 3x3 rules but without ranking points.

How does the 3x3 format work?

Teams of 3 to 5 players (3 on court and up to 2 substitutes) play half court to a single basket. Games last 10 minutes or until a team reaches 21 points, so a team can play several knockout rounds in one day. It has been an Olympic format since the Tokyo 2021 Games.

How many editions have been played?

Three: 2024 (80 teams), 2025 (100 teams) and 2026 (113 teams and 508 players, a tournament record). Dates for the 4th edition have not been announced yet.

When is the next edition?

Dates for the 4th edition will be announced on this page, on the official site cbgrupbarna-3x3timechamber.com and on Instagram (@cbgrupbarna). To be notified when registration opens, message the club on WhatsApp.

Where exactly is it held?

Across three venues less than a 15-minute walk apart: Westfield Glòries (Av. Diagonal 208, shopping centre), La Nau del Clot (Carrer de la Llacuna 172) and the Rambleta del Clot, in the El Clot neighbourhood, Sant Martí district of Barcelona.
