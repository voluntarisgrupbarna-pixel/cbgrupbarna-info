Flectomin, by Laboratorios Fardi: 200 sachets donated to CB Grup Barna

Laboratorios Fardi, a Barcelona pharmaceutical laboratory founded in 1970, has donated 200 Flectomin sachets to the club to help hydrate its teams. Here is the product, who is behind it, and how to get in touch.

CB Grup Barna · 1 Nov 2026

Also available in: català · castellano

Who is Laboratorios Fardi

Fardi is a pharmaceutical laboratory based in Barcelona (Grassot 16, 08025), active since 1970. It develops, manufactures and sells over-the-counter (OTC) medicines, and has expanded into cosmetics, food supplements and medical devices. One of its product lines is Flectomin.

What Flectomin is

Flectomin is an isotonic drink / powdered food supplement formulated with glucose and mineral salts — sodium, potassium and magnesium citrate, and calcium phosphate — intended to help replace fluids and electrolytes during physical exercise or heavy sweating. It is sold at pharmacies and parapharmacies in two formats: a 550 g tub and individual 20 g sachets, dissolved in a large glass of water.

The donation to Barna

Laboratorios Fardi has donated 200 Flectomin sachets to CB Grup Barna, which the club will use during training sessions and matches to help hydrate its teams. It is an in-kind collaboration, with no discount or commercial agreement attached: the laboratory donates the product, and the club explains its origin and composition transparently.

Important notice

Flectomin is a food supplement, not a medicine. This article is for informational purposes only and is not medical advice: it does not replace a balanced diet or a healthcare professional's recommendation. Anyone with diabetes, kidney problems or high blood pressure should check with a professional before using it, due to its glucose and sodium content.

Frequently asked questions

What is Flectomin?

An isotonic drink / powdered food supplement based on glucose and mineral salts (sodium, potassium, magnesium and calcium), intended to help replace fluids and electrolytes during physical exercise. It is sold in 550 g tubs and individual 20 g sachets at pharmacies and parapharmacies.

Who makes Flectomin?

Laboratorios Fardi, a Barcelona-based pharmaceutical laboratory active since 1970, headquartered at Grassot 16 (08025 Barcelona), specialising in OTC medicines, cosmetics and food supplements.

How many Flectomin sachets did Fardi donate to CB Grup Barna?

200 individual 20 g sachets, donated directly by the laboratory as a collaboration with the club, with no discount or commercial agreement attached.

Is Flectomin a medicine?

No: it is a food supplement, not a prescription medicine. As with any supplement, anyone with conditions such as diabetes, kidney problems or high blood pressure should consult a healthcare professional before using it, due to its glucose and sodium content.

Where can Flectomin be bought?

At pharmacies and parapharmacies, in a 550 g tub or boxes of individual 20 g sachets.
