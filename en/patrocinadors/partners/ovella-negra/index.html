About Ovella Negra

L'Ovella Negra is a leisure venue run by the Ovella Negra group in Poblenou (Sant Martí), housed in a former industrial building of more than 2,000 m². It offers craft beer, sangria, tapas, sandwiches and burgers, with table football and pool.
