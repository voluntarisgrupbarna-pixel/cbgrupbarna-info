Nova Farmàcia Clot, CB Grup Barna's newest partner

The pharmacy on Carrer del Clot joins the neighborhood grassroots basketball club's sponsor ecosystem for the 2026/27 season.

CB Grup Barna · 2026-08-17

A new neighborhood partner

Nova Farmàcia Clot, located at Carrer del Clot, 88, becomes a partner of CB Grup Barna starting in the 2026/27 season. It joins the rest of the neighborhood's shops and organizations — Eix Clot, Herbolaris Montserrat, Clínica Dental Bac de Roda, Armand Òptics, among others — that make the grassroots basketball club's day-to-day possible in El Clot.

Why a neighborhood pharmacy matters

CB Grup Barna is a grassroots club with more than 450 families connected to it and sixty years of history in El Clot. A local pharmacy joining as a partner reinforces exactly what the club stands for: a network of neighborhood shops and services made visible through grassroots sport, not a one-off campaign.

What it means to be a Barna partner

Being a CB Grup Barna partner means having a dedicated profile on the sponsor wall at cbgrupbarna.info/patrocinadors, with a direct link from the club's homepage and from the partners and collaborators map. Nova Farmàcia Clot already has its own profile at cbgrupbarna.info/patrocinadors/partners/nova-farmacia-clot, with its Instagram and, further down the line, an offer for Barna families.

Welcome to Barna

The club welcomes Nova Farmàcia Clot and invites everyone to follow them at @novafarmaciaclot. Anyone who wants to meet all of the club's partners can check out the article CB Grup Barna's sponsors: every partner.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Nova Farmàcia Clot?

Nova Farmàcia Clot is a neighborhood pharmacy located at Carrer del Clot 88, Barcelona, in the same neighborhood where CB Grup Barna plays.

Since when has it been a CB Grup Barna partner?

Since the 2026/27 season, as a new addition to the club's sponsor and partner ecosystem.

Where can I see Nova Farmàcia Clot's profile?

At cbgrupbarna.info/patrocinadors/partners/nova-farmacia-clot/, within the partner wall at cbgrupbarna.info/patrocinadors/.
