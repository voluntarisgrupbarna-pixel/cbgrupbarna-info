9 of 12 players on CB Grup Barna's senior B women's team came up through the club

Barcelona's Premi Dona i Esport recognises the Clot club, the second basketball club to win it: inclusive and gender-balanced.

Last season (2025-26), 9 of the 12 players on the senior B women's team came from the club's own youth system; in previous years only 3 or 4 made the step from junior to senior. This pathway, from the escoleta (youngest ages) to the senior teams, is part of the model recognised by the Club Award of the 19th Premi Dona i Esport of Barcelona City Council, worth €3,000.

Founded in 1965 in the Clot neighbourhood, CB Grup Barna is the second basketball club to receive the award, after CB Santa Rosa de Lima-Horta (12th edition). It now has 450 players and 35 federated teams. On court, both the men's and women's senior teams compete in the Supercopa, the top category of Catalan basketball.

«The award is theirs, because since they were little girls they chose to commit to the club, and that is a merit.»

Ana Fernández Durán · Board of CB Grup Barna

«The senior girls are an example to follow: it is the top category you can reach [at the club].»

Youth player · CB Grup Barna

Context and statements

Equality, built into the structure

The club allocates 50% of all its income to the women's line and the other 50% to the men's, so both have the same resources. This commitment also shows on the bench: the coaching staff has 68 coaches, 35 of them women.

From the escoleta to the senior team

At CB Grup Barna, the step from junior to senior is when most women players drop out. That is why the club has analysed this step. In 2025-26, of the 12 players on the senior B women's team, 9 came from the club's own youth system, four of them since the escoleta, and only 3 were new signings; in previous years, only 3 or 4 players made this step.

«The award is theirs»

In her acceptance speech, Ana Fernández Durán, a member of the club's Board, dedicated the award to the players. She recalled that the journey began in 1965 and that the recognition belongs to everyone who has been part of CB Grup Barna.

The model is a complete pathway: the youngest players can see that all the categories and teams they need are there to move up to the senior level. The older players serve as role models, and the club keeps training new women coaches, because the current ones are role models for them too. Each team usually has three coaches, and there are already girls' teams led entirely by three women coaches. In addition, more and more women coaches are coordinating the training sessions and matches of boys' teams.

Doing it well, she stressed, means creating «a safe space where teenagers and young children feel safe» in a sport that is «very inclusive»: «our main source of pride».

«We have many trophies at the club, but we are proud of them, from the youngest to the oldest.»

Ana Fernández Durán · Board of CB Grup Barna

Recognition for grassroots basketball

The award recognises a model that holds that girls and young women should not only have the chance to play basketball, but also to grow, train, take on responsibility and become role models for the next generations. The Premi Dona i Esport values the everyday work of neighbourhood clubs, often far from the media spotlight. For CB Grup Barna it is a boost to keep building a sport in which playing, coaching and leading do not depend on gender. This commitment sits alongside its support for inclusive sport, through the Barna Màgics project, and its close ties to the social life of the Clot and Sant Martí.

About CB Grup Barna

Non-profit grassroots basketball club founded in 1965 in the Clot (Sant Martí, Barcelona), based at La Nau del Clot. It works for gender parity, the growth of women's basketball, the training of women coaches and inclusive sport. More information: cbgrupbarna.info

Frequently asked questions

What award did CB Grup Barna win?

The Club Award of the 19th edition of Barcelona City Council's Premi Dona i Esport, worth €3,000, presented on 4 November 2026.

Why did CB Grup Barna win the award?

For its gender-balanced, inclusive model: 50% of income goes to the women's line and 50% to the men's, 35 of the 68 coaches are women, and 9 of the 12 players on the 2025-26 senior B women's team came from the club's own youth system.

What other basketball club has received this award?

CB Grup Barna is the second basketball club to receive it, after CB Santa Rosa de Lima-Horta, which won the 12th edition.

How can the media contact the club?
