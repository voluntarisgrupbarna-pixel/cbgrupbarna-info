The Barna, with data: eleven lines of research into youth basketball

Three club studies merged into a single report: what the science says about how a young player should be developed, the Marc Gasol precedent and the Barna's real position among 22 Catalan and Spanish clubs.

CB Grup Barna · 2026-08-16

Why a basketball club writes a thesis

CB Grup Barna commissioned and merged three independent studies: a doctoral-level literature review on youth basketball (eleven lines of research, eighteen chapters, close to 900 consultations of academic and federation sources), the catalogue of Marc Gasol's public statements about his own development model, and a benchmark by the Marketing Department covering 92 Instagram accounts verified one by one.

The result is not a document to file away in a drawer: it is the case the club puts to the Board, the District and its sponsors.

What the science says about dropout

  • Dropout concentrates at ages 12–14, not at 11 — it coincides with the move from under-14 to under-16.
  • Enjoyment counts two and a half times more than winning in whether a child keeps playing (correlation 0.45 against 0.18).
  • 92.8% of those who stand out at junior level are not those who stand out as adults. Selecting by performance at 12 does not work.
  • Being born in January or in December decides more than talent: 50.3% of regional selections were born between January and March, and only 6.8% between October and December.

The precedent: Marc Gasol

The Barna model has a documented reference point: Marc Gasol founded a basketball school in Girona in 2014 —not a team— and did not create a first senior side until 2017. Three seasons of youth work before a single senior team.

«We did not rush to reach the ACB.»

It is, point by point, the same order CB Grup Barna has defended for sixty-one years: first the school, then the team.

Frequently asked questions

What does the research conclude about why children quit basketball?

That enjoyment counts two and a half times more than winning games in whether a child keeps playing, and that dropout concentrates at ages 12-14, in the move from under-14 to under-16.

Who is the reference point for the CB Grup Barna model?

Marc Gasol, who founded a basketball school in Girona in 2014 and did not create a first senior team until 2017: three years of youth work before a single senior side.

Where does the Barna rank among Catalonia's development clubs?

6th of 22 Catalan and Spanish development clubs by Instagram followers, and 3rd in structure with 32 federated teams — the leading development club in the city of Barcelona.
