· The club

A neighbourhood club with city-wide reach

A Barcelona basketball club rooted in its district, with girls' and boys' teams from the Escoleta to the senior side. In 2025·26 we closed the season with 1,109 posts and daily coverage of every team.

What we post, and how often

Posting schedule
  • Calendar — 3 to 4 times a week: reports, photos and reels
  • Training — twice a week: the work nobody sees, and technique
  • Club content — 5 times a week: news, signings and renewals
  • Sponsor mentions — weekly, in stories, posts and reels
  • Events — camps, tournaments, finals and activations, as they come

A network of collaborations

In the 25·26 season we mentioned or worked with 197 different accounts: neighbourhood businesses, photographers and local press, the Federació Catalana de Bàsquet and other clubs.

What reaches furthest

An event, a standout player and neighbourhood values. The most-watched piece of the season reaches 35.6K views, and three of the top five are of girls' teams.
