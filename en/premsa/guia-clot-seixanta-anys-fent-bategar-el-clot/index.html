Thank you, Eix Clot

Guia Clot magazine, published by Eix Clot, dedicated its lead feature to CB Grup Barna for the club's 60th anniversary. Thank you, Eix Clot. Thank you, Gemma Collell.

Text by Gemma Collell · Guia Clot · Camp de l'Arpa · 2026-08-08

In El Clot we all know each other, and when someone from the neighbourhood notices the work you do every day, that means more than any headline. That's why we want to say thank you, by name, to Eix Clot — the neighbourhood's association of shopkeepers and entrepreneurs — and to journalist Gemma Collell, who wrote the lead feature in Guia Clot · Camp de l'Arpa magazine about CB Grup Barna: sixty years of grassroots basketball in the neighbourhood, told with care and with solid facts.

We reproduce here the full text of the article, exactly as it was published, so that anyone who didn't get to hold the magazine can read it too.

Full article · Guia Clot · Camp de l'Arpa

El CB Grup Barna · Seixanta anys fent bategar el Clot

Club Bàsquet Grup Barna has become a genuine social and sporting pillar within the Sant Martí district. With inclusion and neighbourhood roots as its banner, the club makes sport accessible to everyone from the legendary court at the Nau del Clot.

To talk about Club Bàsquet Grup Barna is to talk about the living history of El Clot. Founded in 1965, this emblematic club now celebrates sixty years of history, establishing itself as one of the largest and most active grassroots basketball clubs in the entire Sant Martí district. Over six decades, the club has not only watched thousands of players grow up on its courts, but has also managed to turn sport into an invaluable tool of social cohesion for the whole neighbourhood. With around 450 members and close to 40 federated teams in competition, it also stands out for a complete parity between its men's and women's sections.

The key to Grup Barna's success and longevity lies in its unique way of understanding the practice of sport. Far from focusing solely on scoreboards or competitive pressure, the club has always been guided by non-negotiable principles: universal access to basketball, real equality of opportunity, diversity and a deep sense of belonging to the neighbourhood. For the Barna, basketball is everyone's right and an ideal space to build friendships for life, sharing values such as respect, cooperation, health, perseverance and collective commitment.

This firm inclusive philosophy translates into concrete actions that fill the area with pride. The best example of this is the Barna Màgics project, a basketball team specially designed for people with intellectual disabilities. Week after week, this section proves that barriers don't exist where there is passion and a will to come together, becoming a benchmark for integration in the city. The club also keeps up a constant bond with community life in the neighbourhood, regularly collaborating with the Eix Comercial del Clot traders' association, local schools and grassroots organisations, taking part in charitable and festive activities that bring the neighbourhood together.

This valuable and ongoing social commitment received significant official recognition from the city of Barcelona's institutions. In February 2026, the Mayor of Barcelona, Jaume Collboni, personally visited the club's official headquarters at the well-known Nau del Clot. During this formal meeting, the mayor wanted to learn first-hand about the details of this major sporting project, formally celebrate its 60 years of history, and praise the club's strong presence in the community.

Its activity never stops and stays very much alive at any time of year. This very summer, the club has once again become the great driving force of the warmer months thanks to its acclaimed «Time Chamber» Summer Camp, where children combine improving their technical skills with team-building workshops, as well as running the spectacular 3x3 basketball tournaments at the Westfield Glòries site, energising the whole area. If you want to learn more about its work, become part of its big grassroots basketball family or check out its upcoming events, don't hesitate to visit its official website at cbgrupbarna.com. Basketball in El Clot has a glorious past, a vibrant present and a future that keeps on growing.

Who wrote the article about CB Grup Barna in Guia Clot?

The article «El CB Grup Barna: seixanta anys fent bategar el Clot» was written by journalist Gemma Collell, for Guia Clot · Camp de l'Arpa magazine, published by Eix Clot.

Where was the article published?
