Barna, by the numbers: eleven research threads on youth basketball

Three club studies merged into a single report: what science says about how to develop a young player, the Marc Gasol precedent, and Barna's real position among 22 Catalan and Spanish clubs.

CB Grup Barna · 2026-08-16

Why a basketball club writes a thesis

CB Grup Barna commissioned and merged three independent studies: a doctoral-scope literature review on youth basketball development (eleven research threads, eighteen chapters, close to 900 academic and federation sources consulted), the catalogue of Marc Gasol's public statements about his own development model, and a benchmark by the Marketing Department covering 92 Instagram accounts checked one by one.

The result isn't a document to file away: it's the case the club makes to the Board, the District and its sponsors.

What the science says about dropout

  • Dropout is concentrated between ages 12 and 14, not 11 — it coincides with the move from pre-youth to cadet.
  • Fun matters two and a half times more than winning in whether a child keeps playing (correlation 0.45 versus 0.18).
  • 92.8% of standout junior players aren't the ones who stand out as adults. Selecting by performance at age 12 doesn't work.
  • Being born in January or December matters more than talent: 50.3% of players on regional selections were born between January and March, and only 6.8% between October and December.

The precedent: Marc Gasol

Barna's model has a documented reference: Marc Gasol founded a basketball school in Girona in 2014 — not a team — and didn't create the first senior side until 2017. Three seasons of youth development before a single top team.

"We didn't rush to reach the ACB."

It's, point by point, the same order CB Grup Barna has defended for sixty years: the school first, the team after.

Barna doesn't measure itself against Barça or Joventut: it measures itself against clubs doing the same thing, developing children with youth academies and families. Against that real universe, the club is the third-largest structure and the city's leading digital community.

Frequently asked questions

What does the research on why children quit basketball conclude?

That fun matters two and a half times more than winning games in whether a child keeps playing, and that dropout is concentrated between ages 12 and 14, during the move from pre-youth to cadet.

Who is the reference for CB Grup Barna's model?

Marc Gasol, who founded a basketball school in Girona in 2014 and didn't create the first senior team until 2017: three years of grassroots work before a single top team.

Where does Barna rank among Catalonia's grassroots clubs?

6th of 22 Catalan and Spanish grassroots clubs by Instagram followers, and 3rd by structure with 32 federated teams — the leading grassroots club in Barcelona city.
