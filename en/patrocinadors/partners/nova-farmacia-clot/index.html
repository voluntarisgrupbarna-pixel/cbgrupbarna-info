About Nova Farmàcia Clot

Nova Farmàcia Clot is a neighbourhood pharmacy at Carrer del Clot 88, Barcelona, in the heart of El Clot, where CB Grup Barna plays. Since the 2026/27 season it has been part of the club's ecosystem of partners and supporters.
