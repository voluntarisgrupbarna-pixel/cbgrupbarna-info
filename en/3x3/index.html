3x3 Barcelona

The 3x3 basketball tournament CB Grup Barna organises at Westfield Glòries, right next to El Clot. Street format, short back-to-back matches, youth categories and open categories for groups of friends.

What is 3x3 basketball

3x3 is half-court basketball: three players per team and a single basket. Matches are short, the score moves fast and on a single day a team can play several knockout rounds back to back. It's the format that entered the Olympic Games in Tokyo and the one that works best on the street, because it only needs half a court and an afternoon.

Barna's 3x3, at Glòries

CB Grup Barna brings the tournament to Westfield Glòries, on Plaça de les Glòries Catalanes, together with Time Chamber and the Clot and Glòries commercial area. The idea is simple: take basketball out of the sports hall and put it where people already are, a place thousands of people pass through on a Saturday morning.

The tournament has youth categories, designed for club teams, and open categories where any group can sign up without being a federated club. That mix is what gives it its atmosphere: club teams and groups of friends from the neighbourhood share the same court.

How to sign up a team

Registration is done directly on the tournament's official website, cbgrupbarna-3x3timechamber.com, by team and not by player. Places are limited and recent editions have filled up before starting, so the sooner registration is completed, the safer it is. If registration for the next edition hasn't opened yet, advance notice can be requested via the club's WhatsApp or by leaving contact details in the information form.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Westfield Glòries 3x3?

It's the street 3x3 basketball tournament that CB Grup Barna organises at the Westfield Glòries shopping centre in Barcelona, with Time Chamber and the Clot-Glòries commercial area. It's played on an outdoor court, 3-on-3 on a single basket.

Who can play?

There are youth categories and open categories, so both club teams and groups of friends can play. You don't need to be a federated player to sign up for the open categories.

What is the 3x3 format like?

Three players per team on a single basket, on a half court. Matches are short and back to back, so on a single day a team can play several knockout rounds. It's the Olympic basketball format since the Tokyo Games.

When is the next edition?

Dates are announced on this page, on the official website cbgrupbarna-3x3timechamber.com and on Instagram (@cbgrupbarna). The tournament is usually held at the start of summer. Anyone who wants to be notified when registration opens can ask via the club's WhatsApp.

Where exactly is it?

At Westfield Glòries, on Plaça de les Glòries Catalanes in Barcelona, right next to the El Clot neighbourhood and the Sant Martí District.
