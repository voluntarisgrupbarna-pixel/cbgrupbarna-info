What it is
A basketball skills camp. Not a holiday club with a ball: each week works on one specific part of the game.
Who runs it
CB Grup Barna × Time Chamber. The club provides the structure and the coaches; Time Chamber, the individual training methodology.
Where
La Nau del Clot, Barcelona. The club's official venue.
Address
Carrer de la Llacuna, 170-172 · 08018 Barcelona · El Clot neighbourhood, Sant Martí district.
Getting there
Metro L1 Glòries and L2 Clot · Rodalies Clot-Aragó · Clot and Glòries buses. Walking distance from Westfield Glòries.
Who it is for
Seven age groups: Escoleta, under-8, under-10, under-11, under-12, under-14 and under-16. Groups are made by category and level, so a beginner does not train alongside someone who has been competing for years.
Girls and boys
Yes. The camp is mixed and the club has genuine parity between its girls' and boys' sides.
Do you have to be a Barna player?
No. It is open to players from any club in Barcelona and the province. Club players get priority registration and their own price.
When
In summer, in consecutive weeks from late June to early August. The 2026 edition ran from 23 June to 1 August.
Hours
Full day 9:00am to 5:00pm · half day 9:00am to 1:30pm.
Price
Depends on the modality and whether you play for the club. Not published on the site: message us with the age and we'll give it to you the same day. Payment in instalments available.
Lunch
Included in the full day. The half day finishes before lunch.
Services
Morning drop-off service, lunch on the full day and a Friday outing to Illa Fantasia.
What is worked on
Shooting, ball handling, one-on-one play, individual fundamentals and game reading. Each week has its own focus, so anyone coming for more than one week does not repeat.
Who coaches
The club's coaches and Time Chamber's. Robert Willett and Nolan Willett (NBA skills coaches), Ainhoa López (professional player, Spain international), Malak Shady (3x3 MVP), Serge Ibaka (2019 NBA champion) and Yankuba Sima (EuroLeague) have all been through it.
How many take part
More than 200 players per edition, with roughly 50 places a week to keep the working ratio.
Language
Catalan and Spanish. International guest coaches work in English with translation.
How to sign up
By WhatsApp on +34 698 425 153 or through the club's form. Recent editions have filled up before starting.

What the Barna camp is

The basketball camp run by CB Grup Barna is the intensive training programme the club organises during the school holidays in the El Clot neighbourhood. It is not a leisure activity with a ball: it is skills training, with sessions dedicated to one specific part of the game and groups made by age and level.

The club runs it with Time Chamber, which brings the individual training methodology. That combination is what defines the camp: the repetition volume of a skills academy with the team atmosphere of a neighbourhood club with sixty-one years of history.

Who coaches

What separates a skills camp from a holiday club with a ball is not what the leaflet says: it is the coaches who turn up. The day-to-day work is led by the coaches of the club and of Time Chamber, to the same standard as during the season. And every edition invites people who do not usually set foot in a neighbourhood camp:

These are not collaborations announced in a press release: they are recorded, published and can be watched. That is the difference between a camp that puts a well-known name on the cover and a camp where that name steps onto the court.

And outside the summer

The summer camp is the bulk of it, but not the only skills week of the year. During the school holidays the club runs two short editions in the same place and with the same methodology:

  • Christmas camp — between late December and early January.
  • Easter camp — the Flow Camp, one week during the school holidays.

If what you want is to understand what skills training actually means before signing anyone up, that is explained on Basketball skills training in Barcelona.

The camp and the club's 3x3

The camp is not Barna's only summer fixture in El Clot. The club also runs the 3×3 Westfield Glòries, a FIBA-registered tournament with €2,000 in prizes —€1,000 for the women's and €1,000 for the men's, split equally, unique in Barcelona—, 10 categories and three venues in the neighbourhood. Many families do both: camp on summer mornings, tournament on a June weekend.

How it is organised

Each week has its own focus, so anyone coming for more than one week does not repeat content. These are the modules the club has worked on in recent editions:

01 23–27 June

Flow Camp

Game reading and decision-making.

02 30 June–4 July

TC Basics

Fundamentals: footwork, balance, mechanics.

03 7–11 July

Shooting Academy

Shooting, in every situation.

04 14–18 July

One & One Mastery

One-on-one play: fakes and attacking creativity.

05 21–25 July

Ballhandling Lab

Ball handling and dribbling under pressure.

06 28 July–1 August

Skills Lab Experience

Combined skills work: the finale of the edition.

The venue

La Nau del Clot, Carrer de la Llacuna 170-172, 08018 Barcelona — the main sports venue of CB Grup Barna, in the El Clot neighbourhood. Camp hours: 9am–1:30pm for the half day, 9am–5pm for the full day, with a drop-off service and midday meal included on the full day.

Who it is aimed at

Children who play basketball or want to start, whether they are with CB Grup Barna or come from another club. Each week the club reserves places for its own players and places open to players from outside, and groups are closed by age and level so that nobody trains above or below where they belong.

If your child has not started yet and is between 4 and 8, the natural route is not the camp but the Escoleta, the club's basketball school, which runs all year with Julio Torralba.

Next edition

The dates of the next edition and the opening of registration are announced on this page and on @cbgrupbarna. Recent editions have filled up before starting, so the safe way in is to ask for advance notice: the club writes to everyone on the list when places open, a day before publishing it.

Frequently asked questions

Which coaches and players have visited the camp?

Recent editions have hosted Ainhoa López, a professional player and Spain international who trained in El Clot; Robert Willet (@bballwillett), an NBA skills trainer; and Malak Shady, a basketball creator and 3x3 MVP. They don't drop in for a photo: they run sessions on court with the camp players.

How many players came to the camp in summer 2026?

Around 150 girls and boys across the six weeks of the 2026 edition, in groups by age and level and with a limit of roughly 50 places per week. Players came both from CB Grup Barna and from other clubs in Barcelona and the province.

What ages is the basketball camp for?

The CB Grup Barna camp is for youth players, from Escoleta age up to cadet and junior categories. Groups are organised by age and level, so a child who's just starting doesn't train alongside someone who's been competing for years.

Do you have to play for CB Grup Barna to sign up?

No. The camp is open to players from any club in Barcelona and the province. Every week there are places for Barna players and places for players from outside.

How many places are there per week?

The camp works with a limit of around 50 places per week to keep a good coach-to-player ratio. Weeks fill up in registration order, and recent editions have sold out before starting.

Where is the camp held?

In the El Clot neighbourhood, Sant Martí District of Barcelona. La Nau del Clot is CB Grup Barna's main sports venue.

When does registration open for the next edition?

The dates for the next edition are announced on this page and on Instagram (@cbgrupbarna). Anyone who wants to be notified before it opens to the public can ask via the club's WhatsApp (+34 698 425 153) and will be added to the notification list.

When is the summer camp? Dates and weeks

In summer, in consecutive weeks from late June to early August. The 2026 edition ran from 23 June to 1 August: six weeks, and each one can be booked separately. The dates for the next edition are announced on this page and on Instagram (@cbgrupbarna).
