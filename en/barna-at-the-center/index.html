Barna at the Center

The classic sports thesis is “higher division, bigger audience, better sponsors”. CB Grup Barna is testing another: more community, more activation, more content and more values. Here are the concrete campaigns that already prove it.

The center is not the competition

A club doesn't have to wait for promotion to have a strong sponsorship model. Barna already has the assets brands look for, and competition is just one circle among others: around it sit families, territory, events, content and values.

Two commercial streets as club partners

Eix del Clot and Eix Comercial de Sant Martí, the two commercial associations, are Barna partners. That means real local influence: hundreds of district shops that already know the club, and a club that is a meeting point for neighbourhood families. A brand joining Barna also joins the conversation of local retail.

Physical presence at the arena

La Nau del Clot (Parc del Clot) draws people every weekend. According to the published FCBQ calendar, Barna plays at home an average of 8 games per weekend in autumn, peaking at 10. Counting players, coaching staff, families, the visiting team and referees/table, we estimate about 45 people per game: ~370 people every weekend (up to ~450 on busy weekends) and about 7,000 people-entries per season, from the 17 teams with a published calendar alone.

Current agreements and delivered activations

Every example is a real campaign or partnership, not a promise. Every partner from the 2025-26 season has renewed for 2026-27: we have not lost a single one.

Event

Westfield Glòries × 3x3

Basketball leaves the gym and enters one of Barcelona's major shopping centres: 113 teams (a record) in June 2026, spread across Westfield, La Nau and the Rambleta. The club doesn't just play: it organises, drives footfall and creates experiences.

Influencers

Influencers at the 3x3

Six creators came to the tournament, with 1.9 million combined followers: Ari Geli (1 M), Rubén RG (401 k), Hers0n (198 k), Ismael Bouss (138 k), Pol Fores (118 k) and Edu Gil (63.4 k). Content, interviews and reels that reach beyond the community.

Naming

Instax Fujifilm × Senior A Women

Team naming, jersey, gamedays and Media Day. Photography fits club life naturally, and the brand moves from sitting next to Barna to being part of its story.

All year

Camps × Time Chamber

Christmas, February, Easter and summer. The summer camp grew around 30% in revenue from 2025 to 2026: when the competition ends, the club keeps existing for families.

International

International camps

Camps attended by NBA and EuroLeague players. Kids from El Clot train with role models they normally only see on TV: an asset no outside competition can give a brand.

Recognition

Federation's best-practice award

A week ago the federation awarded Barna for its best practices. A recent, external endorsement for any brand joining the club.

Finalists

Premi Dona i Esport

Barna is a finalist for the Premi Dona i Esport: women's basketball as half the club and as a recognised value, with women coaches and role models across the structure.

Architecture

Architecture Showcases · Barcelona 2026

Barcelona 2026, World Capital of Architecture, places architects' models in shops across all ten districts. La Nau del Clot takes part with two models from Gómez & Diaz arquitectes (CORTILS 59 and BELLAVISTA 3), in a safe indoor space. The arena becomes an exhibition room for the city.

In talks

LED screen at La Nau

Possible free loan of an LED screen for the arena in 2026-27, pending the company's decision. On-screen advertising, banner, social media and launch: a new commercial surface at La Nau for every weekend game.

Lines in development

Open conversations, not agreements yet. We keep them apart from delivered activations so nothing is inflated.

  • Beauty and personal care — locker rooms, travel, Media Day and community content.
  • Social impact — a blood-donation drive aiming for 40 donations.
  • Rare diseases — a solidarity jersey or a themed 3x3.
  • Women's leadership — role models, women coaches and content with a specialised organisation.

Owned media

Instagram has passed 700,000 views in some months of 2026, with a reel-first strategy. On top of that come WhatsApp, email and physical presence. A brand doesn't just buy a fence banner: it joins a year-round content schedule, including our own series La feina que no es veu, Radar Barna and Efecte Clark, which can be sponsored without sponsoring a whole team.

Barna as brand ambassador

A partner brand doesn't just appear at Barna: Barna takes it into the neighbourhood. Every family, player and coach is an ambassador: 450+ families who talk to their circle every week, 400 players wearing the shirt and partner colours, and an owned channel (Instagram, WhatsApp, email) that tells the partnership in its own voice. A recommendation from a Clot family is worth more than any banner.

What a brand really buys

  • Visibility and content
  • Community and belonging
  • Experiences and product trial

  • Reputation and social impact
  • Territory: El Clot and Sant Martí
  • Physical and digital activation

Barna competes on values

Women's basketball across the whole structure, Barna Màgics, Escoleta from age 4, open days with 200+ interested children and Cistella Petita. A company joining Barna isn't linked to a recent project: it's linked to a club founded in 1965 and rooted in Barcelona.

You don't need to reach the top league to be interesting to brands. Brands need to understand everything Barna already is.
