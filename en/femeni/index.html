Women's basketball for real, not for a campaign

CB Grup Barna puts 50% of its budget into women's basketball when the sector average is 35%. Eight federated teams, 38 women coaches and a path that runs from the Escoleta at age 4 all the way to the Lliga Femenina Endesa. It's not a quota policy: it's how the club has been built since 1965.

The Barna Method

A system. Not a campaign.

Three pillars that work together and reinforce each other. The club has built them organically over six decades, and today they are the subject of academic study at UB-INEFC.

01

Equal investment

50% of the club's budget goes to women's basketball. The sector average is 35%.

With €140,000-160,000 a year, that means €70,000-80,000 for women's basketball. It doesn't take more money: it takes splitting up what's already there differently.

02

Leadership pipeline

38 active women coaches, 65.5% of the technical staff. They coach 40 teams every week in El Clot.

A player who becomes a coach — and who coaches men's teams too — changes how girls, boys and families see things far more than any equality campaign.

03

Community response

53.7% of the audience the club reaches on social media is women. In grassroots basketball, digital audiences tend to run 60-70% male.

With no targeted campaign at all. When a club practises equality instead of just talking about it, the community that belongs to it shows up on its own.

"We don't look for them. They find us."

The path

From age 4 to the Lliga Endesa

The Barna Method's Leadership Cycle: six stages that explain how a girl who joins the Escoleta can end up competing at the elite level — or coaching from the bench.

  1. EscoletaAt age 4 she joins the club and, from day one, sees women coaching.
  2. DevelopmentFederated competition at every category: mini, under-14, cadet and junior.
  3. CoachThe player joins the coaching staff. She coaches women's, mixed and men's teams.
  4. Copa CatalunyaThe senior team competes at regional level.
  5. Super Copa FCBQCompetitive excellence and a gateway to the national leagues.
  6. Lliga EndesaTalent reaches the elite. The example has a name: Ainhoa López.

Club ambassador

The Ainhoa Effect

Ainhoa López Rodríguez started playing here as a girl. She came up through every category, was capped internationally at youth level, and reached the Lliga Femenina Endesa. An autoimmune illness stopped her right when she was at her peak.

She came back to the neighbourhood to recover, and training with the club's players she went on to compete professionally again. Today she is CB Grup Barna's first official ambassador and graduated Cum Laude from UB-INEFC.

"I came back to the neighbourhood to recover. And the neighbourhood gave me basketball back."

It matters because the structural problem with Catalan women's basketball isn't only about money: it's about role models close to home. Girls watch the WNBA on television, but they have no one to look up to on their own street. Here, they do.

Strategic project

Women's Line: kit designed for them

Every season, more than 200 girls, teenagers and women wear the club's kit. And until now they wore clothes that hadn't been designed for them: scaled-down men's sizes, shirts that ran too long, waists that didn't fit. The industry calls it "shrink it and pink it".

The differences aren't about size, they're structural: hip-to-waist ratio, shoulder width, torso length, support, and the need for dark fabric to compete without worrying about stains during menstruation. Discomfort with a uniform is documented as a direct factor in teenage girls dropping out of sport.

CB Grup Barna is developing the first collection by a grassroots club in Catalonia designed from scratch for the female body: new patterns, functional materials and co-creation with the players, who test the prototypes and have the final say.

For families

Frequently asked questions

How many women's teams does the club have?

In the 2026-27 season there are eight in federated FCBQ competition, from Under-14 through Senior, plus the Escoleta groups and the Barna Màgics adapted-sport team. The Senior Women's A team competes in the Super Copa Femenina.

My daughter has never played. Can she start?

Yes. The Escoleta welcomes girls and boys aged 4 to 7 with no prior experience, and places open up in the developmental categories during the season if there's room. Write to us and we'll tell you what suits her age.

What does it cost? Are there scholarships?

The club maintains social scholarships so no girl is left out for financial reasons. Tell us about your situation and we'll look into it with no obligation: request information.

Who coaches the women's teams?

38 active women coaches, 65.5% of the club's technical staff. Many are former players trained at the club, and they coach women's, mixed and men's teams alike.

Where does training and competition take place?

At La Nau del Clot, C/ Llacuna 172, inside Parc del Clot (08018 Barcelona), in the Sant Martí district. Each team's matches and schedules are on the match days page.
