Honours

What the club has won on court, season by season. It has never been the main point —that's the story we tell on the history page— but it's real, and it deserves to be written down in one place.

2025-26 · Both senior teams in the FCBQ Supercup

CB Grup Barna was the only club in Barcelona with both senior teams —men's and women's— qualified for and competing in the Catalan Basketball Federation's Supercup in the same season: the top regional competition, one step below the leap to the national leagues.

The women's senior team finished the regular season in 4th place, with a 17-7 record. Judit Ortiz was picked for the 2026 Women's FCBQ Supercup All Star team, and Marta Zori finished among the competition's top three-point scorers. The men's senior team competed at the same level without leaving the neighbourhood.

Barna Màgics · 2025-26 ACELL Catalan League

The club's inclusive basketball team, together with the Fundació Mullor, closed the season as runners-up in the Catalan League after a hard-fought game against Joventut, overcoming significant injury absences in the previous rounds. They also finished third at the La Seu d'Urgell tournament.

2026-27 · Both senior teams, again

The 2026-27 season tips off in September with both senior teams once again entered in the FCBQ Supercup: not a one-off season, but the result of years of sustained development.

The origins · the table tennis section

In the club's early years, before it became a basketball-only entity, Grup Barna also had a table tennis section, which won three Barcelona Championships (1968, 1969 and 1970) and a Spanish Championship. Over time, the section stopped being played.

Frequently asked questions

What honours has CB Grup Barna won?

The standout achievement is being, in the 2025-26 season, the only club in Barcelona with both senior teams —men's and women's— in the FCBQ Supercup at the same time. On top of that, the Barna Màgics finished runners-up in the ACELL Catalan League, and, back in the club's early years, its table tennis section won three Barcelona Championships and a Spanish Championship.

Has CB Grup Barna's senior team ever won a league?

No, and the club does not hide it: the achievement of both senior teams is competing year after year in the FCBQ Supercup, the top regional competition, not any league title. The women's senior team finished 4th in the 2025-26 season, with a 17-7 record.

What are the Barna Màgics and what have they won?

They are the club's inclusive basketball team, with players with intellectual disabilities, competing in the ACELL league together with the Fundació Mullor. In the 2025-26 season they finished runners-up in the Catalan League and third at the La Seu d'Urgell tournament.

Why doesn't the club make more of its honours?

Because what it has always claimed as most important is not sporting honours, however good they are, but the other thing: a place where generations of kids from El Clot feel at home, learn to play and make friendships that in many cases last a lifetime. The titles are real and now written down, but they do not define the club.
