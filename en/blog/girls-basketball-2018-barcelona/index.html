Looking for the 2018 girls: why a whole club goes after one birth year

On Saturday 19 September, at La Nau del Clot, the girls born in 2018 who want to give it a go train with us. It is worth explaining why a club focuses on one particular year, and what lies behind this kind of call.

CB Grup Barna · 2026-09-10

A youth team is built by birth year

In Catalan basketball, teams are not built by school year but by birth year. That means every player born in 2018 will compete together, season after season, until they reach senior level. And it means something less obvious too: if one year is short of players, that group carries the problem for ten years.

With eight or nine players a team trains well, keeps moving, and can lose one to an injury or a change of school without anything falling apart. With five, every absence is half a session. So when a club sees that a birth year is running tight, it does not wait: it goes looking for players.

Why 2018, and why girls

CB Grup Barna has the same number of teams and the same budget for its women's and men's lines, and 65.5% women on its coaching staff. That does not hold up on its own: it holds up if every birth year has enough girls. Right now, 2018 is the one that needs to grow.

This is not particular to this club. Catalan youth basketball has historically had fewer girls than boys, and the gap widens exactly at the ages when they start dropping out. We set it out with data in why girls quit basketball. A neighbourhood club cannot answer that by regretting it: it has to open the door and go and find them, year by year.

A Supercopa player's first dribble looks like any other child's: bad, with two hands and eyes glued to the ball.

What happens on Saturday 19 September

That day, from 09:00 to 10:30 at La Nau del Clot (carrer de la Llacuna 170-172), there is the Saturday session of the Escoleta, which is where everyone coming for the first time has their trial. The 2018 girls train inside that session, with the group they would join.

  • It is free and commits you to nothing: you try one day and then you decide.
  • No experience needed. No dribbling, no previous playing, no federation licence.
  • What to bring: sports clothes, indoor court shoes and a water bottle. We provide the ball.
  • Places are booked through the form on the home page, so we can plan the groups: cbgrupbarna.info.

Anyone who cannot make the 19th misses nothing: the whole of September is open days and we give you another day, from the Escoleta to the senior teams.

And after the trial, what then

If she stays, she trains twice a week — Wednesdays at Escola Casas and Saturdays at La Nau — and registers with the club, which explains the fee and what it covers before anyone commits to anything. No family has to decide anything on the day of the trial.

And if she does not stay, that is fine too. A child who tries a sport and decides it is not for her has learned something anyway. What we do not want is for her never to have been able to try, because her birth year did not have enough girls to make a team.

Frequently asked questions

When is the basketball trial for girls born in 2018?

On Saturday 19 September 2026, from 09:00 to 10:30, at La Nau del Clot (carrer de la Llacuna 170-172, Barcelona), inside the Escoleta's Saturday session. It is free and places are booked through the form on cbgrupbarna.info.

Do you need to have played basketball before coming?

No. The trial is for starting from scratch: no need to know how to dribble, no kit and no federation licence. Just sports clothes, indoor court shoes and a water bottle.

Why are teams built by birth year and not by school year?

Because that is the Catalan Basketball Federation's rule: every player born in the same year competes in the same category, whatever school year she is in.

What if we cannot come on 19 September?

The whole of September is open days at CB Grup Barna. The same form lets you ask for another day and the club replies with the day and time that would suit her age.

Do you want to come and try it?

Leave us a name and a contact and we will tell you the day and time. The first session is free.
