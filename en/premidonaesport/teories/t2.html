Theory 2 · Research framework

The Recognition Ladder.

35.7% of players. 9% in the advanced coaching course. The gap is measured by the progression rate, not by the number of participants.

The second hidden pattern

What the official figures don't show.

Women's licences are growing: +19,165 in Spain (2023-24), 67% of total growth. It would seem the system works. But the official figures hide a devastating pattern: the recognition ladder is broken.

Level% WomenLoss vs players

The hidden pattern is that the celebration of growth in players masks the collapse in progression. It is like celebrating that more people are entering a building without mentioning that the lift is broken and the stairs are out.

Theoretical formulation

The Recognition Ladder Theory.

In any sporting system, the gender gap is not correctly measured by the number of participants but by the progression rate between rungs of power. A progression rate below 60% between any pair of consecutive rungs indicates a structural barrier, not a lack of talent.

In Spanish basketball:

  • →
    From player to coach in training: 67.5% (24.1/35.7). Indicates a barrier.
  • →
    From coach in training to Advanced Course: 37.3% (9/24.1). Indicates a severe barrier.

The Barna Method neutralises them by generating a natural progression within the club itself, without waiting for the federation system to correct its own barriers.

The 38: the most important figure in the research

The system does not measure what matters.

CB Grup Barna has 38 active coaches. None holds a federation licence. To the FCBQ, they do not exist.

This reveals the deepest pattern of all: the system does not measure what matters. The statistics count the qualified, not those who coach. Real talent is invisible to the official figures.

If CB Grup Barna's 38 coaches existed statistically, the percentage of women coaches in Barcelona would rise significantly. Multiplied across every club in Catalonia with unlicensed coaches, the "shortage of coaches" problem reveals itself for what it really is: a measurement problem, not a talent problem.

«The future is already coaching. It just needs to be recognised.»

Doctoral research · UB-INEFC · 2026

To correct this, the club has created the Barna 8M Fund: an annual grant to fund training and the federation licence for the club's coaches.
