Summer camp

Campus Time Chamber 2026: programme, prices and venue

Six weeks, six different technical focuses, at La Nau del Clot. The camp CB Grup Barna runs with Time Chamber already has its programme and prices locked in for the summer.

CB Grup Barna · 2026-08-16

Skills training, not a summer playscheme

CB Grup Barna's camp isn't a leisure activity with a ball thrown in: it's skills training, with sessions dedicated to one specific aspect of the game and groups formed by age and level. The club runs it with Time Chamber, which brings the individual-training methodology — short, highly repetitive sessions on a single aspect of the game. Barna brings the other half: sixty years of a neighborhood club's team atmosphere.

The six weeks

  • Flow Camp (June 23–27) — Offensive flow and reading the game in motion.
  • TC Basics (June 30–Jul 4) — Fundamentals: footwork, balance, mechanics.
  • Shooting Academy (Jul 7–11) — Mechanics, rhythm and shooting confidence.
  • One & One Mastery (Jul 14–18) — 1-on-1 play, fakes and offensive creativity.
  • Ballhandling Lab (Jul 21–25) — Ball handling and dribbling under pressure.
  • Skills Lab Experience (Jul 28–Aug 1) — Combined work on every skill.

Each week has its own identity: anyone who comes for more than one week won't repeat content.

The venue: La Nau del Clot

Carrer de la Llacuna 172, 08018 Barcelona — the club's main sports facility, in the El Clot neighborhood. Drop-off from 9am, with lunch included in the full-day option.

Who can come

The camp is open to players from any club in Barcelona and the wider province, not only Barna's own. Every week there are places for club members and places for outside players, and groups are formed by age and level.

The full programme, with every week, the prices and photos from last year's edition, is on the camp's page. There's also a complete presentation to share with families.

Frequently asked questions

How much does the Time Chamber camp cost?

€195 for the full week (9am to 5pm, lunch included) and €160 for the half day (9am to 1:30pm). It can be paid in up to 3 instalments.

What weeks does the camp run?

Six: Flow Camp, TC Basics, Shooting Academy, One & One Mastery, Ballhandling Lab and Skills Lab Experience, each with a different technical focus.

Do you need to be a CB Grup Barna player to sign up?

No. The camp is open to players from any club in Barcelona and the wider province.
