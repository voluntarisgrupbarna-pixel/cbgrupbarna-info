CB Grup Barna sponsors: all the partners of the 2026/27 season

23 companies and organisations, most of them from the Clot and Sant Martí, make the club's day-to-day life possible. Here is the full list, with a link to each one's page.

CB Grup Barna · 2026-08-17

How many sponsors CB Grup Barna has

CB Grup Barna has 23 active partners and sponsors with their own page for the 2026/27 season —including Wilson, official ball supplier— plus several institutional and press collaborators (Barcelona City Council, the Generalitat de Catalunya and neighbourhood media such as Eix Clot or Canal Disset). It is not an isolated logo on a shirt: every partner has content, a physical presence at La Nau and access to a community of more than 450 families.

The full list of Barna partners

These are all the club's current sponsors and collaborators, grouped by sector. Each name links to its individual page, with the company, its offer for Barna families (where there is one) and its Instagram.

Neighbourhood shops and restaurants

  • Eix Clot — association of businesses in the Clot · Camp de l'Arpa.
  • Eix Comercial Sant Martí — association of more than 200 shops in the district.
  • La Melosa — smash burger restaurant on Carrer del Clot.
  • Mercat dels Encants — landmark market at Plaça de les Glòries.

Health and wellbeing

  • Nova Farmàcia Clot — neighbourhood pharmacy on Carrer del Clot.
  • Herbolaris Montserrat — herbalist on Carrer de Guipúscoa since 1982.
  • Clínica Dental Bac de Roda — general dentistry and orthodontics.
  • Stepback Podologia — sports podiatry specialising in basketball.
  • Tot Salut — physiotherapy, osteopathy and psychology centre.
  • Aquamiga — reverse osmosis water filtration on rental.

Optics and imaging

  • Armand Òptics — chain of opticians founded in 1988.
  • Foto Jané — photography and photojournalism studio.
  • Instax Fujifilm — instant cameras and film.

Leisure and tourism

  • Westfield Glòries — shopping centre and home of the club's 3x3 tournament.
  • Illa Fantasia — water park in Vilassar de Dalt.
  • Ovella Negra — leisure venue in Poblenou.

Professional services

  • Romeo Abogados — property, civil and family law.
  • Manual Colors — large-format digital printing since 1976.

Sport, training and social impact

  • Time Chamber — high-performance academy, runs the Camp with the club.
  • GBK · Globasket — international youth basketball tournament in Lloret de Mar.
  • Panteres Grogues — leading LGBTQ+ sports club, basketball section.
  • Fundació Mullor — training and employment support for young people.

Official supplier

  • Wilson — official ball supplier for every team at the club.

Collaborators without their own page include institutions such asBarcelona City Council and the Generalitat de Catalunya, and neighbourhood media and organisations such as Canal Disset or Clotfede. The geolocated detail of all of them, inside and outside the neighbourhood, is on the map of partners and collaborators.

How a company becomes a Barna sponsor

The club offers three transparent starting points —Digital presence from €300 a year, Brand in motion from €500 per activation and Sports sponsorship from €1,500 per team per season— plus tailor-made collaboration for brands wanting a specific objective and specific deliverables. Many collaborations are in kind: sports equipment, transport, physiotherapy, catering or printing, valued the same as a financial contribution.

Anyone wanting information can write on WhatsApp to +34 698 425 153 or to marqueting@cbgrupbarna.info. All the levels and the full detail are on the Companies Partners.

Frequently asked questions

How many sponsors does CB Grup Barna have?

CB Grup Barna has 23 active partners and sponsors with their own page for the 2026/27 season —including Wilson, official ball supplier— plus several institutional and press collaborators (Barcelona City Council, the Generalitat de Catalunya, neighbourhood media).

Who are CB Grup Barna's sponsors?

CB Grup Barna's partners include Westfield Glòries, Time Chamber, Eix Clot, Herbolaris Montserrat, Clínica Dental Bac de Roda, Aquamiga, Armand Òptics, Manual Colors, La Melosa, Foto Jané, Mercat dels Encants, Tot Salut, Ovella Negra, Romeo Abogados, Fundació Mullor, Eix Comercial Sant Martí, GBK · Globasket, Illa Fantasia, Panteres Grogues, Stepback Podologia, Instax Fujifilm, Nova Farmàcia Clot and Wilson. The full, up-to-date list is at cbgrupbarna.info/patrocinadors/.

How does a company become a CB Grup Barna sponsor?

By writing on WhatsApp to +34 698 425 153 or by email to marqueting@cbgrupbarna.info. The club prepares a specific proposal based on the brand's objective, with levels from €300 a year and in-kind collaboration for those contributing a product or service.

Where can I see each Barna sponsor's page?

At cbgrupbarna.info/patrocinadors/ you will find the wall with every partner and, clicking on each one, the individual page with the company, its offer for Barna families (where there is one) and its Instagram. The map with locations is at cbgrupbarna.info/partners-mapa/.
