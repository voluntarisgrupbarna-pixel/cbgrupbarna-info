Nova Farmàcia Clot, new CB Grup Barna partner

The pharmacy on Carrer del Clot joins the sponsor ecosystem of the neighbourhood's youth basketball club for the 2026/27 season.

CB Grup Barna · 2026-08-17

A new partner from the neighbourhood

Nova Farmàcia Clot, at Carrer del Clot, 88, becomes a partner of CB Grup Barna from the 2026/27 season. It joins the other local shops and organisations —Eix Clot, Herbolaris Montserrat, Clínica Dental Bac de Roda, Armand Òptics, among others— that make the day-to-day life of the Clot's youth basketball club possible.

Why a neighbourhood pharmacy matters

CB Grup Barna is a development club with more than 450 families involved and sixty-one years of history in the Clot. A local pharmacy coming in as a partner reinforces exactly what the club stands for: a network of neighbourhood shops and services made visible through youth sport, not through a one-off campaign.

What being a Barna partner means

Being a CB Grup Barna partner means having your own page on the sponsor wall at cbgrupbarna.info/patrocinadors, linked directly from the club's home page and from the map of partners and collaborators. Nova Farmàcia Clot already has its own page at cbgrupbarna.info/patrocinadors/partners/nova-farmacia-clot, with its Instagram and, later on, its offer for Barna families.

Welcome to the Barna

The club welcomes Nova Farmàcia Clot and invites you to follow them at @novafarmaciaclot. If you would like to know every partner of the club, read the article CB Grup Barna sponsors: all the partners.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Nova Farmàcia Clot?

Nova Farmàcia Clot is a neighbourhood pharmacy at Carrer del Clot 88, Barcelona, in the same neighbourhood where CB Grup Barna plays.

Since when has it been a CB Grup Barna partner?

Since the 2026/27 season, as a new addition to the club's ecosystem of sponsors and partners.

Where can I see the Nova Farmàcia Clot page?

At cbgrupbarna.info/patrocinadors/partners/nova-farmacia-clot/, within the partner wall at cbgrupbarna.info/patrocinadors/.
