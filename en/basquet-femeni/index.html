The 2026-27 season begins

Preseason kicks off the first week of September across all women's categories (Infantil, Cadet and Júnior, A and B teams). Senior A women debut at the Super Copa Femenina on 12 September, at home —Nau del Clot— against Geieg Vedruna B Tot Net. If your daughter wants to start before the league kicks off, now is the best time: the Open Days are active and the first training session is a free, no-commitment trial.

The Barna Method

That's the name we give our own model for women's skills development: our own research into the gender gap in basketball in Barcelona and Catalonia, applied to how we coach, how we allocate resources and how we support each player, category by category. It isn't just talk — that's why the project has been a finalist for the Premi Dona i Esport in two consecutive editions.

How far it goes

Senior A women compete in the Super Copa Femenina FCBQ, the top territorial category in Catalonia, with a Senior B in Primera Categoria Femenina just below. In between, women's teams at every category —Infantil, Cadet, Júnior— with the same double-team design (A and B) as the men's line, so that no player has to settle for less than the level she deserves.

Positioning

Among Catalonia's leading clubs, Barna is the only one with two senior teams — first and reserve — in both genders at once. FC Barcelona, for example, has had no women's team since its project closed in 2025.

An Escoleta that has produced Liga Femenina Endesa captains

Ainhoa López, today captain of Spar Girona in the Liga Femenina Endesa, started at Barna's Escoleta. She's not the only one: the Escoleta page documents the full journeys of players who came up through this very court.
