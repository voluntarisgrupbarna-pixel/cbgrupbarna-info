Awards

19th Premi Dona i Esport

CB Grup Barna is once again a finalist this year for Barcelona City Council's Premi Dona i Esport (Institut Barcelona Esports), which recognises women and organisations that stand out for their commitment to women's sport and physical activity. The award has five categories —the Premi Mireia Tapiador for Promoting Sport, Athletes, Media, Sports Clubs and Women in Sports Management— with a prize of up to €3,000 per category awarded by the jury. The 19th edition ceremony is on 4 November 2026.
