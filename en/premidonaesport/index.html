CB Grup Barna

61 years in El Clot. One neighbourhood. One model.

CB Grup Barna was founded in 1965 in the El Clot neighbourhood, in Barcelona's Sant Martí District. Joan Manuel Serrat, a neighbourhood resident, was one of the entity's co-founders.

Today: 450+ members, 40 teams (20 girls' + 20 boys', a 1:1 ratio), 38 active women coaches (65.5% of the technical staff), among the 20 best clubs in Catalonia.

From El Clot to the elite:
a neighbourhood's legacy.

The Barna Method

A system. Not a campaign.

01
Equal Investment — The 50%

CB Grup Barna allocates 50% of its budget to women's basketball. The sector average is 35%. Investment builds level. Level builds role models. Role models build demand.

02
Leadership Pipeline — The 38 Coaches

38 active women coaches — 65.5% of the technical staff. No federation coaching licence. As far as the FCBQ is concerned, they don't exist. But they coach 40 teams every week in El Clot.

03
Organic Response — The 53.7%

53.7% of the social media audience is female. With no planned campaign at all. Women can tell the club practises equality instead of just talking about it.

Research framework

The 5 Theories of the Barna Method

T1
The Invisible Market Theory
Women's talent operates as an invisible market. The first club to correct the undervaluation will capture the surplus of talent. Akerlof (1970) · WNBA +183%.
→ Read the full theory
T2
The Recognition Ladder Theory
35.7% players → 9% Curs Superior head coaches. The ladder loses between 32% and 75% of women's talent at every rung. Eagly & Carli (2007).
→ Read the full theory
T3
The Cultural Irreversibility Theory
A programme can be cancelled. A culture cannot. 61 years of continuous practice have built a culture at CB Grup Barna where parity is the norm. Schein (1992).
→ Read the full theory
T4
The Generational Lag Theory
Participation data (35.7% licences) versus power data (0% club presidencies in BCN). A systemic 20-30 year lag. Mannheim (1952).

The Productive Authenticity Theory
Organisations that practise their values generate an organic response that outperforms any planned strategy. 53.7% female audience with no campaigns. Holt (2004).

Conclusions of the candidacy

Why this candidacy.

01

A basketball club, not an exception

This candidacy isn't presented from a place of singularity or exceptionalism. It's presented from a sober, measurable statement: CB Grup Barna has 250 federated women players, more than any other basketball club in Barcelona city. The comparison is strict: only with other basketball clubs, because basketball has its own structural dynamics that can't be equated with other sports.

02

What this means for Barcelona

If the largest club has 250 licences and the city average sits at 54, it means Barcelona's women's talent is fragmented across small clubs, without the critical mass needed to build a top-level competitive structure. This is one of the factors behind Barcelona having no women's team in LF2. Fragmentation is the problem. Concentrating talent in projects like CB Grup Barna is part of the solution.

03

What already works here

What CB Grup Barna demonstrates every week at the Parc del Clot is that parity isn't an aspiration: it's an established practice. 20 women's teams. 20 men's teams. 38 active women coaches. 65.5% female technical staff. 50% of the budget for women's basketball. And above all: real, continuous development, from age 5 through to senior level, where no girl in the neighbourhood is left without a place to play.

04

Why recognition matters

For us, the 19è Premi Dona i Esport is a lever: to make a working model visible, so other Barcelona clubs can replicate it, so institutions reinforce it, and so the girls of the neighbourhood know their club has been recognised as a civic benchmark. We're not asking for a lifetime-achievement award. We're asking for our track record to stop being an exception.

"Equality in sport isn't legislated. It's built, from the ground up, from a neighbourhood. And sometimes, when it works, all it takes is for someone to show it."

From crisis to opportunity

Barcelona,
capital of women's basketball.

Recognising a crisis is the first step to solving it. Barcelona's women's basketball is living through a diagnosed structural crisis. But Barcelona also has every asset it needs to become the benchmark for women's basketball in Catalonia and southern Europe: 134 grassroots girls' teams, a network of clubs with history, proximity to FC Barcelona, Joventut and Granollers, and a new generation that no longer accepts the status quo.

What follows are 6 concrete proposals with a 2030 horizon. Some CB Grup Barna can lead alone. Others require an alliance with clubs, institutions and sponsors. All of them are necessary.

01
Sporting ambition · 2030

Barcelona LF2 Plan 2030

The first Barcelona-based player with her team in Liga Femenina 2, playing in Barcelona city. A strategic alliance between Barcelona clubs with competitive women's sections. Roadmap: 2026-27 selection of allied clubs · 2028 joint competitive structure · 2030 first BCN team in LF2.

Leads: clubs with senior sections · Supports: IBE, FCBQ
02
Development · 2027

El Clot Women's
Development Centre

Stop the drain of talent to Cornellà and Mataró. A centre in the Parc del Clot where the best players from every club in Barcelona city can train four days a week without having to travel. Initial capacity: 60 players. Sustained through an agreement with IBE and partner clubs.

Leads: CB Grup Barna · Supports: BCN clubs, IBE
03
Competition · 2027

BCN Women's League

A competition where talent can meet. Summer league and winter tournaments with every women's club in Barcelona city. Broadcasts, individual awards, media visibility and dedicated sponsorship. A format inspired by 3x3 Westfield Glòries, already proven, but at city scale.

Leads: CB Grup Barna + Time Chamber + BCN clubs alliance
04
Recruitment · 2026

"Barcelona Talent" Programme

Identify talent before it leaves. An early identification programme in the city's schools and clubs, with scholarships that let players combine their studies and sporting career without having to leave Barcelona. Goal: go from 0.75% to 3% talent conversion in 5 years.

Leads: IBE + BCN clubs · Ally: Catalan universities
05
Governance · 2026

Citizens' Pact for
Women's Basketball

An agreement signed by Barcelona City Council, IBE, FCBQ, the 17 clubs with women's sections and major sponsors. Dedicated budget. Prime-time court slots for women's games (not 9:30pm). Institutional visibility. Measurable commitments, audited annually.

Leads: Barcelona City Council · Involves: every stakeholder
06
Leadership · 2030

100 Coaches by 2030

Multiply the technical pipeline. A training, mentoring and certification programme to reach 100+ active women coaches in Barcelona city before 2030. CB Grup Barna as the benchmark training club, in alliance with the FCBQ and its Coach Mentoring programme. Wipe out the 9% figure for women in the Curs Superior.

Leads: CB Grup Barna · Ally: FCBQ Mentoring

We're not asking Barcelona to stop being what it is. We're asking Barcelona to also be what it can be: a city where any girl can play basketball, develop, reach the elite and come back — without ever having to leave.
