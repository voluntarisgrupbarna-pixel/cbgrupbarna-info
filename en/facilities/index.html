A common question

If you do not know which court your child plays or trains at, the quickest way to find out is to look at their team's page, updated daily with the next fixtures and the venue assigned by the federation. For theEscoleta (ages 4 to 8): Wednesdays 17:30 to 18:40 at Escola Casas, and Saturdays 09:00 to 10:30 at La Nau del Clot. For any question, Julio Torralba on 646 205 526.

Frequently asked questions

Where exactly is La Nau del Clot?

At carrer de la Llacuna 170-172, inside Parc del Clot, 08018 Barcelona. It is the club's main venue, in the Sant Martí district.

Which courts does CB Grup Barna train and play on?

Six venues in the Sant Martí district, all close to each other: La Nau del Clot, Escola Provençals, La Farigola del Clot, La Rambleta del Clot, Escola Casas and Claror Marítim.

Which court does my team use?

It depends on the age group and the time slot, and it can change from week to week. Your team's calendar has the exact answer every week: it updates daily from the Catalan Basketball Federation's official calendar.

Where is the club? Venue address and how to get there

The main venue is La Nau del Clot, at Carrer de la Llacuna 170-172, inside Parc del Clot, 08018 Barcelona, in the Sant Martí district. Take metro L1 (Glòries) or L2 (Clot), or Rodalies commuter rail to Clot-Aragó. The club uses five more courts in the neighbourhood depending on the team and the timetable.
