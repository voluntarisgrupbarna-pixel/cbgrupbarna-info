Basketball skills training in Barcelona

It is not a holiday activity with a ball: it is individual technical work, in short, highly repetitive sessions on a single part of the game — shooting, ball handling, 1v1, reading the game — in groups by age and level. CB Grup Barna runs it with Time Chamber three times a year, always during school holidays: Christmas, Easter and summer. Open to players from any club in Barcelona.

Frequently asked questions

What families ask most

What is basketball skills training?

Individual work on a single part of the game — shooting, ball handling, 1v1, reading the game — with a lot of repetition, in groups by age and level. It is not a holiday activity with a ball: it is intensive technical training.

Who runs the skills training at CB Grup Barna?

The club runs it with Time Chamber, who bring the individual-training method. Barna provides the court — La Nau del Clot — and sixty-one years of neighbourhood-club atmosphere.

When does the skills training take place?

Three times a year, always during school holidays: Christmas, Easter and summer (June-July), the last of these in the fullest format, the six-week Campus Time Chamber.

Do you have to be a CB Grup Barna player to sign up?

No. It is open to players from any club in Barcelona and the province.

Where does the skills training take place?

At La Nau del Clot, CB Grup Barna's main court in the Sant Martí district of Barcelona.

Would you like to try a skills session?

Write to us and we will tell you about the next edition — Christmas, Easter or summer — with places available.
