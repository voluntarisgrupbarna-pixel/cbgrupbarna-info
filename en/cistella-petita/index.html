Cistella Petita

If the 3x3 is basketball's big party open to the whole city, Cistella Petita is the little ones' party: CB Grup Barna's and the neighbourhood's mini-basketball festival, designed so children aged 3 to 8 get to experience their big day as players.

Request information Discover the Escoleta

The little ones' big day

Matches adapted to the children's size, games, medals for everyone and families in the stands. It's often the first contact many children have with basketball and with CB Grup Barna — and the natural entry point into the club's Escoleta.

For the club, Cistella Petita is much more than a fun day out: it's the first link in a journey that starts at age 3 and, as shown by Barna's two senior first teams, can reach the elite of Catalan basketball without ever leaving the same club.

How to sign up

You don't need to be part of the Escoleta to take part: the Cistella Petita is a day open to the whole neighbourhood. Dates and registration for the next edition are announced on this page and on Instagram (@cbgrupbarna); for any questions, you can write to the club's WhatsApp or contact Julio Torralba directly at 646 205 526.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Cistella Petita?

CB Grup Barna's and the neighbourhood's mini-basketball festival, designed so children aged 3 to 8 get to experience their big day as players: adapted matches, games, medals and families in the stands.

Who can take part?

Children aged 3 to 8, whether or not they're already part of the club's Escoleta. For many children it's their first contact with basketball and with CB Grup Barna.

When is the next edition?

The Cistella Petita is held once a year, in June. Dates are announced on this page and on Instagram (@cbgrupbarna).

Does the Cistella Petita lead into the Escoleta?

Yes. It's the natural entry point into CB Grup Barna's Escoleta, the basketball introduction programme for children aged 4 to 7.

Want your child to take part?

We'll let you know when registration opens for the next edition.
