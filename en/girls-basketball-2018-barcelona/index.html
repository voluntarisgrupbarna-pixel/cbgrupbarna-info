What exactly we are looking for

We are not building a new team: the 2018 team already plays. What we are looking for are new players, and there are two kinds, and both suit us:

  • Girls who have never played. This is the most common case and the one that works best. At this age nobody arrives knowing how to play; they learn it here.
  • Girls who already play somewhere else —at school, at another club— and want to see what it is like with a league team.

There are no cuts and no selection on the first day. She comes, trains for an hour and a half with the team and, at the end, you decide.

Why the Barna, if your child is a girl

This question has an answer made of numbers, not of intentions:

  • Eight federated women's teams, from premini to senior. The whole path is here: a girl who starts now does not have to change club to keep playing at 16 or at 20.
  • 50% of the club's budget goes to the women's side, and there are as many female players as male ones.
  • 65.5% female coaches on the technical staff. Your daughter will see women coaching from day one.
  • From this same court in El Clot has come a captain of the Liga Femenina Endesa.

All of this is explained, with the method and the data, on the club's women's basketball page.

What the first day is like

You arrive five minutes early, someone from the staff meets you and your daughter goes onto the court with the rest of the team. She does the same session the others do: warm-up, ball drills and a small game. An hour and a half. Parents can stay and watch from the stand —plenty of people prefer that on the first day, and it is perfectly normal.

At the end of the session we talk to you: how it went, what you thought, and what she would like to do. Nobody will ask you to decide anything that morning.

Where La Nau del Clot is

Carrer de la Llacuna, 170-172, 08018 Barcelona, inside Parc del Clot, in the Sant Martí district. It is walking distance from El Clot, Camp de l'Arpa, Glòries, Poblenou and the Sagrada Família. The club has trained here since 1965.

Book a place

Places are given in booking order and each Saturday takes 50. Booking takes a minute and you will get a confirmation by email.

Book a place for 19 September

If you would rather ask first, write to the club's WhatsApp: 698 425 153, or to marqueting@cbgrupbarna.info.

What happens to your data

What you write on the form is read by the club's sporting management and is used only to organise the trial and reply to you. We do not add it to any list and we do not use it for anything else. The detail is in the privacy policy, and how we protect children's data, in Child Protection.

Frequently asked questions

Where can an 8-year-old girl play basketball in Barcelona?

At CB Grup Barna, at La Nau del Clot (carrer de la Llacuna, 170-172, 08018 Barcelona), where the team of girls born in 2018 is looking for new players. The club has eight league teams for girls and women, from premini to senior, so a girl who starts now does not have to change club to keep playing.

When are the trials for girls born in 2018?

On Saturday 19 September 2026, 9.00 to 10.30 am, at La Nau del Clot, with a second day on Saturday 26 September at the same time and place. The trial is free and you need to book: each Saturday takes 50 children, in booking order.

Does a girl need previous experience to start playing basketball?

No. Most girls who join the 2018 team have never played, and that is the case that works best: at this age nobody arrives knowing how to play. There is also room for girls who already play at school or at another club and want to see what a league team is like.

Are there cuts or a selection on the first day?

No. There is no selection and no cut. She comes, trains for an hour and a half with the team and, at the end of the session, we talk to the family. Nobody has to decide anything that morning, and parents are welcome to watch from the stand.

Does CB Grup Barna have girls-only teams?

Yes. The club has eight league teams for girls and women, from premini to senior. 50% of the budget goes to the women's side, there are as many female players as male ones and 65.5% of the technical staff are female coaches. From 4 to 8, though, the Escoleta is mixed.

What should she bring on the trial day?

Sports clothes, indoor court shoes and a water bottle. The club provides the ball and the trial is free.
