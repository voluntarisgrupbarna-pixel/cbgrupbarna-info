Observatori Barna

The club is moving from publishing news to publishing a point of view: how to properly develop a young person through sport, and what a club's role is inside a neighbourhood. A new article every week, written from inside the Clot.

Cadence · one article a week Source · the club's own data and voices By · CB Grup Barna

01

The question behind it all

What does success mean in youth basketball?

The usual answer is winning. At Barna we'd defend another one: that a 6-year-old reaches 18 still wanting to play, having learned to compete, live alongside others, make mistakes and belong to something. Nobody in Barcelona's grassroots basketball has claimed this ground yet, and sixty-one years developing players in the same neighbourhood give us a voice to do it.

Development isn't measured in wins alone.

Observatori Barna's compass

02

Why now

Grassroots basketball is shifting from "how to play better" to "how are we developing players", and Barna can get there with its own authority.

The FCBQ is already focusing on the role of the trusted adult in youth sport, and the Basket Girlz programme (FEB/Endesa) has trained more than 650 coaches to curb girls dropping out of the game. The club already has the key asset to speak on this as an equal: Premi Dona i Esport · El Mètode Barna, its own published research on the gender gap in Catalan basketball.
