Where the Barna stands among the big names of Catalan basketball

Barça, Joventut, Manresa, Girona… when people talk about the big names of Catalan basketball, a neighbourhood club is rarely included. The official club records at basquetcatala.cat tell a different story. This is positioning for the whole club —men's and women's— not just the women's project.

The Barna's pyramid, from the inside

The club's official record at basquetcatala.cat shows more than 34 federated teams, from Pre-Mini (ages 8-9) up to Senior, with parallel men's and women's structures and, in most categories, up to three levels (A, B and often a third promotion level). In both senior teams —men's and women's— there's a first team and a reserve, and both first teams compete in Super Copa FCBQ, the top Catalan regional category.

The four benchmark clubs

Among the Catalan clubs with a first team in Lliga ACB or Lliga Femenina Endesa, the usual pattern is that the youth system and the senior first team are not the same entity.

  • FC Barcelona — 10 federated teams in Catalonia, all men's, from Mini to Junior, with no Pre-Mini. No women's team at all: the blaugrana project (Barça CBS, with CB Santfeliuenc) reached Lliga F Endesa but closed at the end of the 2024-25 season for financial reasons. Its men's reserve team plays Copa Catalunya, not Super Copa; the first team plays Lliga ACB.
  • Joventut Badalona (la Penya) — the largest youth base in Catalonia: 53 teams, from Pre-Mini to Junior, in both genders. No senior team of its own within this entity, neither men's nor women's: whoever reaches the top leaves the club.
  • Bàsquet Manresa — actually two different clubs depending on gender: the men's youth system (17 teams, no senior) and the Manresa CBF women's side, a separate entity with its own board, budget and brand, with a senior team in Lliga Femenina 2, the national league.
  • Fundació Bàsquet Girona — 23 teams, from Pre-Mini to Junior, in both genders, chaired by Marc Gasol. It has no senior team of its own in either gender either: the first team plays in Lliga Endesa, outside this record.

The real difference

It's not about having a women's team or a large youth base —almost every club analysed has that. It's that at the Barna, training up and reaching the competitive ceiling always runs through the same board, the same budget and the same club, in both genders at once. None of the four benchmarks has it set up this way.

At the big clubs, the youth system and the first team are almost never the same entity. At the Barna, they are: the same player who joins the Escoleta at age four can go on to play the Super Copa without ever changing shirts.

What makes us different

Of the clubs analysed, Grup Barna is the only one with two senior teams —first and reserve— in both genders at once, within the same entity that runs the entire formative pyramid, both competing in Super Copa FCBQ. Neither Barça, nor la Penya, nor Manresa nor Girona have it this integrated. We're not the club with the most teams in Catalonia —la Penya has 53— nor the most renowned. We are, though, one of the few where training up and competing at the highest level happen at the same club, without ever breaking the thread, in both genders.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Barna a competitive or a formative club?

Both at once: it uses the whole FCBQ ladder (1st year, Preferent/Interterritorial, Promoció) and it also reaches the top rung, with both senior teams in Super Copa FCBQ.

Does FC Barcelona have a women's basketball team?

Not since the end of the 2024-25 season. Its project, Barça CBS (with CB Santfeliuenc), reached Lliga F Endesa but closed for financial reasons.

Does la Penya (Joventut Badalona) have its own senior team?

Not within the same entity as its youth system. La Penya has 53 formative teams, the largest youth base in Catalonia, but whoever reaches senior level leaves the club.

What makes CB Grup Barna different?

It's the only one of the compared clubs with two senior teams —first and reserve— in both genders at once, within the same entity that runs the whole formative pyramid, both competing in Super Copa FCBQ.
